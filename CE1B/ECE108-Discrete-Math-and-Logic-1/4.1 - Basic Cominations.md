### Number of arrangements

> Numbers of 26-letter strings, each letter used exactly once

$$ N=P(26, 26) = \frac{26!}{0!} = 26! $$

> Number of ways to get sets of size 5 from the alphabet

$$ N =

= \frac{26!}{(26-5)! \cdot 5!}
= \begin{pmatrix}26 \\ 26-5\end{pmatrix}
