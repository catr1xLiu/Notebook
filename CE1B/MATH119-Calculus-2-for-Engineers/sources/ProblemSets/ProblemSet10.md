## Practice Problem Set 10

Topics: Comparison Tests, Alternating Series, Absolute/Conditional Convergence

- 1. Determine whether the following series converge absolutely, converge conditionally, or diverge. Justify your conclusions as thoroughly as you can.
  - (a)  $\sum_{n=2}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n}{\ln n}$  **Answer:** converges conditionally
  - (b)  $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{n}{1 + n\sqrt{n}}$  Answer: diverges
  - (c)  $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (-1)^n \frac{(2+(-1)^n)}{2^n}$  Answer: converges absolutely
  - (d)  $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\sin(4n)}{4^n}$  **Answer:** converges absolutely
  - (e)  $\sum_{n=2}^{\infty} \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{n}\right)$  **Answer:** diverges
  - (f)  $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n 3^{1/n}}{n}$  Answer: converges conditionally
  - (g)  $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\arctan n}{n}$  Answer: diverges
  - (h)  $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1+\frac{1}{n}}{e^n}$  Answer: converges absolutely
  - (i)  $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^n \left( \sqrt{n^2 + 2n} n \right)$  Answer: diverges
  - (j)  $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\sqrt{n}}{n^2 + 2n + 2}$  Answer: converges absolutely
  - (k)  $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \left(\frac{n}{n+10}\right)^{2n}$  Answer: diverges
  - (l)  $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n}{n^n}$  Answer: converges absolutely
