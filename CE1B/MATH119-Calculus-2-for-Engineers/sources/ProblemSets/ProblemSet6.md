## Practice Problem Set 6

**Topics:** Triple Integrals, Cylindrical and Spherical Coordinates

1. Evaluate the following:

(a) 
$$\int_{1}^{3} \int_{0}^{x} \int_{0}^{\ln z} e^{y} \ln x \, dy \, dz \, dx$$
 Answer:  $\frac{5}{9}$ 

(b) 
$$\int_{-2}^{2} \int_{-\sqrt{4-x^2}}^{\sqrt{4-x^2}} \int_{(x^2+y^2)^2}^{16} x^2 dz dy dx$$
 Answer:  $32\pi$ 

- 2. Find the volume of the tetrahedron bound by the planes x=0,y=0,z=0 and x+2y+2z-4=0. Answer:  $\frac{8}{3}$
- 3. Evaluate  $\iiint_D \frac{1}{x^2 + y^2 + z^2} dV$  where D is the region between the spheres  $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 4$  and  $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 8$ . Answer:  $8\pi(\sqrt{2} 1)$
- 4. Find the volume of the solid under the paraboloid  $z=2-x^2-y^2$ , above the xy-plane, and inside the cylinder  $x^2+y^2=1$ . **Answer:**  $\frac{3\pi}{2}$
- 5. Find the volume of the "ice cream cone" given by the region bounded between the two surfaces  $z = \sqrt{3x^2 + 3y^2}$  and the top half of  $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 64$ . Answer:  $\frac{512\pi}{3}(2 \sqrt{3})$
- 6. Evaluate the following integral by changing the order of integration:

$$\int_{0}^{1} \int_{0}^{1-x} \int_{y}^{1} \frac{\sin(\pi z)}{z(2-z)} dz dy dx. \text{ Answer: } \frac{1}{\pi}$$

- 7. Let D be the region inside the cylinder  $x^2 + y^2 = 1$  and between the planes z = 1 and z = y + 2.

  - (b) Set up in Cartesian coordinates the triple integral that computes the mass of D, if the density is  $\rho(x, y, z) = x^2 + y^2 + 1$ .
