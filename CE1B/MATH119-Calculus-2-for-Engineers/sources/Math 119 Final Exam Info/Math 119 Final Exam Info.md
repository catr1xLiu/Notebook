## S26 Final Exam Information

- The final exam for Math 119 will take place on Monday, August 10, from 4:00 pm to 6:30 pm.
- Students will be writing the exam in areas 6, 7, and 8 of the Physics Activities Complex (PAC). Each student is responsible for checking their room and seating number on Odyssey before the exam date. This information will be available on Odyssey around 3 days before the exam day. It is essential that students show up to the correct room and sit in the correct place, as the midterm will be printed with each student's name and ID already on it.
- As we discussed in ealier classes, the final exam will questions will be on the the topics starting triple integrals till the end (binomial series). Students must justify their work unless it is specifically mentioned not to do so in the question.
- Three previous final exams have been added on Learn in the "Final Exam" section for practice.
- The formula sheet I added to on Learn in the "Final Exam" section will be provided with the question sheet.
- Neither calculators nor any other aid material are allowed. This will be a closed book exam.
- Please make sure to bring your WatCard and keep it on the desk during the entire exam period.
- Your final grade will be calculated according to the course outline:
  - 20% on the assignments (best 8 out of 9),
  - 30% on the midterm exam,
  - 50% on the final exam.
- Students who miss the exam due to illness must submit a Verification of Illness Form (VIF) as soon as possible, and will either receive an "INC" (incomplete) or DNW (did not write) depending on each single case. Please note that a "DNW" grade is a fail. Students who miss the exam without submitting a VIF will not pass this course.
- The graded final exams will not be returned to students and the corresponding solutions will not be posted on Learn.
