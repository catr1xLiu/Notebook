
> [!abstract] Introduction
> ## ECE 140 — Linear Circuits

**Instructor:** Vincent Gaudet · **Location:** E7 5343 (LEC), E7 4437 (TUT), E2 3344 (LAB)
**Schedule:** LEC MWF 11:30AM–12:20PM | TUT Tue 2:30–4:20PM | LAB Wed 1:30–4:20PM
**Textbook:** *Electric Circuits*, Nilsson & Riedel, 12th ed. (~$78 for 6-month access). **Non-programmable calculator required** for exams (TI-30X IIS, Casio FX-300MS, or Sharp EL-531X — available at WStore).

The foundational circuit analysis course for all of electrical engineering. You will systematically analyze circuits containing resistors, capacitors, inductors, and op-amps using node-voltage, mesh, Thévenin/Norton, and superposition methods. The course then extends these to AC steady-state using phasors and impedance. The lab component teaches real bench skills — oscilloscopes, function generators, multimeters, and breadboarding. CDT (Circuit Design Task) sessions drill exam-style problem solving under time pressure.

> [!hint] Hint
> ## Labs & CDTs

5 in-person sessions (~3 hours each) in **E2-3344**. Build and debug real circuits on breadboards. Measure voltages, currents, transient waveforms, and frequency responses using bench oscilloscopes and function generators. The goal is to verify theoretical predictions from lecture and develop hands-on intuition.

**CDTs** (Circuit Design Tasks) are in-class timed problem-solving sessions during lecture time — exam-style circuit analysis problems. Bring your calculator to every CDT and lab.
