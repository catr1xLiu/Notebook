
> [!abstract] Introduction
> ## ECE 124 — Digital Circuits & Systems

**Instructor:** Anwar Hasan · **Location:** E7 5343 (LEC), STC 0060 (TUT), E2 2364 (LAB)
**Schedule:** LEC MWF 8:30–9:20AM | TUT Mon 1:30–2:20PM | LAB Thu 1:30–4:20PM

The bridge between Boolean logic and real hardware. The course covers Boolean algebra and logic gates, combinational circuit design (K-maps, adders, muxes, decoders), sequential circuits (latches, flip-flops, FSMs — synchronous and asynchronous), number representations, and implementation technologies. Circuits are designed and implemented on an actual **FPGA using Verilog**. Directly foundational for all future digital systems courses.

> ## Lab Structure

Labs are done in **pairs** (same lab section — form your group on LEARN before the first session). Each $\approx 3$-hour session in E2-2364 runs three things simultaneously:

1. Instructor presents the **next** lab
2. You **demo** completed work from the **previous** lab (graded)
3. You **design and test** the **current** lab on FPGA hardware

A timed **quiz** covering the previous lab's concepts is held at the end of each session. The first session is an exception: presentation + design/test only (no demo or quiz yet).
