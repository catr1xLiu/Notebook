> ## ECE 108 — Discrete Math & Logic 1

**Instructor:** Mahesh Tripunitara · **Location:** E7 5343 (LEC), E7 5353 (TUT)
**Schedule:** LEC Mon 9:30–10:20AM / Tue & Thu 8:30–9:20AM | TUT Fri 1:30–2:20PM
**Textbook:** Tripunitara's own course notes — free on LEARN. No purchase needed.

The mathematical foundations of computer engineering. The course requires a mental gear shift: the focus is on reading and writing **rigorous formal proofs** — not calculating, but *proving*. It builds from propositional logic and proof techniques through set theory, functions, cardinality, combinatorics, and discrete probability.
