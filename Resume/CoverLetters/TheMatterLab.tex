\opening{Dear Prof. Alan Aspuru-Guzik and Prof. Varinia Bernales,}

I am applying for the Robotics Research Intern position to bring my experience in contact-rich manipulation and multi-GPU policy training to the precise, safety-critical demands of your self-driving chemistry labs.

At the National Research Council Canada (NRC), I built a custom Python teleoperation pipeline coupling a Haply Inverse3 haptic controller with a UR10e robot, streaming real-time force and torque data to enable force-aware demonstration collection --- well-suited to teaching robots to handle delicate lab apparatus. As reported in our RoboTac 2026 submission, this setup yields demonstrations with a 64\% reduction in peak contact force compared to the force-blind baseline. I trained the downstream Diffusion Policy in PyTorch on a 36-node V100 SLURM cluster, and led a refactor of its inference pipeline into a modular three-component architecture for codebase extensibility.

While my hands-on training has centered on diffusion policies, I follow the broader VLA literature --- $\pi_0$ and force-aware variants --- which gives me useful context for deploying VLAs in your lab. I have shipped physics simulation code at scale (MapleSim, an open-source library with 500+ users across the FRC robotics community) and recently drafted MuJoCo simulation pipeline plans at the NRC for incoming students to implement, so I can come up to speed quickly on IsaacLab or IsaacSim.

I would be excited to contribute to your mission of AI-accelerated scientific discovery. Thank you for your time, and I look forward to hearing from you.

Sincerely yours, \\
