# Deep Learning Framework for Autonomous Racing 

The autonomous racing field has seen substantial success with trajectory optimization in individual time trials. But it faces a bottleneck in real-time decision-making under pressure from opponents. I wish to explore a deep learning framework for autonomous racing vehicles. I should start by creating a learned trajectory latent yielding all feasbile trajectory a vehicle can trace. Then, generative models can be initialized via imitation learning from real-world professional driver demonstrations. Reinforcement learning will also be possible once we have an accurate simulator. The goal is to train a policy that matches top drivers in decision-making, produces time-optimal trajectories, and operates at a speed fast enough for real-time control. Further, the system should be transferable across vehicle types via a shared latent space and parameter-conditioned decoder.
