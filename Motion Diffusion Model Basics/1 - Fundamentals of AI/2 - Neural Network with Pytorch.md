> ## Fundamentals of Neural Networks

**Neural Network** is the fundamental algorithm behind Artificial Intelligence. It has a special structure, which can be graphically represented as a large set of connected *Nodes* forming a *Net*. 

![[2.1 - Network1|100%]]
Where the input is abstracted to a handful of **values** (0~1) forming the first layer. (**Input Layer**). The abstraction process is often called preprocessing and be very complex itself depending on tasks. (see the example later for more details)

Each **Neuron** is a connected *Node* in the graph. The **value** of a **Neuron** (0~1) represents how "activated" the Neuron is. The value is derived as a weighted average of **all** the **Neurons** from the previous level.
