We adopt the motion capture dataset of Van Criekinge et al.~\cite{vancriekinge2023dataset} (ages 21-86) and transform data into a HumanML3D-compatible skeletal graph \cite{guo2022generating} for learning. The framework is proposed in Fig. \ref{fig:framework}.


\textbf{Age classification.}
A Spatial-Temporal Graph Convolutional Network (ST-GCN)~\cite{yan2018stgcn} is trained to classify motion clips into three age groups (Young, Mid, Elderly).  The penultimate layer of the network is a latent vector that encapsulates the biomechanical signatures of aging (e.g., velocity variance, joint motion range), denoted as $z_{age}$.


\textbf{Age-conditioned generation.}
We use a diffusion-based motion generative model~\cite{tevet2023human, chen2022mld} conditioned on both text and age embeddings.  The age-specific latent vector $z_{age}$, encoded via a small MLP, modulates diffusion steps through cross-attention, enabling controllable synthesis of age-consistent motion.
