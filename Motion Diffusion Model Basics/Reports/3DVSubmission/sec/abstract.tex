Age influences walking patterns through subtle kinematic signatures such as reduced velocity and postural shifts, yet capturing these biomechanical cues remains challenging. Recent advances in human motion recognition and generation have achieved impressive realism, but whether they can learn and reproduce age-related motion remains underexplored. We present a proof-of-concept framework that learns and synthesizes aging signatures using graph and diffusion networks. A graph convolutional network predicts subject age from captured motion, while a diffusion model generates new motion conditioned on age. This work bridges gait analysis and motion synthesis, laying the foundation for age-aware motion generation systems that support AI for Design and Aging in Place programs.
