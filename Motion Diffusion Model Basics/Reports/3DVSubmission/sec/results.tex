We have successfully implemented a robust, production-ready pipeline that converts clinical Plug-in-Gait marker data to the HumanML3D skeleton standard. Our primary technical result is the elimination of "mesh inflation" and "crouch-walking" (Groucho Marx effect) artifacts through a multi-stage VPoser optimization. By transitioning from naive marker-to-joint mapping to vertex-based skin mapping, we achieve an anatomically plausible fit across the entire adult lifespan (ages 21–86). While the ST-GCN validation and LoRA-MDM training are currently in progress, our initial data audit confirms that the subtle kinematic markers of aging—such as reduced hip extension and stride length—are preserved in the final 263-dimensional feature space. This infrastructure provides a scalable foundation for synthesizing diverse, age-representative datasets to improve the robustness of elderly-care monitoring systems.
