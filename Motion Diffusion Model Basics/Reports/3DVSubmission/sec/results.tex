\textbf{4. Results \& Future Work}
Our pipeline reliably transforms clinical motion data into
HumanML3D format while preserving age-related kinematic
cues such as reduced hip extension and stride length
(Fig.~\ref{fig:heatmap}). Multi-stage VPoser fitting
resolves retargeting artifacts including mesh inflation and
crouch-walking (Fig.~\ref{fig:artifact}), yielding
anatomically plausible motion across all adult ages.
ST-GCN and latent-conditioned diffusion
models~\cite{chen2022mld, hu2022lora, sawdayee2025dance}
are being trained for age signal extraction and
reproduction. Future work will incorporate physical
regularization and longitudinal data for personalized aging
trajectories.
