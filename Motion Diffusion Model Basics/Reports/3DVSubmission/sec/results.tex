Our pipeline reliably transforms clinical motion data into the HumanML3D standard while preserving age-related kinematic cues such as reduced hip extension and stride length. ST-GCN and Latent-Conditioned Diffusers \cite{hu2022lora, sawdayee2025dance, chen2022mld} are being trained for extracting aging signals and reproducing them with diffusion model. This infrastructure enables scalable generation of age-representative motion datasets, advancing age-aware synthesis for rehabilitation, monitoring, and human-robot interaction.
