We have successfully developed a robust, production-ready data-reconstruction pipeline that converts the clinical Plug-In Gait dataset into the HumanML3D standard. The primary result is the elimination of “inflated shapes” and “crouch-walking” artifacts through a multi-stage optimization using VPoser. While the ST-GCN and MDM networks are still being trained, multiple data audits (reduced hip extension, stride length, velocity e.t.c.) confirm that the subtle kinematic markers of aging are preserved in the final 263-dimensional feature space. This infrastructure provides a foundation for the processing of age-representative datasets and training of age-aware motion synthetics models.
