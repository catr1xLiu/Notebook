Aging alters gait through well-documented biomechanical changes: reduced velocity, shorter stride, compensatory hip strategies, and increased double-support time~\cite{boyer2023nia, studenski2011gait}. These signatures carry diagnostic and prognostic value, yet most generative models are biased toward healthy young adults. While Spatial-Temporal Graph Convolutional Networks (ST-GCN)~\cite{yan2018stgcn} and text-to-motion generative models such as MDM~\cite{tevet2023human} achieve realistic results, their ability to capture age-related biomechanical variation remains untested. We ask: \emph{Can age-specific kinematic signatures be extracted and reproduced by data-driven models?}
