Our preliminary findings reveal both promise and challenges in modeling age-related motion. Limited balanced datasets, especially for older adults, and inconsistencies in clinical capture protocols constrain generalization. Future work will extend the framework toward bio-mechanically informed generation with joint torque estimation and physical regularization for realistic motion. We also plan to incorporate longitudinal data for personalized modeling of functional trajectories, advancing inclusive and age-aware motion synthesis.
