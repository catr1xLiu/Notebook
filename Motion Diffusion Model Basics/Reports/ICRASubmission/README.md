## Workshop Focus

The workshop targets **long-term human motion prediction** for intelligent systems that 
share space or interact with humans. Topics of interest relevant to this submission:

- **Human-Robot Interaction considering predictions** — robots must anticipate how 
  humans, including elderly users, will move over extended horizons
- **Motion and task planning in dynamic environments** — planning under uncertainty 
  introduced by age-related gait variability
- **Human motion prediction and safety** — predicting fall-risk or hazardous motion 
  patterns in eldercare contexts
- **Evaluation of prediction algorithms: datasets, metrics, and benchmarks** — our 
  pipeline and clinical metrics contribution is directly relevant here
- **Multi-modal Foundation Model Integration** — conditioning diffusion models on 
  age and language aligns with this theme
- **Social and Predictive Navigation** — age-aware motion priors improve robot 
  navigation around elderly pedestrians

The audience is robotics and HRI researchers, not computer vision. Framing must 
connect generation to **prediction and planning**, not to visual realism alone.
