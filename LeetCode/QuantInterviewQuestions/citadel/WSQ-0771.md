> [!example] Question 771 · Citadel
> ## Software Engineer Interview

Given a set of numbers \[a, b, c, d, e\] and a target t, find the number of triplets from the set whose sum is less than or equal to t.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=772)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> ## Solution
>
> We are given five numbers $\{a,b,c,d,e\}$ and a threshold $t$.  
> The task is to count how many $3$-element subsets (triplets) have sum at most $t$.
>
> ### Step 1: List all triplets
>
> A set of $5$ elements has
>
> $$
> \binom{5}{3} = 10
> $$
>
> triplets. They are:
>
> $$
> (a,b,c),\ (a,b,d),\ (a,b,e),\ (a,c,d),\ (a,c,e),\ (a,d,e),\ (b,c,d),\ (b,c,e),\ (b,d,e),\ (c,d,e).
> $$
>
> ### Step 2: Check each triplet sum
>
> For each triplet, compute its sum and test whether it is at most $t$.
>
> Define the indicator for each triplet by
>
> $$
> I(x,y,z)=
> \begin{cases}
> 1, & x+y+z \le t,\\
> 0, & x+y+z > t.
> \end{cases}
> $$
>
> Then the required number of triplets is
>
> $$
> N
> =
> I(a,b,c)+I(a,b,d)+I(a,b,e)+I(a,c,d)+I(a,c,e)+I(a,d,e)
> $$
> $$
> \qquad\qquad
> +I(b,c,d)+I(b,c,e)+I(b,d,e)+I(c,d,e).
> $$
>
> ### Step 3: Final expression
>
> Hence the number of triplets whose sum is $t$ or less is
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \sum_{\{x,y,z\}\subseteq \{a,b,c,d,e\}} \mathbf{1}(x+y+z \le t)
> }
> $$
>
> and explicitly,
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \mathbf{1}(a+b+c\le t)+\mathbf{1}(a+b+d\le t)+\mathbf{1}(a+b+e\le t)
> +\mathbf{1}(a+c+d\le t)+\mathbf{1}(a+c+e\le t)+\mathbf{1}(a+d+e\le t)
> +\mathbf{1}(b+c+d\le t)+\mathbf{1}(b+c+e\le t)+\mathbf{1}(b+d+e\le t)+\mathbf{1}(c+d+e\le t)
> }
> $$
>
> where each indicator contributes $1$ if the corresponding triplet sum is at most $t$, and $0$ otherwise. This total is the desired count.
