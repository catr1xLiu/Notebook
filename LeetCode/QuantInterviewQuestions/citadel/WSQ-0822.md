> [!example] Question 822 · Citadel
> ## Quant Trading Intern Interview

Given a biased coin (with probability p of landing heads), what is the probability of flipping two heads (HH) in two consecutive tosses?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=823)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let the probability of heads on a single flip be $p$, so the probability of tails is $1-p$.
>
> To obtain $HH$ as a consecutive outcome on two flips, both flips must be heads. Since the flips are independent,
>
> $$
> P(HH)=P(H)\cdot P(H)=p\cdot p=p^2.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability of success of $HH$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{p^2}.
> $$
