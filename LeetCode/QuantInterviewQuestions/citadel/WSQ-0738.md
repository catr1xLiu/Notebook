> [!example] Question 738 · Citadel
> ## Quantitative Analyst Interview

What is the expected value of a fair six-sided die?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=739)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The expected value of a fair six-sided die is found by averaging its possible outcomes.
>
> Let $X$ be the result of one roll. Then $X$ takes values $1,2,3,4,5,6$, each with probability $\frac{1}{6}$.
>
> Thus,
> $$
> E[X] = \sum_{x=1}^{6} x \cdot P(X=x)
> = 1\cdot \frac{1}{6} + 2\cdot \frac{1}{6} + 3\cdot \frac{1}{6} + 4\cdot \frac{1}{6} + 5\cdot \frac{1}{6} + 6\cdot \frac{1}{6}.
> $$
>
> Factor out $\frac{1}{6}$:
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{1}{6}(1+2+3+4+5+6).
> $$
>
> Since
> $$
> 1+2+3+4+5+6 = 21,
> $$
> we obtain
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{21}{6} = \frac{7}{2} = 3.5.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected value of the die is
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{7}{2}}.
> $$
