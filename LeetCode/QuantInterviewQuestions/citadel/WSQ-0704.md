> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

What is the angle between the minute hand and the hour hand at 12:15?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=705)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> At $12{:}15$:
>
> - The **minute hand** points at $15$ minutes, which is the $3$ on the clock, so its position is
>   $$
>   15 \times 6^\circ = 90^\circ
>   $$
>   from the $12$.
>
> - The **hour hand** moves continuously. In $15$ minutes, it moves
>   $$
>   \frac{15}{60} \times 30^\circ = 7.5^\circ
>   $$
>   from the $12$.
>
> Thus, the angle between the two hands is
> $$
> 90^\circ - 7.5^\circ = 82.5^\circ
> $$
>
> Therefore, the angle between the minute hand and hour hand at $12{:}15$ is
> $$
> \boxed{82.5^\circ}
> $$
