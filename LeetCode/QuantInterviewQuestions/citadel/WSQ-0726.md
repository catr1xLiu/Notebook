> [!example] Question 726 · Citadel
> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

Given an array of numbers, find three numbers such that their product is the maximum possible.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=727)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> ## Solution
>
> To maximize the product of three numbers in an array, observe that there are only two relevant possibilities:
>
> 1. The product of the **three largest numbers**.
> 2. The product of the **two smallest numbers** (possibly very negative) and the **largest number**.
>
> This is because:
>
> - If all large numbers are positive, the maximum product comes from the three largest values.
> - If the array contains negative numbers, then two very small negative numbers can produce a large positive product when multiplied together, and this may exceed the product of the three largest values.
>
> Thus, the answer is
>
> $$
> \max\left( a_{n-1}a_{n-2}a_{n-3},\; a_0a_1a_{n-1} \right)
> $$
>
> after sorting the array in nondecreasing order.
>
> ### Algorithm
>
> 1. Sort the array.
> 2. Compute:
>    - $p_1 =$ product of the three largest elements.
>    - $p_2 =$ product of the two smallest elements and the largest element.
> 3. Return $\max(p_1, p_2)$.
>
> ### Example
>
> For the array
>
> $$
> [-10, -10, 1, 3, 2]
> $$
>
> after sorting:
>
> $$
> [-10, -10, 1, 2, 3]
> $$
>
> Compute:
>
> $$
> p_1 = 3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 = 6
> $$
>
> $$
> p_2 = (-10)\cdot(-10)\cdot 3 = 300
> $$
>
> Hence the maximum product is
>
> $$
> 300
> $$
>
> and the three numbers are
>
> $$
> -10,\,-10,\,3
> $$
>
> ### Python implementation
>
> ```python
> def maximum_product_of_three(nums):
>     nums.sort()
>     return max(nums[-1] * nums[-2] * nums[-3],
>                nums[0] * nums[1] * nums[-1])
> ```
>
> ### Time complexity
>
> Sorting takes
>
> $$
> O(n \log n)
> $$
>
> so the overall time complexity is
>
> $$
> O(n \log n)
> $$
>
> with
>
> $$
> O(1)
> $$
>
> extra space apart from the sorting overhead.
>
> ### Final answer
>
> The maximum product of any three numbers in an array is the larger of:
>
> $$
> \text{(product of the three largest numbers)}
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \text{(product of the two smallest numbers and the largest number)}.
> $$
>
> So after sorting, compute
>
> $$
> \max\left( a_{n-1}a_{n-2}a_{n-3},\; a_0a_1a_{n-1} \right).
> $$
