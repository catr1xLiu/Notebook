> [!example] Question 679 · Citadel
> ## Software Engineer Interview

Given a set of numbers \[a, b, c, d, e\] and a target value t, find the number of triplets in the set whose sum is at most t.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=680)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let the five numbers be $a,b,c,d,e$, and let the set be written as
> $$
> S=\{a,b,c,d,e\}.
> $$
> We must count how many $3$-element subsets (triplets) of $S$ have sum at most $t$.
>
> ## Step 1: List all triplets
>
> A set of $5$ elements has
> $$
> \binom{5}{3}=10
> $$
> distinct triplets. They are:
> $$
> \{a,b,c\},\ \{a,b,d\},\ \{a,b,e\},\ \{a,c,d\},\ \{a,c,e\},\ \{a,d,e\},\ \{b,c,d\},\ \{b,c,e\},\ \{b,d,e\},\ \{c,d,e\}.
> $$
>
> ## Step 2: Check each triplet sum against $t$
>
> For each triplet, compute its sum and test whether it is at most $t$:
> $$
> a+b+c \le t,
> $$
> $$
> a+b+d \le t,
> $$
> $$
> a+b+e \le t,
> $$
> $$
> a+c+d \le t,
> $$
> $$
> a+c+e \le t,
> $$
> $$
> a+d+e \le t,
> $$
> $$
> b+c+d \le t,
> $$
> $$
> b+c+e \le t,
> $$
> $$
> b+d+e \le t,
> $$
> $$
> c+d+e \le t.
> $$
>
> ## Step 3: Count the true cases
>
> The required number is the count of inequalities above that are true. Equivalently, using indicator notation,
> $$
> N
> =
> \mathbf{1}_{\{a+b+c\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{a+b+d\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{a+b+e\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{a+c+d\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{a+c+e\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{a+d+e\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{b+c+d\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{b+c+e\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{b+d+e\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{c+d+e\le t\}}.
> $$
>
> Thus, the number of triplets whose sum is $t$ or less is exactly
> $$
> \boxed{
> \mathbf{1}_{\{a+b+c\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{a+b+d\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{a+b+e\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{a+c+d\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{a+c+e\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{a+d+e\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{b+c+d\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{b+c+e\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{b+d+e\le t\}}
> +\mathbf{1}_{\{c+d+e\le t\}}
> }.
> $$
>
> If actual numerical values for $a,b,c,d,e,$ and $t$ are given, evaluate these $10$ sums and count how many satisfy the condition.
