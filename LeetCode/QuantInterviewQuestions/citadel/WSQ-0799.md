> [!example] Question 799 · Citadel
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

What is the expected value of the cube of a fair six-sided die roll?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=800)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> For a fair six-sided die, let $X$ be the outcome, so that
>
> $$
> P(X=k)=\frac{1}{6}, \qquad k=1,2,3,4,5,6.
> $$
>
> The expected value of the cube of the roll is
>
> $$
> E[X^3]=\sum_{k=1}^6 k^3 \, P(X=k)
> =\frac{1}{6}\sum_{k=1}^6 k^3.
> $$
>
> Compute the sum:
>
> $$
> 1^3+2^3+3^3+4^3+5^3+6^3
> =1+8+27+64+125+216
> =441.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> E[X^3]=\frac{441}{6}=\frac{147}{2}=73.5.
> $$
>
> Thus, the expected value is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{147}{2}}.
> $$
