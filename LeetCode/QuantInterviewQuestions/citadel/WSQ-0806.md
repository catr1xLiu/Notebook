> [!example] Question 806 · Citadel
> ## Summer Intern Interview

What is the expected value when rolling two fair six-sided dice?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=807)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> For one fair six-sided die, the expected value is
>
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6} = \frac{21}{6} = 3.5.
> $$
>
> If $X$ and $Y$ are the results of two dice, then the expected value of their sum is
>
> $$
> E[X+Y] = E[X] + E[Y] = 3.5 + 3.5 = 7.
> $$
>
> Thus, the expected value of two dice (their total) is
>
> $$
> \boxed{7}.
> $$
>
> If instead one means the expected value of each individual die, it is
>
> $$
> \boxed{3.5}.
> $$
