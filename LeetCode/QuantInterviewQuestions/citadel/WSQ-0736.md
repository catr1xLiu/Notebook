> [!example] Question 736 · Citadel
> ## Quantitative Analyst Interview

Suppose you roll two fair six-sided dice. What is the probability that the sum of the two dice is equal to 8?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=737)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the probability that the sum of two fair six-sided dice is $8$, count:
>
> 1. **Total possible outcomes**:  
>    Each die has $6$ outcomes, so
>    $$
>    6 \times 6 = 36
>    $$
>    equally likely outcomes.
>
> 2. **Favorable outcomes**:  
>    The pairs that sum to $8$ are
>    $$
>    (2,6),\ (3,5),\ (4,4),\ (5,3),\ (6,2)
>    $$
>    so there are $5$ favorable outcomes.
>
> Therefore, the probability is
> $$
> \frac{5}{36}.
> $$
>
> So, the answer is
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{36}}.
> $$
