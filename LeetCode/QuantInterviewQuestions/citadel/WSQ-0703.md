> [!example] Question 703 · Citadel
> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

What is the expected number of samples drawn from a uniform distribution on \[0,1\] required so that their sum exceeds 1?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=704)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $N$ be the minimum number of independent samples $X_1, X_2, \dots$ from the uniform distribution on $[0,1]$ such that
>
> $$
> X_1 + X_2 + \cdots + X_N > 1.
> $$
>
> We seek $E[N]$.
>
> ## Step 1: Express $E[N]$ via tail probabilities
>
> For any positive-integer-valued random variable $N$,
>
> $$
> E[N] = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \Pr(N > n).
> $$
>
> Here, the event $N > n$ means that after taking $n$ samples, the sum has still not exceeded $1$, i.e.
>
> $$
> N > n \iff X_1 + \cdots + X_n \le 1.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> E[N] = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \Pr(X_1 + \cdots + X_n \le 1).
> $$
>
> For $n=0$, the empty sum is $0$, so
>
> $$
> \Pr(X_1 + \cdots + X_0 \le 1) = 1.
> $$
>
> Hence,
>
> $$
> E[N] = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \Pr(S_n \le 1),
> $$
>
> where $S_n = X_1 + \cdots + X_n$.
>
> ## Step 2: Compute $\Pr(S_n \le 1)$
>
> Since the $X_i$ are independent and uniform on $[0,1]$, the probability
>
> $$
> \Pr(S_n \le 1)
> $$
>
> is the volume in the unit cube $[0,1]^n$ of the region
>
> $$
> \{(x_1,\dots,x_n) : x_1+\cdots+x_n \le 1\}.
> $$
>
> This region is the standard $n$-simplex, whose volume is
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{n!}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \Pr(S_n \le 1) = \frac{1}{n!}.
> $$
>
> ## Step 3: Sum the series
>
> Substituting into the expectation formula gives
>
> $$
> E[N] = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n!}.
> $$
>
> But this is the exponential series:
>
> $$
> \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n!} = e.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> E[N] = e.
> $$
>
> ## Final answer
>
> The expected number of samples required is
>
> $$
> \boxed{e \approx 2.71828}.
> $$
