> [!example] Question 821 · Citadel
> ## Quant Trading Intern Interview

What is the expected value of the sum when rolling two fair six-sided dice?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=822)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ and $Y$ be the outcomes of the two fair six-sided dice, and let
>
> $$
> S = X + Y
> $$
>
> be their sum.
>
> Each die is equally likely to show any value in $\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$, so for one die,
>
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6} = \frac{21}{6} = 3.5
> $$
>
> Similarly,
>
> $$
> E[Y] = 3.5
> $$
>
> Using linearity of expectation,
>
> $$
> E[S] = E[X+Y] = E[X] + E[Y] = 3.5 + 3.5 = 7
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected value of the sum of two dice is
>
> $$
> \boxed{7}
> $$
