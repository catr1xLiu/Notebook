> [!example] Question 732 · Citadel
> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

Given that log X is normally distributed with mean 0 and variance 1, compute the expected value of X.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=733)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $Y=\log X$. Since $Y \sim N(0,1)$, we have
>
> $$
> X=e^Y.
> $$
>
> Thus $X$ has a lognormal distribution with parameters $\mu=0$ and $\sigma^2=1$.
>
> For a lognormal random variable $X=e^Y$ with $Y \sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$,
>
> $$
> \mathbb{E}[X]=\mathbb{E}[e^Y]=e^{\mu+\sigma^2/2}.
> $$
>
> Substituting $\mu=0$ and $\sigma^2=1$ gives
>
> $$
> \mathbb{E}[X]=e^{0+1/2}=e^{1/2}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\mathbb{E}[X]=\sqrt{e}}.
> $$
