> [!example] Question 903 · Optiver
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

Estimate how many ways there are to add three positive integers to obtain a sum of 25.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=904)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To count the number of ways to write $25$ as a sum of three positive integers, we seek the number of solutions to
>
> $$
> x_1 + x_2 + x_3 = 25
> $$
>
> where $x_1, x_2, x_3 \ge 1$ are integers.
>
> ## Method: Stars and Bars
>
> For positive integer solutions to
>
> $$
> x_1 + x_2 + x_3 = n,
> $$
>
> the number of solutions is
>
> $$
> \binom{n-1}{3-1}.
> $$
>
> Here $n=25$, so the number of solutions is
>
> $$
> \binom{24}{2}.
> $$
>
> Compute:
>
> $$
> \binom{24}{2} = \frac{24 \cdot 23}{2} = 276.
> $$
>
> ## Conclusion
>
> The number of ways is
>
> $$
> \boxed{276}.
> $$
>
> If instead one means unordered triples (so that, for example, $1+2+22$ and $22+2+1$ are considered the same), then the count would be different. Under the usual ordered interpretation, the answer is
>
> $$
> \boxed{276}.
> $$
