> [!example] Question 963 · Optiver
> ## Quantitative Analyst Interview

In a sequence of fair coin tosses with each outcome being either Heads (H) or Tails (T), what is the probability that the sequence HHH appears before HTH?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=964)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let the desired probability be $p$, where $p$ is the probability that the pattern $\text{HHH}$ appears before the pattern $\text{HTH}$ in an infinite sequence of fair coin tosses.
>
> ## Step 1: Define states
>
> Track the relevant suffix of the current toss sequence, namely the longest suffix that is also a prefix of either target pattern.
>
> The target patterns are:
>
> - $\text{HHH}$
> - $\text{HTH}$
>
> The useful states are:
>
> - $S$: start state, no relevant suffix
> - $H$: last toss gives suffix $\text{H}$
> - $HH$: suffix $\text{HH}$
> - $HT$: suffix $\text{HT}$
>
> Let the corresponding probabilities of eventually getting $\text{HHH}$ before $\text{HTH}$ be:
>
> - $p_S$
> - $p_H$
> - $p_{HH}$
> - $p_{HT}$
>
> We want $p_S$.
>
> Also note the absorbing outcomes:
>
> - From state $HH$, tossing $\text{H}$ produces $\text{HHH}$, so success probability is $1$.
> - From state $HT$, tossing $\text{H}$ produces $\text{HTH}$, so success probability is $0$.
>
> ## Step 2: Write recurrence equations
>
> From the start state $S$:
>
> - Toss $\text{H}$ with probability $\frac12$, moving to $H$
> - Toss $\text{T}$ with probability $\frac12$, remaining effectively at $S$
>
> Thus,
> $$
> p_S=\frac12 p_H+\frac12 p_S.
> $$
>
> From state $H$:
>
> - Toss $\text{H}$ with probability $\frac12$, moving to $HH$
> - Toss $\text{T}$ with probability $\frac12$, moving to $HT$
>
> So,
> $$
> p_H=\frac12 p_{HH}+\frac12 p_{HT}.
> $$
>
> From state $HH$:
>
> - Toss $\text{H}$ with probability $\frac12$, obtaining $\text{HHH}$ first, so probability $1$
> - Toss $\text{T}$ with probability $\frac12$; the new relevant suffix is $HT$
>
> Hence,
> $$
> p_{HH}=\frac12\cdot 1+\frac12 p_{HT}.
> $$
>
> From state $HT$:
>
> - Toss $\text{H}$ with probability $\frac12$, obtaining $\text{HTH}$ first, so probability $0$
> - Toss $\text{T}$ with probability $\frac12$; no relevant suffix remains, so return to $S$
>
> Thus,
> $$
> p_{HT}=\frac12\cdot 0+\frac12 p_S=\frac12 p_S.
> $$
>
> ## Step 3: Solve the system
>
> From
> $$
> p_S=\frac12 p_H+\frac12 p_S,
> $$
> we get
> $$
> p_S=p_H.
> $$
>
> Then
> $$
> p_{HT}=\frac12 p_S.
> $$
>
> Next,
> $$
> p_{HH}=\frac12+\frac12 p_{HT}
> =\frac12+\frac12\cdot \frac12 p_S
> =\frac12+\frac14 p_S.
> $$
>
> Now substitute into
> $$
> p_H=\frac12 p_{HH}+\frac12 p_{HT}.
> $$
>
> Since $p_H=p_S$, this gives
> $$
> p_S=\frac12\left(\frac12+\frac14 p_S\right)+\frac12\left(\frac12 p_S\right).
> $$
>
> Simplify:
> $$
> p_S=\frac14+\frac18 p_S+\frac14 p_S
> =\frac14+\frac38 p_S.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
> $$
> p_S-\frac38 p_S=\frac14,
> $$
> so
> $$
> \frac58 p_S=\frac14.
> $$
>
> Hence,
> $$
> p_S=\frac14\cdot \frac85=\frac25.
> $$
>
> ## Final answer
>
> The probability that $\text{HHH}$ appears before $\text{HTH}$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{2}{5}}.
> $$
