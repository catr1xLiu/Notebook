> [!example] Question 893 · Optiver
> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

Identify any arbitrage opportunities, if any, in the given matrix of currency conversion rates.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=894)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To identify an arbitrage opportunity in a matrix of currency conversion rates, one must check whether there exists a cycle of currencies whose product of exchange rates exceeds $1$.
>
> ## Criterion for Arbitrage
>
> Suppose the exchange-rate matrix is $R = (r_{ij})$, where $r_{ij}$ is the amount of currency $j$ obtained from one unit of currency $i$. An arbitrage opportunity exists if there is a sequence of currencies
> $$
> i_1 \to i_2 \to \cdots \to i_k \to i_1
> $$
> such that
> $$
> r_{i_1 i_2} \, r_{i_2 i_3} \cdots r_{i_k i_1} > 1.
> $$
> This means that starting with $1$ unit of currency $i_1$, converting around the cycle yields more than $1$ unit of the original currency.
>
> ## What Is Needed
>
> The actual matrix of conversion rates is required in order to compute these products and determine whether arbitrage exists.
>
> ## Conclusion
>
> Since the matrix of currency conversion rates was not provided, no specific arbitrage opportunity can be identified. Once the matrix is given, check all currency cycles and look for any product greater than $1$; such a cycle is an arbitrage opportunity.
