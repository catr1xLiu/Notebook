> [!example] Question 996 · Optiver
> ## Trader Interview

Calculate the square roots of the following numbers: 600, 6000, 60000, and 24000.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=997)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Use nearby perfect squares and factor out powers of $10$.
>
> $$
> \sqrt{600}=\sqrt{100\cdot 6}=10\sqrt{6}\approx 10(2.449)=24.49
> $$
>
> $$
> \sqrt{6000}=\sqrt{1000\cdot 6}=10\sqrt{60}=10\sqrt{4\cdot 15}=20\sqrt{15}\approx 20(3.873)=77.46
> $$
>
> Equivalently,
>
> $$
> \sqrt{6000}=10\sqrt{60}\approx 77.46
> $$
>
> $$
> \sqrt{60000}=\sqrt{10000\cdot 6}=100\sqrt{6}\approx 100(2.449)=244.95
> $$
>
> $$
> \sqrt{24000}=\sqrt{1000\cdot 24}=10\sqrt{240}=10\sqrt{16\cdot 15}=40\sqrt{15}\approx 40(3.873)=154.92
> $$
>
> Final values:
>
> - $$\sqrt{600}\approx 24.49$$
> - $$\sqrt{6000}\approx 77.46$$
> - $$\sqrt{60000}\approx 244.95$$
> - $$\sqrt{24000}\approx 154.92$$
