> [!example] Question 962 · Optiver
> ## Quantitative Analyst Interview

1. What is 73 × 87? Provide your answer without using a calculator and explain your reasoning. 2. What is the probability of drawing exactly two aces from a shuffled deck of cards?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=963)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To compute the probability of drawing exactly two aces from a standard $52$-card deck, one must first specify how many cards are being drawn. The natural interpretation is:
>
> A hand of $5$ cards is drawn uniformly at random from a shuffled $52$-card deck. What is the probability that the hand contains exactly $2$ aces?
>
> ## Approach
>
> Use counting:
>
> - Count the number of $5$-card hands with exactly $2$ aces.
> - Divide by the total number of possible $5$-card hands.
>
> ## Step 1: Count favorable hands
>
> There are $4$ aces in the deck, and we want exactly $2$ of them:
>
> $$
> \binom{4}{2}
> $$
>
> The remaining $3$ cards must be chosen from the $48$ non-aces:
>
> $$
> \binom{48}{3}
> $$
>
> Thus the number of favorable hands is
>
> $$
> \binom{4}{2}\binom{48}{3}.
> $$
>
> ## Step 2: Count total hands
>
> The total number of $5$-card hands from $52$ cards is
>
> $$
> \binom{52}{5}.
> $$
>
> ## Step 3: Form the probability
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> P(\text{exactly }2\text{ aces})=\frac{\binom{4}{2}\binom{48}{3}}{\binom{52}{5}}.
> $$
>
> ## Step 4: Simplify numerically
>
> Compute each binomial coefficient:
>
> $$
> \binom{4}{2}=6,
> \qquad
> \binom{48}{3}=\frac{48\cdot 47\cdot 46}{3\cdot 2\cdot 1}=17{,}296,
> $$
>
> so the numerator is
>
> $$
> 6\cdot 17{,}296=103{,}776.
> $$
>
> Also,
>
> $$
> \binom{52}{5}=2{,}598{,}960.
> $$
>
> Hence
>
> $$
> P=\frac{103{,}776}{2{,}598{,}960}\approx 0.03993.
> $$
>
> ## Final answer
>
> $$
> \boxed{P(\text{exactly }2\text{ aces in a }5\text{-card hand})=\frac{\binom{4}{2}\binom{48}{3}}{\binom{52}{5}}\approx 0.03993}
> $$
>
> So the probability is about
>
> $$
> \boxed{3.99\%}.
> $$
>
> For the mental-math example,
>
> $$
> 73\times 87=(80-7)(80+7)=80^2-7^2=6400-49=6351.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \boxed{73\times 87=6351.}
> $$
