> [!example] Question 901 · Optiver
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

Divide 2 by 17 to four decimal places without using any calculator or external materials.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=902)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To compute $\dfrac{2}{17}$ to $4$ decimal places, use long division.
>
> We divide $2.0000\ldots$ by $17$:
>
> - $17$ goes into $2$ exactly $0$ times, so the quotient begins with $0.$
> - Bring down a $0$: $20 \div 17 = 1$, remainder $20 - 17 = 3$
> - Bring down a $0$: $30 \div 17 = 1$, remainder $30 - 17 = 13$
> - Bring down a $0$: $130 \div 17 = 7$, remainder $130 - 119 = 11$
> - Bring down a $0$: $110 \div 17 = 6$, remainder $110 - 102 = 8$
> - Bring down a $0$: $80 \div 17 = 4$, remainder $80 - 68 = 12$
>
> Thus,
> $$
> \frac{2}{17} = 0.11764\ldots
> $$
>
> To $4$ decimal places, look at the first $5$ decimal digits: $0.11764$.
>
> The first $4$ decimal places are $0.1176$, and the next digit is $4$, so no rounding up is needed.
>
> $$
> \frac{2}{17} \approx 0.1176
> $$
>
> So the answer is
>
> $$
> \boxed{0.1176}
> $$
