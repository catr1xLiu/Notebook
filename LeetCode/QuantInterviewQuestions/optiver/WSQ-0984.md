> [!example] Question 984 · Optiver
> ## Trader Interview

What is the result of 2/5 + 11/4?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=985)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To add the fractions $\frac{2}{5}$ and $\frac{11}{4}$, first find a common denominator.
>
> The least common denominator of $5$ and $4$ is $20$.
>
> Rewrite each fraction with denominator $20$:
> $$
> \frac{2}{5} = \frac{8}{20}, \qquad \frac{11}{4} = \frac{55}{20}
> $$
>
> Now add:
> $$
> \frac{8}{20} + \frac{55}{20} = \frac{63}{20}
> $$
>
> Thus,
> $$
> \frac{2}{5} + \frac{11}{4} = \frac{63}{20}
> $$
>
> In mixed-number form,
> $$
> \frac{63}{20} = 3\frac{3}{20}.
> $$
