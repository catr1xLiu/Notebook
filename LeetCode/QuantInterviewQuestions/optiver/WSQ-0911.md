> [!example] Question 911 · Optiver
> ## Quant Trader Intern Interview

I throw two fair 6-sided dice. Which expected value is larger: the expected value of the smaller number, or the expected value of the difference between the two numbers?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=912)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let the two dice rolls be $X$ and $Y$, where $X,Y \in \{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$ are independent and each outcome is equally likely.
>
> We compare the expectations of:
>
> - the smaller die: $\min(X,Y)$,
> - the difference between the dice: $|X-Y|$.
>
> ## 1. Expected value of the smaller die
>
> Use the identity
> $$
> E[\min(X,Y)] = \sum_{k=1}^{6} P(\min(X,Y)\ge k).
> $$
>
> Now,
> $$
> P(\min(X,Y)\ge k)=P(X\ge k \text{ and } Y\ge k).
> $$
> Since the dice are independent,
> $$
> P(\min(X,Y)\ge k)=\left(\frac{7-k}{6}\right)^2.
> $$
> Therefore,
> $$
> E[\min(X,Y)] = \sum_{k=1}^{6} \left(\frac{7-k}{6}\right)^2
> = \frac{1^2+2^2+3^2+4^2+5^2+6^2}{36}.
> $$
> Compute:
> $$
> 1^2+2^2+3^2+4^2+5^2+6^2 = 91.
> $$
> Hence,
> $$
> E[\min(X,Y)] = \frac{91}{36}.
> $$
>
> ## 2. Expected value of the difference
>
> We compute $E[|X-Y|]$. For each possible difference $d$:
>
> - $d=0$: there are $6$ outcomes,
> - $d=1$: there are $10$ outcomes,
> - $d=2$: there are $8$ outcomes,
> - $d=3$: there are $6$ outcomes,
> - $d=4$: there are $4$ outcomes,
> - $d=5$: there are $2$ outcomes.
>
> Thus,
> $$
> E[|X-Y|]
> = \frac{0\cdot 6 + 1\cdot 10 + 2\cdot 8 + 3\cdot 6 + 4\cdot 4 + 5\cdot 2}{36}.
> $$
> Compute the numerator:
> $$
> 10+16+18+16+10=70.
> $$
> So,
> $$
> E[|X-Y|] = \frac{70}{36} = \frac{35}{18}.
> $$
>
> ## 3. Comparison
>
> Compare
> $$
> \frac{91}{36} \quad \text{and} \quad \frac{70}{36}.
> $$
> Since
> $$
> \frac{91}{36} > \frac{70}{36},
> $$
> the expected value of the smaller die is larger.
>
> Moreover, the difference is
> $$
> \frac{91}{36}-\frac{70}{36}=\frac{21}{36}=\frac{7}{12}.
> $$
>
> ## Final answer
>
> The **smaller die** has the larger expected value.
>
> $$
> E[\min(X,Y)] = \frac{91}{36}, \qquad E[|X-Y|] = \frac{35}{18} = \frac{70}{36}.
> $$
>
> So the smaller die exceeds the difference in expectation by
>
> $$
> \frac{7}{12}.
> $$
