> [!example] Question 950 · Optiver
> ## Trading Interview

How many cards are needed to build a 100-storey house of cards, assuming each storey is constructed in the standard way?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=951)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To count the cards in a standard triangular house of cards with $100$ stories, count:
>
> 1. the **leaning pairs** that form the inverted $V$ supports, and  
> 2. the **horizontal cards** laid between adjacent supports.
>
> ## 1. Number of leaning pairs
>
> On the bottom level there are $100$ pairs, on the next level $99$, and so on, up to $1$ pair at the top.
>
> Thus the total number of pairs is
>
> $$
> 1+2+3+\cdots+100=\frac{100\cdot 101}{2}=5050.
> $$
>
> Each pair uses $2$ cards, so the number of leaning cards is
>
> $$
> 2\cdot 5050=10100.
> $$
>
> ## 2. Number of horizontal cards
>
> Between the pairs, horizontal cards are placed.  
> On the bottom level there are $99$, then $98$, and so on, up to $1$.
>
> So the total number of horizontal cards is
>
> $$
> 1+2+3+\cdots+99=\frac{99\cdot 100}{2}=4950.
> $$
>
> ## 3. Total number of cards
>
> Therefore the total number of cards is
>
> $$
> 10100+4950=15050.
> $$
>
> Hence, a $100$-storey house of cards requires
>
> $$
> \boxed{15050}
> $$
>
> cards.
