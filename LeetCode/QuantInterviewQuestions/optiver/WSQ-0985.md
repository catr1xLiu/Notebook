> ## Trader Interview

What are the first 5 decimal digits in the decimal expansion of 1/13?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=986)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute
> $$
> \frac{1}{13} = 0.\overline{076923}.
> $$
>
> The decimal expansion repeats with period $6$, so the first $5$ digits after the decimal point are
> $$
> 0,\ 7,\ 6,\ 9,\ 2.
> $$
>
> Hence, the first $5$ decimal digits in $\frac{1}{13}$ are
> $$
> 07692.
> $$
