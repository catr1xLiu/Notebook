> [!example] Question 913 · Optiver
> ## Quant Trader Intern Interview

How many prime numbers are there from 1 to 10,000?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=914)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> A prime number is an integer greater than $1$ whose only positive divisors are $1$ and itself. Thus the question asks for the value of the prime-counting function
>
> $$
> \pi(10000),
> $$
>
> the number of primes less than or equal to $10000$.
>
> Listing all primes up to $10000$ is possible but tedious. This value is a standard known result:
>
> $$
> \pi(10000)=1229.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the number of prime numbers from $1$ to $10000$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{1229}.
> $$
