> [!example] Question 912 · Optiver
> ## Quant Trader Intern Interview

What is the next term in the sequence: 1/1, 1/2, 1/3, 1/5, 1/8?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=913)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The denominators form the sequence
>
> $$
> 1,\;2,\;3,\;5,\;8
> $$
>
> Each term after the first two is the sum of the previous two:
>
> $$
> 1+2=3,\quad 2+3=5,\quad 3+5=8
> $$
>
> So the next denominator is
>
> $$
> 5+8=13
> $$
>
> Hence the next term is
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{13}
> $$
