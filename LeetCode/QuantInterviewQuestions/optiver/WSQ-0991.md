> [!example] Question 991 · Optiver
> ## Trader Interview

What is the value of 3/6 + 7/4?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=992)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute with a common denominator.
>
> The least common denominator of $6$ and $4$ is $12$. Rewrite each fraction:
>
> $$
> \frac{3}{6} = \frac{6}{12}, \qquad \frac{7}{4} = \frac{21}{12}.
> $$
>
> Now add:
>
> $$
> \frac{6}{12} + \frac{21}{12} = \frac{27}{12}.
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> \frac{27}{12} = \frac{9}{4}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the sum is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{9}{4}}.
> $$
