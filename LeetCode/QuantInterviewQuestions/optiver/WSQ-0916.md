> [!example] Question 916 · Optiver
> ## Quantitative Trading Intern Interview

What is the result of dividing 3/4 by 1/2? Show your calculations.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=917)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> ## Dividing Fractions Mentally
>
> To divide fractions mentally, use the rule
>
> $$
> \frac{a}{b} \div \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a}{b} \times \frac{d}{c}.
> $$
>
> That is, **keep the first fraction, change division to multiplication, and flip the second fraction**.
>
> ### Example: $\frac{3}{4} \div \frac{1}{2}$
>
> Apply the rule:
>
> $$
> \frac{3}{4} \div \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{2}{1}.
> $$
>
> Now multiply:
>
> $$
> \frac{3 \times 2}{4 \times 1} = \frac{6}{4}.
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> \frac{6}{4} = \frac{3}{2} = 1\frac{1}{2}.
> $$
>
> So,
>
> $$
> \frac{3}{4} \div \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2} = 1\frac{1}{2}.
> $$
>
> ## Mental Shortcut
>
> Dividing by $\frac{1}{2}$ is the same as multiplying by $2$. Thus,
>
> $$
> \frac{3}{4} \div \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{4} \times 2 = \frac{6}{4} = \frac{3}{2}.
> $$
>
> ## Another Quick Example
>
> $$
> \frac{2}{3} \div \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{3} \times 4 = \frac{8}{3} = 2\frac{2}{3}.
> $$
>
> ## Final Answer
>
> $$
> \frac{3}{4} \div \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2} = 1\frac{1}{2}.
> $$
