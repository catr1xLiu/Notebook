> [!example] Question 946 · Optiver
> ## Quantitative Trader Intern Interview

Calculate the value of 1.02 multiplied by (19.25 minus 0.36).

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=947)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute the expression step by step:
> $$
> 1.02 \times (19.25 - 0.36)
> $$
>
> First evaluate the parentheses:
> $$
> 19.25 - 0.36 = 18.89
> $$
>
> Then multiply:
> $$
> 18.89 \times 1.02 = 18.89 + 0.02 \times 18.89
> $$
> $$
> = 18.89 + 0.3778 = 19.2678
> $$
>
> Therefore, the value is
> $$
> \boxed{19.2678}
> $$
