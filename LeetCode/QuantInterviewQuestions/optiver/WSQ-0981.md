> [!example] Question 981 · Optiver
> ## Summer Intern Interview

Make a market on the percentage of the Earth's Equator that passes through land.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=982)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To make a market, estimate the fraction of the Equator’s total circumference that lies over land.
>
> The Equator passes through land in the following countries, moving around the globe:
>
> - Ecuador
> - Colombia
> - Brazil
> - São Tomé and Príncipe
> - Gabon
> - Republic of the Congo
> - Democratic Republic of the Congo
> - Uganda
> - Kenya
> - Somalia
> - Indonesia
>
> Most of the Equator is over ocean, with only relatively short segments crossing South America, Africa, and Indonesian islands.
>
> A reasonable geographic estimate is:
>
> - South America: about $4{,}500$ km
> - Africa: about $4{,}800$ km
> - Indonesia and islands: about $2{,}000$ km
>
> So total land distance is approximately
>
> $$
> 4{,}500 + 4{,}800 + 2{,}000 = 11{,}300 \text{ km}.
> $$
>
> The Earth’s equatorial circumference is about
>
> $$
> 40{,}075 \text{ km}.
> $$
>
> Thus the land fraction is
>
> $$
> \frac{11{,}300}{40{,}075} \approx 0.282.
> $$
>
> So the percentage is about
>
> $$
> 28.2\%.
> $$
>
> For a prediction market, a practical market might be centered slightly lower to reflect uncertainty in coastline definitions and exact island crossings. A good market would be:
>
> $$
> 25\% \text{ to } 30\%
> $$
>
> with a midpoint near
>
> $$
> 27\%.
> $$
>
> Hence a sensible quoted market is:
>
> $$
> 27\% \pm 3\%.
> $$
>
> Final answer: make the market at approximately **$27\%$**, say **$24\%$ bid / $30\%$ offer**.
