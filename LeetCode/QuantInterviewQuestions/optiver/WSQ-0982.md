> [!example] Question 982 · Optiver
> ## Trader Interview

If a fair coin is flipped until the first heads appears, what is the expected number of flips?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=983)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the number of flips until the first heads appears. Since the coin is fair, each flip has probability $\tfrac12$ of being heads, so $X$ has a geometric distribution with parameter $p=\tfrac12$.
>
> For a geometric random variable counting the trial of the first success,
>
> $$
> \mathbb{E}[X]=\frac{1}{p}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \mathbb{E}[X]=\frac{1}{1/2}=2.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected number of flips is
>
> $$
> \boxed{2}.
> $$
