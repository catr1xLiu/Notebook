> [!example] Question 914 · Optiver
> ## Jr Trader Interview

What is the result of dividing 335 by 7?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=915)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To compute $335 \div 7$, note that
>
> $$
> 7 \times 47 = 329
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> 335 - 329 = 6.
> $$
>
> So the quotient is $47$ with remainder $6$.
>
> $$
> 335 \div 7 = 47 \tfrac{6}{7}
> $$
>
> Final answer:
>
> $$
> 47 \text{ remainder } 6
> $$
>
> or equivalently
>
> $$
> \frac{335}{7} = 47\tfrac{6}{7}.
> $$
