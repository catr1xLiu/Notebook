> [!example] Question 951 · Optiver
> ## Internship Interview

Estimate how many digits are in 100 factorial (100\!).

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=952)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To estimate how many integers appear in the product $100!$, interpret the question as asking for the number of decimal digits of $100!$.
>
> The number of digits of a positive integer $N$ is
>
> $$
> \lfloor \log_{10} N \rfloor + 1.
> $$
>
> Thus we need
>
> $$
> \lfloor \log_{10}(100!) \rfloor + 1.
> $$
>
> Using Stirling’s approximation,
>
> $$
> n! \approx \sqrt{2\pi n}\left(\frac{n}{e}\right)^n,
> $$
>
> so for $n=100$,
>
> $$
> \log_{10}(100!) \approx \log_{10}\!\left(\sqrt{2\pi\cdot 100}\right) + 100\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{100}{e}\right).
> $$
>
> Compute the terms:
>
> $$
> \log_{10}\!\left(\sqrt{200\pi}\right)
> = \frac12 \log_{10}(200\pi)
> \approx \frac12 \log_{10}(628.319)
> \approx \frac12(2.798)
> \approx 1.399,
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> 100\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{100}{e}\right)
> = 100(\log_{10}100 - \log_{10}e)
> = 100(2 - 0.4343)
> = 156.57.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \log_{10}(100!) \approx 1.399 + 156.57 = 157.969.
> $$
>
> Hence the number of digits is
>
> $$
> \lfloor 157.969 \rfloor + 1 = 158.
> $$
>
> So $100!$ contains approximately $\boxed{158}$ digits.
