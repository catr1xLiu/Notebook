> ## Quantitative Researcher Intern Interview

How many digits are there in 100\! (100 factorial)?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=979)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To determine the number of digits in $100!$, use the fact that a positive integer $N$ has
>
> $$
> \lfloor \log_{10} N \rfloor + 1
> $$
>
> digits.
>
> Thus the number of digits in $100!$ is
>
> $$
> \left\lfloor \log_{10}(100!) \right\rfloor + 1.
> $$
>
> Using properties of logarithms,
>
> $$
> \log_{10}(100!)=\log_{10}(1\cdot 2\cdot 3\cdots 100)
> =\sum_{k=1}^{100}\log_{10} k.
> $$
>
> Evaluating this sum gives
>
> $$
> \log_{10}(100!) \approx 157.9700036547.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \left\lfloor 157.9700036547 \right\rfloor + 1 = 157 + 1 = 158.
> $$
>
> Hence, $100!$ has
>
> $$
> \boxed{158}
> $$
>
> digits.
