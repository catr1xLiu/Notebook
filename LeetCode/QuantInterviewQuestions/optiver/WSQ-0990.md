> [!example] Question 990 · Optiver
> ## Trader Interview

What is the expected value of a single six-sided dice roll if you are allowed to re-roll once, and keep the higher number from the two rolls?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=991)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the result of the first fair six-sided die roll.
>
> After seeing $X=x$, the optimal strategy is:
>
> - keep $x$ if its value is at least as large as the expected value of a fresh roll,
> - otherwise reroll once.
>
> A fresh roll has expected value
>
> $$
> E[\text{fresh roll}] = \frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6} = 3.5.
> $$
>
> So it is better to reroll when $x<3.5$, that is, when $x=1,2,3$, and to keep the result when $x=4,5,6$.
>
> Hence the expected payoff is
>
> $$
> E = \frac{1}{6}(3.5+3.5+3.5+4+5+6).
> $$
>
> Compute:
>
> $$
> E=\frac{10.5+15}{6}=\frac{25.5}{6}=4.25.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected value is
>
> $$
> \boxed{4.25 = \frac{17}{4}}.
> $$
