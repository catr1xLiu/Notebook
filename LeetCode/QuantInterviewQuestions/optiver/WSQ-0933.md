> [!example] Question 933 · Optiver
> ## Software Engineer Interview

Given two dates (for example, 12/21/2001 and 4/13/2007), calculate the number of days between them.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=934)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the number of days between $12/21/2001$ and $4/13/2007$, count the days from $12/21/2001$ up to $4/13/2007$.
>
> ## Step 1: Count full years after $12/21/2001$
>
> From $12/21/2001$ to $12/21/2006$ is $5$ full years.
>
> These years contribute:
>
> - $12/21/2001 \to 12/21/2002$: $365$ days
> - $12/21/2002 \to 12/21/2003$: $365$ days
> - $12/21/2003 \to 12/21/2004$: $366$ days since $2004$ is a leap year
> - $12/21/2004 \to 12/21/2005$: $365$ days
> - $12/21/2005 \to 12/21/2006$: $365$ days
>
> So the total for these $5$ years is
>
> $$
> 365+365+366+365+365=1826
> $$
>
> ## Step 2: Count days from $12/21/2006$ to $4/13/2007$
>
> Break this into months:
>
> - $12/21/2006 \to 12/31/2006$: $10$ days
> - January $2007$: $31$ days
> - February $2007$: $28$ days
> - March $2007$: $31$ days
> - $4/1/2007 \to 4/13/2007$: $13$ days
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 10+31+28+31+13=113
> $$
>
> ## Step 3: Add the totals
>
> $$
> 1826+113=1939
> $$
>
> ## Final Answer
>
> The number of days between $12/21/2001$ and $4/13/2007$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{1939}
> $$
>
> If both the start date and end date are counted inclusively, then the count is
>
> $$
> \boxed{1940}
> $$
