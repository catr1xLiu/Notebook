> [!example] Question 993 · Optiver
> ## Trader Interview

What is the formula to calculate the angle between the hour and minute hands of a clock at a given time?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=994)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let the time be $h$ hours and $m$ minutes.
>
> - The minute hand moves at $6^\circ$ per minute, so its angle from $12$ o’clock is
> $$
> \theta_m = 6m.
> $$
>
> - The hour hand moves at $30^\circ$ per hour and also $0.5^\circ$ per minute, so its angle from $12$ o’clock is
> $$
> \theta_h = 30h + 0.5m.
> $$
>
> Hence the angle between the two hands is
> $$
> \theta = \left| \theta_h - \theta_m \right|
> = \left| 30h + 0.5m - 6m \right|
> = \left| 30h - 5.5m \right|.
> $$
>
> Since the smaller angle between the hands is usually desired, the formula is
> $$
> \boxed{\theta = \min\!\left(\left|30h - 5.5m\right|,\; 360 - \left|30h - 5.5m\right|\right)}.
> $$
>
> If $h$ is given on a $12$-hour clock, use $h \in \{0,1,\dots,11\}$ by replacing $12$ with $0$ if needed.
