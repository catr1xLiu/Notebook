> [!example] Question 1007 · Optiver
> ## Trader Interview

What are the six inputs in an equity option pricing model?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1008)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The standard six inputs in an equity option pricing model (such as Black–Scholes) are:
>
> 1. **Current stock price**  
>    $S_0$
>
> 2. **Exercise (strike) price**  
>    $K$
>
> 3. **Time to expiration**  
>    $T$
>
> 4. **Risk-free interest rate**  
>    $r$
>
> 5. **Volatility of the stock price**  
>    $\sigma$
>
> 6. **Expected dividends on the stock**  
>    Often represented by a dividend yield $q$ or by discrete dividend amounts
>
> Thus, the six inputs are
> $$
> S_0,\; K,\; T,\; r,\; \sigma,\; q.
> $$
