> [!example] Question 998 · Optiver
> ## Trader Interview

What is the smallest multiple of 77 that is greater than 70,000? What about 700,000?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=999)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the smallest multiple of $77$ greater than a given number $N$, compute
>
> $$
> \left\lfloor \frac{N}{77} \right\rfloor + 1
> $$
>
> and then multiply by $77$.
>
> ## 1. Smallest multiple of $77$ greater than $70{,}000$
>
> Compute:
>
> $$
> \frac{70{,}000}{77} \approx 909.09
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \left\lfloor \frac{70{,}000}{77} \right\rfloor = 909
> $$
>
> The next integer is $910$, so the desired multiple is
>
> $$
> 910 \cdot 77 = 70{,}070
> $$
>
> ## 2. Smallest multiple of $77$ greater than $700{,}000$
>
> Compute:
>
> $$
> \frac{700{,}000}{77} \approx 9090.90
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \left\lfloor \frac{700{,}000}{77} \right\rfloor = 9090
> $$
>
> The next integer is $9091$, so the desired multiple is
>
> $$
> 9091 \cdot 77 = 700{,}007
> $$
>
> Therefore, the smallest multiple of $77$ greater than $70{,}000$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{70{,}070}
> $$
>
> and the smallest multiple of $77$ greater than $700{,}000$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{700{,}007}
> $$
