> [!example] Question 1006 · Optiver
> ## Trader Interview

What is the value of (3/21) divided by (1/7)?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1007)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute by multiplying by the reciprocal:
>
> $$
> \frac{3/21}{1/7}
> =
> \frac{3}{21}\cdot\frac{7}{1}.
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> \frac{3}{21}=\frac{1}{7},
> $$
>
> we get
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{7}\cdot 7 = 1.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the value is
>
> $$
> \boxed{1}.
> $$
