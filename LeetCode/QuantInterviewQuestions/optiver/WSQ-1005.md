> [!example] Question 1005 · Optiver
> ## Trader Interview

Evaluate: (5/16) divided by (12/3).

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1006)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute the denominator first:
>
> $$
> \frac{12}{3} = 4
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> \frac{5/16}{12/3} = \frac{5/16}{4} = \frac{5}{16} \cdot \frac{1}{4} = \frac{5}{64}
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \frac{5/16}{12/3} = \frac{5}{64}
> $$
