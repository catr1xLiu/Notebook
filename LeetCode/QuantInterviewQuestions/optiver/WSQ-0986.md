> [!example] Question 986 · Optiver
> ## Trader Interview

What is the square root of 30,000 (calculated mentally)?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=987)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Benader eerst:
>
> $$173^2 = 29929,\qquad 174^2 = 30276$$
>
> Dus:
>
> $$\sqrt{30000}$$
>
> ligt tussen $173$ en $174$, en dichter bij $173$.
>
> Een goede schatting is:
>
> $$\sqrt{30000} \approx 173{,}2$$
>
> Controle:
>
> $$173{,}2^2 \approx 29998{,}24$$
>
> Dus:
>
> $$\sqrt{30000} \approx 173{,}2$$
>
> Antwoord: **ongeveer $173{,}2$**.
