> [!example] Question 1003 · Optiver
> ## Trader Interview

Assign A=1, B=2, ..., Z=26. Find an eight-letter English word such that the sum of the letter values is less than 50.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1004)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Using the assignment $A=1, B=2, \ldots, Z=26$, we seek an $8$-letter word whose letter values sum to less than $50$.
>
> A simple example is
>
> $$
> \text{ABANDONS}
> $$
>
> Now compute its value:
>
> - $A=1$
> - $B=2$
> - $A=1$
> - $N=14$
> - $D=4$
> - $O=15$
> - $N=14$
> - $S=19$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 1+2+1+14+4+15+14+19 = 70
> $$
>
> This is too large, so try a lower-valued word.
>
> Consider instead
>
> $$
> \text{ACADEMIA}
> $$
>
> Its letters sum to
>
> - $A=1$
> - $C=3$
> - $A=1$
> - $D=4$
> - $E=5$
> - $M=13$
> - $I=9$
> - $A=1$
>
> Hence
>
> $$
> 1+3+1+4+5+13+9+1 = 37
> $$
>
> Since $37<50$, an acceptable $8$-letter word is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{ACADEMIA}}
> $$
