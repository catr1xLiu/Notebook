> [!example] Question 910 · Optiver
> ## Quant Trader Intern Interview

Complete the sequence: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=911)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The sequence lists consecutive prime numbers:
>
> $$2,\ 3,\ 5,\ 7,\ 11,\ 13$$
>
> The next prime after $13$ is $17$.
>
> Thus, the completed sequence continues with
>
> $$17$$
