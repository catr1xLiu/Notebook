> [!example] Question 852 · Citadel Securities
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

What is the house edge of a standard game of roulette?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=853)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The **house edge** of roulette is the casino’s average advantage per bet, expressed as a percentage of the amount wagered.
>
> ## American roulette
> American roulette has $38$ pockets: $1$ through $36$, plus $0$ and $00$.
>
> For a standard even-money bet (such as red/black), the player wins with probability
>
> $$
> \frac{18}{38}
> $$
>
> and loses with probability
>
> $$
> \frac{20}{38}.
> $$
>
> If the bet is $1, then the expected value to the player is
>
> $$
> E = 1 \cdot \frac{18}{38} + (-1)\cdot \frac{20}{38}
> = \frac{18-20}{38}
> = -\frac{2}{38}
> = -\frac{1}{19}
> \approx -0.05263.
> $$
>
> So the house edge is
>
> $$
> 5.26\%.
> $$
>
> ## European roulette
> European roulette has $37$ pockets: $1$ through $36$, plus a single $0$.
>
> For the same $1 even-money bet, the player wins with probability
>
> $$
> \frac{18}{37}
> $$
>
> and loses with probability
>
> $$
> \frac{19}{37}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> E = 1 \cdot \frac{18}{37} + (-1)\cdot \frac{19}{37}
> = -\frac{1}{37}
> \approx -0.02703.
> $$
>
> So the house edge is
>
> $$
> 2.70\%.
> $$
>
> ## Final answer
> - **American roulette:** $5.26\%$
> - **European roulette:** $2.70\%$
