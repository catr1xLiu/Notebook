> [!example] Question 856 · Citadel Securities
> ## Quant Trader Intern Interview

What is the expected number of rolls needed to see each side of a fair six-sided die at least once?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=857)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $T$ be the number of rolls needed to see all $6$ faces of a fair die at least once.
>
> This is a standard **coupon collector** problem. Decompose $T$ into stages:
>
> - $T_1$: rolls needed to get the first new face,
> - $T_2$: additional rolls needed to get a second new face,
> - $\dots$,
> - $T_6$: additional rolls needed to get the sixth new face.
>
> Then
> $$
> T = T_1 + T_2 + \cdots + T_6.
> $$
>
> ## Step 1: Expected time for each stage
>
> If $k-1$ distinct faces have already been seen, then there are $6-(k-1)=7-k$ unseen faces remaining. On each roll, the probability of getting a new face is
> $$
> \frac{7-k}{6}.
> $$
> Hence $T_k$ is geometric with success probability
> $$
> p_k = \frac{7-k}{6},
> $$
> so
> $$
> \mathbb{E}[T_k] = \frac{1}{p_k} = \frac{6}{7-k}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
> $$
> \mathbb{E}[T]
> = \sum_{k=1}^6 \mathbb{E}[T_k]
> = \sum_{k=1}^6 \frac{6}{7-k}.
> $$
>
> Reindexing,
> $$
> \mathbb{E}[T]
> = 6\left(1 + \frac12 + \frac13 + \frac14 + \frac15 + \frac16\right).
> $$
>
> ## Step 2: Compute the sum
>
> The harmonic sum is
> $$
> 1 + \frac12 + \frac13 + \frac14 + \frac15 + \frac16
> = \frac{147}{60}
> = \frac{49}{20}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
> $$
> \mathbb{E}[T] = 6 \cdot \frac{49}{20} = \frac{294}{20} = \frac{147}{10}.
> $$
>
> ## Final answer
>
> $$
> \boxed{\mathbb{E}[T] = \frac{147}{10} = 14.7}
> $$
>
> So the expected number of rolls needed to see every side of a fair die at least once is
> $$
> \boxed{14.7}.
> $$
