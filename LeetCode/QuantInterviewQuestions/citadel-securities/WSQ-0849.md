> [!example] Question 849 · Citadel Securities
> ## Trader Interview

Given constraints on the correlations between three random variables, provide bounds for the correlation between one pair of them.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=850)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let
> $$
> \rho_{12} = \operatorname{Corr}(X_1,X_2), \qquad
> \rho_{13} = \operatorname{Corr}(X_1,X_3), \qquad
> \rho_{23} = \operatorname{Corr}(X_2,X_3).
> $$
>
> Suppose $\rho_{13}$ and $\rho_{23}$ are known, and we want bounds on $\rho_{12}$.
>
> ## Correlation matrix condition
>
> The correlation matrix of $(X_1,X_2,X_3)$ is
> $$
> R=
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 1 & \rho_{12} & \rho_{13}\\
> \rho_{12} & 1 & \rho_{23}\\
> \rho_{13} & \rho_{23} & 1
> \end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> Since $R$ must be positive semidefinite, its determinant must satisfy
> $$
> \det(R)\ge 0.
> $$
>
> Compute:
> $$
> \det(R)
> =
> 1 + 2\rho_{12}\rho_{13}\rho_{23} - \rho_{12}^2 - \rho_{13}^2 - \rho_{23}^2.
> $$
>
> Thus
> $$
> 1 + 2\rho_{12}\rho_{13}\rho_{23} - \rho_{12}^2 - \rho_{13}^2 - \rho_{23}^2 \ge 0.
> $$
>
> Rearrange this as a quadratic inequality in $\rho_{12}$:
> $$
> \rho_{12}^2 - 2\rho_{13}\rho_{23}\rho_{12} + \bigl(\rho_{13}^2+\rho_{23}^2-1\bigr)\le 0.
> $$
>
> ## Solve the quadratic inequality
>
> The roots are
> $$
> \rho_{12}
> =
> \rho_{13}\rho_{23}
> \pm
> \sqrt{(1-\rho_{13}^2)(1-\rho_{23}^2)}.
> $$
>
> Therefore $\rho_{12}$ must lie between these roots:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \rho_{13}\rho_{23}
> -
> \sqrt{(1-\rho_{13}^2)(1-\rho_{23}^2)}
> \;\le\;
> \rho_{12}
> \;\le\;
> \rho_{13}\rho_{23}
> +
> \sqrt{(1-\rho_{13}^2)(1-\rho_{23}^2)}
> }.
> $$
>
> ## Final answer
>
> Given $\rho_{13}$ and $\rho_{23}$, the sharp possible bounds on $\rho_{12}$ are
> $$
> \boxed{
> \rho_{12}\in
> \left[
> \rho_{13}\rho_{23}
> -
> \sqrt{(1-\rho_{13}^2)(1-\rho_{23}^2)},
> \;
> \rho_{13}\rho_{23}
> +
> \sqrt{(1-\rho_{13}^2)(1-\rho_{23}^2)}
> \right].
> }
> $$
>
> Equivalently, for three random variables with specified correlations to a third one, the correlation between the remaining pair is constrained exactly by this interval.
