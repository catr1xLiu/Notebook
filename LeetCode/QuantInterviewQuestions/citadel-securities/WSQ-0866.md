> [!example] Question 866 · Citadel Securities
> ## Quantitative Trading Intern Interview

James goes to the gym every Monday and Tuesday. On every other day of the week, he independently has a 30% chance of going to the gym. On a random day, James is spotted at the gym. What is the probability that it is Monday?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=867)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $G$ be the event that James is at the gym.
>
> He always goes to the gym on Monday and Tuesday, so
>
> $$
> P(G\mid \text{Mon})=1,\qquad P(G\mid \text{Tue})=1.
> $$
>
> On each of the other $5$ days,
>
> $$
> P(G\mid \text{day})=0.3.
> $$
>
> Since each day is equally likely,
>
> $$
> P(G)=\frac17\left(1+1+5(0.3)\right)=\frac17(3.5)=\frac12.
> $$
>
> By Bayes' rule,
>
> $$
> P(\text{Mon}\mid G)=\frac{P(G\mid \text{Mon})P(\text{Mon})}{P(G)}
> =\frac{1\cdot \frac17}{\frac12}
> =\frac27.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac27}
> $$
