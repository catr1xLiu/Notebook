> [!example] Question 861 · Citadel Securities
> ## Quantitative Trading Intern Interview

James goes to the gym every Monday and Tuesday. On every other day of the week, he independently has a 30% chance of going to the gym. On a random day, James is spotted at the gym. What is the probability that it is Monday?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=862)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $M$ be the event that the day is Monday, and let $G$ be the event that James is at the gym.
>
> He always goes to the gym on Monday and Tuesday, and on each of the other $5$ days he independently goes with probability $0.3$.
>
> Since each day of the week is equally likely to be the day on which he is observed,
>
> $$
> P(G)=\frac{1}{7}(1+1+5\cdot 0.3)=\frac{1}{7}(3.5)=\frac12.
> $$
>
> Also,
>
> $$
> P(M\cap G)=P(M)=\frac17,
> $$
>
> since he always goes to the gym on Monday.
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> P(M\mid G)=\frac{P(M\cap G)}{P(G)}=\frac{1/7}{1/2}=\frac{2}{7}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{2}{7}}
> $$
