> ## Quantitative Trader Intern Interview

What is the expected value of a fair 6-sided die?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=878)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> For a fair $6$-sided die, the possible outcomes are $1,2,3,4,5,6$, each with probability $\frac{1}{6}$.
>
> The expected value is
>
> $$
> E[X] = \sum_{x=1}^{6} x \cdot P(X=x)
> = \sum_{x=1}^{6} x \cdot \frac{1}{6}
> = \frac{1}{6}(1+2+3+4+5+6).
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> 1+2+3+4+5+6 = 21,
> $$
>
> we get
>
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{21}{6} = \frac{7}{2} = 3.5.
> $$
>
> Thus, the expected value of a fair $6$-sided die is
>
> $$
> \boxed{3.5}.
> $$
