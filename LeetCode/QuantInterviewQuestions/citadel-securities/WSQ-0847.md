> [!example] Question 847 · Citadel Securities
> ## Trader Interview

What is the square root of 800 rounded to two decimal places?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=848)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To approximate $\sqrt{800}$ to two decimal places, factor $800$ as
>
> $$
> 800 = 16 \cdot 50.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> \sqrt{800} = \sqrt{16}\sqrt{50} = 4\sqrt{50}.
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> \sqrt{50} \approx 7.0710678,
> $$
>
> we get
>
> $$
> \sqrt{800} \approx 4 \times 7.0710678 = 28.2842712.
> $$
>
> Rounding to two decimal places gives
>
> $$
> \sqrt{800} \approx 28.28.
> $$
