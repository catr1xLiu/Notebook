> [!example] Question 344 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 9:30?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=345)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> At $9{:}30$:
>
> - The minute hand points at $30$ minutes, which is at $180^\circ$ from $12$.
> - The hour hand is halfway between $9$ and $10$.
>
> Since each hour mark is $30^\circ$ apart, the hour hand at $9$ is at
>
> $$
> 9 \times 30^\circ = 270^\circ
> $$
>
> and in $30$ minutes it moves halfway to $10$, adding
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{2}\times 30^\circ = 15^\circ.
> $$
>
> So the hour hand is at
>
> $$
> 270^\circ + 15^\circ = 285^\circ.
> $$
>
> The angle between the hands is
>
> $$
> |285^\circ - 180^\circ| = 105^\circ.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the angle between the hands is
>
> $$
> \boxed{105^\circ}.
> $$
