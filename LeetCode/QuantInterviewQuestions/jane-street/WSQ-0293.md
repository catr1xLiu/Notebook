> [!example] Question 293 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trading Interview

What is the minimum number of people required to guarantee that there exist seven people who are born in the same month?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=294)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Apply the pigeonhole principle.
>
> There are $12$ months, which are the $12$ “boxes,” and people are the “objects” placed into these boxes according to their birth month.
>
> To avoid having $7$ people in any one month, each month can contain at most $6$ people. Thus the greatest number of people possible without forcing $7$ in the same month is
>
> $$
> 12 \cdot 6 = 72.
> $$
>
> Therefore, one more person guarantees that some month has at least $7$ people:
>
> $$
> 72 + 1 = 73.
> $$
>
> So the minimum number of people required is
>
> $$
> \boxed{73}.
> $$
