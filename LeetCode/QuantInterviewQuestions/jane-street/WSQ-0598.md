> [!example] Question 598 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

Convert 16/32 to decimal.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=599)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To convert $\frac{16}{32}$ to a decimal, simplify first:
>
> $$
> \frac{16}{32} = \frac{1}{2}
> $$
>
> Then write $\frac{1}{2}$ as a decimal:
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{2} = 0.5
> $$
>
> Thus, the decimal form is:
>
> $$
> 0.5
> $$
