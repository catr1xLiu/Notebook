> [!example] Question 174 · Jane Street
> ## Intern Interview

It is currently 10:02. What is the angle between the hour and minute hands on a clock?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=175)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> At $10{:}02$, the minute hand is at
>
> $$
> 2 \times 6^\circ = 12^\circ
> $$
>
> from the $12$ o’clock position, since each minute corresponds to $6^\circ$.
>
> The hour hand is at
>
> $$
> 10 \times 30^\circ + 2 \times 0.5^\circ = 300^\circ + 1^\circ = 301^\circ
> $$
>
> from the $12$ o’clock position, since each hour corresponds to $30^\circ$ and each minute moves the hour hand by $0.5^\circ$.
>
> The difference between the two positions is
>
> $$
> |301^\circ - 12^\circ| = 289^\circ.
> $$
>
> The smaller angle between the hands is
>
> $$
> 360^\circ - 289^\circ = 71^\circ.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the angle between the two hands is
>
> $$
> \boxed{71^\circ}.
> $$
