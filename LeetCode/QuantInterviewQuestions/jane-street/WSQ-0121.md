> [!example] Question 121 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Intern Interview

If there are 8 people and they all shake hands with each other once, how many handshakes are there? If there are four couples among the 8 people who do not shake hands with each other, how many handshakes are there now?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=122)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Each handshake is determined by choosing a pair of people.
>
> For $8$ people, the number of pairs is
>
> $$
> \binom{8}{2}=\frac{8\cdot 7}{2}=28.
> $$
>
> So there are
>
> $$
> 28
> $$
>
> handshakes.
>
> Now suppose the $8$ people consist of $4$ couples, and within each couple the two people do not shake hands. There are still $28$ possible pairs total, but we must exclude the $4$ couple-pairs.
>
> Thus the number of handshakes is
>
> $$
> 28-4=24.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the answers are:
>
> $$
> 28 \text{ handshakes}
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> 24 \text{ handshakes}.
> $$
