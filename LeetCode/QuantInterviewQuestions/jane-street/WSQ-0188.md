> [!example] Question 188 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trading Intern Interview

What is the probability of getting exactly 3 heads when tossing 5 fair coins?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=189)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the probability of getting exactly $3$ heads in $5$ tosses of a fair coin, use the binomial model.
>
> The total number of possible outcomes when tossing $5$ coins is
>
> $$
> 2^5 = 32.
> $$
>
> The number of ways to choose which $3$ of the $5$ tosses are heads is
>
> $$
> \binom{5}{3} = 10.
> $$
>
> Thus, the probability is
>
> $$
> P(\text{exactly } 3 \text{ heads}) = \frac{\binom{5}{3}}{2^5} = \frac{10}{32} = \frac{5}{16}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{16}}.
> $$
