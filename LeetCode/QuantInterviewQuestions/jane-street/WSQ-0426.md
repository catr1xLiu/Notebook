> [!example] Question 426 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is 45 multiplied by 69?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=427)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To compute $45 \cdot 69$, use the distributive property:
>
> $$
> 45 \cdot 69 = 45 \cdot (70 - 1)
> $$
>
> $$
> = 45 \cdot 70 - 45 \cdot 1
> $$
>
> $$
> = 3150 - 45
> $$
>
> $$
> = 3105
> $$
>
> Therefore, the product is
>
> $$
> \boxed{3105}
> $$
