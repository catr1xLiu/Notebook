> [!example] Question 513 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

What is the probability of obtaining exactly 2 tails in 5 coin flips?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=514)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> For $5$ independent flips of a fair coin, the number of tails follows a binomial distribution with parameters $n=5$ and $p=\tfrac12$.
>
> The probability of getting exactly $2$ tails is
>
> $$
> P(X=2)=\binom{5}{2}\left(\frac12\right)^2\left(\frac12\right)^3
> $$
>
> since we must choose which $2$ of the $5$ flips are tails.
>
> Now compute:
>
> $$
> \binom{5}{2}=10
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \left(\frac12\right)^2\left(\frac12\right)^3=\left(\frac12\right)^5=\frac{1}{32}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> P(X=2)=10\cdot \frac{1}{32}=\frac{10}{32}=\frac{5}{16}.
> $$
>
> Thus, the probability of obtaining exactly $2$ tails in $5$ coin flips is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{16}}.
> $$
