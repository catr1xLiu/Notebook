> [!example] Question 622 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

If you roll a fair coin 12 times, what is the expected product of the number of heads and the number of tails?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=623)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $H$ be the number of heads in $12$ fair coin tosses. Then the number of tails is
>
> $$
> T = 12 - H.
> $$
>
> We want
>
> $$
> E[HT] = E\bigl[H(12-H)\bigr] = 12E[H] - E[H^2].
> $$
>
> Since $H \sim \mathrm{Binomial}(12,\tfrac12)$,
>
> $$
> E[H] = 12 \cdot \frac12 = 6,
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \mathrm{Var}(H) = 12 \cdot \frac12 \cdot \frac12 = 3.
> $$
>
> Using
>
> $$
> E[H^2] = \mathrm{Var}(H) + (E[H])^2,
> $$
>
> we get
>
> $$
> E[H^2] = 3 + 6^2 = 39.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> E[HT] = 12 \cdot 6 - 39 = 72 - 39 = 33.
> $$
>
> \[
> \boxed{33}
> \]
