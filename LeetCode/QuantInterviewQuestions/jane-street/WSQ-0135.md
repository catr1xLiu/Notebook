> [!example] Question 135 · Jane Street
> ## Intern Interview

If I roll two dice, what is the expected value of the product of the two faces?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=136)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ and $Y$ be the results of the two fair six-sided dice. The quantity of interest is the product
>
> $$
> XY.
> $$
>
> Since the dice are independent,
>
> $$
> E[XY] = E[X]E[Y].
> $$
>
> Each die is uniformly distributed on $\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$, so
>
> $$
> E[X] = E[Y] = \frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6} = \frac{21}{6} = \frac{7}{2}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> E[XY] = \frac{7}{2}\cdot \frac{7}{2} = \frac{49}{4}.
> $$
>
> So the expected value of the product is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{49}{4}}.
> $$
