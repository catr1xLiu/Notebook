> [!example] Question 120 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader - Intern Interview

What is the expected value of the sum when two fair six-sided dice are rolled?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=121)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ and $Y$ be the outcomes of two fair six-sided dice, and let
>
> $$
> S = X + Y
> $$
>
> be their sum.
>
> Since each die is fair,
>
> $$
> E[X] = E[Y] = \frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6} = \frac{21}{6} = 3.5
> $$
>
> By linearity of expectation,
>
> $$
> E[S] = E[X+Y] = E[X] + E[Y]
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> E[S] = 3.5 + 3.5 = 7
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected value of the sum of two dice is
>
> $$
> \boxed{7}
> $$
