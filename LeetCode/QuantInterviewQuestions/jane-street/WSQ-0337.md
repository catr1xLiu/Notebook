> [!example] Question 337 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is the sum of all odd numbers between 1 and 80?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=338)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The odd integers between $1$ and $80$ are
>
> $$
> 1, 3, 5, \dots, 79.
> $$
>
> This is an arithmetic sequence with:
>
> - first term: $a_1 = 1$
> - last term: $a_n = 79$
> - common difference: $d = 2$
>
> First find the number of terms:
>
> $$
> n = \frac{79 - 1}{2} + 1 = \frac{78}{2} + 1 = 39 + 1 = 40.
> $$
>
> Now use the sum formula for an arithmetic sequence:
>
> $$
> S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n).
> $$
>
> So,
>
> $$
> S_{40} = \frac{40}{2}(1 + 79) = 20 \cdot 80 = 1600.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the sum of all the odd numbers between $1$ and $80$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{1600}.
> $$
