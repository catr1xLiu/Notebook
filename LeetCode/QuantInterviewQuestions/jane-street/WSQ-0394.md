> [!example] Question 394 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is 15% of 115?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=395)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find $15\%$ of $115$, convert the percent to a decimal:
>
> $$
> 15\% = 0.15
> $$
>
> Then multiply:
>
> $$
> 0.15 \times 115 = 17.25
> $$
>
> Thus, $15\%$ of $115$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{17.25}
> $$
