> [!example] Question 626 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

X is uniformly distributed on \[0, 5\]. What is E(\|5 - X\|) if X is continuous vs. discrete? Which value is larger, and why?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=627)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X \sim \mathrm{Unif}[0,5]$. We want to compare $E(|5-X|)$ in the continuous and discrete uniform cases.
>
> ## 1. Continuous uniform on $[0,5]$
>
> Since $X$ takes values in $[0,5]$, we have $|5-X| = 5-X$. Thus
>
> $$
> E(|5-X|) = E(5-X) = 5 - E(X).
> $$
>
> For a continuous uniform random variable on $[0,5]$,
>
> $$
> E(X) = \frac{0+5}{2} = 2.5.
> $$
>
> Hence
>
> $$
> E(|5-X|) = 5 - 2.5 = 2.5.
> $$
>
> So in the continuous case,
>
> $$
> E(|5-X|) = 2.5.
> $$
>
> ## 2. Discrete uniform on $\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}$
>
> If instead $X$ is discrete uniform on the six values $\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}$, then each value has probability $\frac{1}{6}$. Again, since $X \le 5$,
>
> $$
> |5-X| = 5-X.
> $$
>
> Therefore
>
> $$
> E(|5-X|) = E(5-X) = 5 - E(X).
> $$
>
> For the discrete uniform distribution on $\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}$,
>
> $$
> E(X) = \frac{0+1+2+3+4+5}{6} = \frac{15}{6} = 2.5.
> $$
>
> Thus
>
> $$
> E(|5-X|) = 5 - 2.5 = 2.5.
> $$
>
> Equivalently, by direct averaging:
>
> $$
> E(|5-X|) = \frac{1}{6}\bigl(|5-0|+|5-1|+|5-2|+|5-3|+|5-4|+|5-5|\bigr)
> $$
>
> $$
> = \frac{1}{6}(5+4+3+2+1+0) = \frac{15}{6} = 2.5.
> $$
>
> ## 3. Comparison
>
> Both are the same:
>
> $$
> E(|5-X|) = 2.5
> $$
>
> in both the continuous and discrete cases.
>
> ## 4. Why are they the same?
>
> The key reason is that on both supports, $X \le 5$, so
>
> $$
> |5-X| = 5-X.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> E(|5-X|) = 5 - E(X).
> $$
>
> In both the continuous uniform distribution on $[0,5]$ and the discrete uniform distribution on $\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}$, the mean is
>
> $$
> E(X) = 2.5.
> $$
>
> So both expectations must be equal.
>
> ## Final answer
>
> $$
> E(|5-X|) = 2.5
> $$
>
> for both the continuous and discrete versions. Neither is larger; they are equal because in both cases $|5-X|=5-X$ and both distributions have mean $2.5$.
