> [!example] Question 389 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

If two teams are equally talented, what is the probability that a 7-game series between them reaches the 7th game?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=390)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> If the two teams are equally talented, then each game is independent and each team has probability $1/2$ of winning any given game.
>
> A best-of-$7$ series reaches the $7$th game exactly when, after $6$ games, each team has won $3$ games. Thus we need the probability of getting exactly $3$ wins for one team in $6$ games.
>
> The number of such sequences is
> $$
> \binom{6}{3} = 20.
> $$
> Each particular sequence of $6$ games has probability
> $$
> \left(\frac12\right)^6 = \frac{1}{64}.
> $$
> Therefore,
> $$
> P(\text{series reaches game }7)
> =
> \binom{6}{3}\left(\frac12\right)^6
> =
> 20 \cdot \frac{1}{64}
> =
> \frac{5}{16}.
> $$
>
> Thus, the chance that the series reaches the $7$th game is
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{16}}
> $$
> which is $31.25\%$.
