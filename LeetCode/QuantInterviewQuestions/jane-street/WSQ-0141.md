> [!example] Question 141 · Jane Street
> ## Intern Interview

What is the sum of the first 30 even numbers?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=142)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The first $30$ even numbers are
>
> $$
> 2,4,6,\dots,60.
> $$
>
> This is an arithmetic sequence with first term $a_1 = 2$, last term $a_{30} = 60$, and number of terms $n = 30$.
>
> Using the sum formula for an arithmetic sequence,
>
> $$
> S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n),
> $$
>
> we get
>
> $$
> S_{30} = \frac{30}{2}(2 + 60) = 15 \cdot 62 = 930.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the sum of the first $30$ even numbers is
>
> $$
> \boxed{930}.
> $$
