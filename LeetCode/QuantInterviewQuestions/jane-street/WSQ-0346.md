> [!example] Question 346 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is the minimum number of people required to ensure that at least 7 of them share a birthday in the same month?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=347)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Apply the Pigeonhole Principle.
>
> There are $12$ months. To avoid having $7$ people in the same month, each month can have at most $6$ people.
>
> Thus the largest number of people possible without forcing $7$ in one month is
>
> $$
> 12 \cdot 6 = 72.
> $$
>
> Therefore, one more person guarantees that some month has at least $7$ birthdays:
>
> $$
> 72 + 1 = 73.
> $$
>
> So the minimum number of people required is
>
> $$
> \boxed{73}.
> $$
