> [!example] Question 657 · Jane Street
> ## Quant Trader Intern Interview

What is the probability that the sequence HHT appears before HTT in a sequence of fair coin tosses?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=658)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Consider consecutive flips of a fair coin, and let
>
> - $A =$ the event that the pattern $\text{HHT}$ appears before $\text{HTT}$,
> - $p =$ the desired probability.
>
> The key is to track the relevant recent history of flips. Define states by the longest suffix of the current sequence that is also a prefix of either target pattern.
>
> The relevant states are:
>
> - $S_0$: no useful suffix,
> - $S_H$: the current suffix is $\text{H}$,
> - $S_{HH}$: the current suffix is $\text{HH}$,
> - $S_{HT}$: the current suffix is $\text{HT}$.
>
> Let the probability of eventually getting $\text{HHT}$ before $\text{HTT}$ from these states be
>
> $$
> p_0,\; p_H,\; p_{HH},\; p_{HT},
> $$
>
> respectively. We want $p_0$.
>
> ## Step 1: Set up equations
>
> From $S_0$:
>
> - Flip $\text{H}$ with probability $\tfrac12$, going to $S_H$.
> - Flip $\text{T}$ with probability $\tfrac12$, staying at $S_0$.
>
> So
>
> $$
> p_0 = \frac12 p_H + \frac12 p_0.
> $$
>
> From $S_H$:
>
> - Flip $\text{H}$ with probability $\tfrac12$, going to $S_{HH}$.
> - Flip $\text{T}$ with probability $\tfrac12$, going to $S_{HT}$.
>
> Thus
>
> $$
> p_H = \frac12 p_{HH} + \frac12 p_{HT}.
> $$
>
> From $S_{HH}$:
>
> - Flip $\text{T}$ with probability $\tfrac12$, completing $\text{HHT}$, so success.
> - Flip $\text{H}$ with probability $\tfrac12$; the new useful suffix is still $\text{HH}$.
>
> Hence
>
> $$
> p_{HH} = \frac12 \cdot 1 + \frac12 p_{HH}.
> $$
>
> From $S_{HT}$:
>
> - Flip $\text{T}$ with probability $\tfrac12$, completing $\text{HTT}$, so failure.
> - Flip $\text{H}$ with probability $\tfrac12$; the new suffix is $\text{H}$.
>
> Therefore
>
> $$
> p_{HT} = \frac12 \cdot 0 + \frac12 p_H = \frac12 p_H.
> $$
>
> ## Step 2: Solve the system
>
> From
>
> $$
> p_{HH} = \frac12 + \frac12 p_{HH},
> $$
>
> we get
>
> $$
> p_{HH} = 1.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> p_H = \frac12(1) + \frac12 p_{HT}.
> $$
>
> Using $p_{HT} = \tfrac12 p_H$,
>
> $$
> p_H = \frac12 + \frac12 \cdot \frac12 p_H
> = \frac12 + \frac14 p_H.
> $$
>
> So
>
> $$
> \frac34 p_H = \frac12,
> \qquad
> p_H = \frac{2}{3}.
> $$
>
> Finally, from
>
> $$
> p_0 = \frac12 p_H + \frac12 p_0,
> $$
>
> we obtain
>
> $$
> \frac12 p_0 = \frac12 p_H
> \quad\Rightarrow\quad
> p_0 = p_H = \frac{2}{3}.
> $$
>
> ## Conclusion
>
> The probability that $\text{HHT}$ occurs before $\text{HTT}$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{2}{3}}.
> $$
