> [!example] Question 388 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is 37 multiplied by 43?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=389)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute the product by distribution:
> $$
> 37 \cdot 43 = 37(40+3) = 37\cdot 40 + 37\cdot 3 = 1480 + 111 = 1591.
> $$
>
> Thus,
> $$
> 37 \cdot 43 = 1591.
> $$
