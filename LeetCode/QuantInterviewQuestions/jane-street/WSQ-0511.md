> [!example] Question 511 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

If January 1st, 2015 was a Monday, which day of the week would January 1st, 2025 fall on?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=512)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To determine the day on $1^\text{st}$ Jan $2025$, count the total shift in weekdays from $1^\text{st}$ Jan $2015$ to $1^\text{st}$ Jan $2025$.
>
> Each ordinary year shifts the day by $1$, and each leap year shifts it by $2$.
>
> From $2015$ to $2024$, the years are:
>
> $$
> 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024
> $$
>
> Among these, the leap years are:
>
> $$
> 2016,\ 2020,\ 2024
> $$
>
> So there are:
>
> - $7$ ordinary years
> - $3$ leap years
>
> Hence the total shift is:
>
> $$
> 7 \cdot 1 + 3 \cdot 2 = 7 + 6 = 13
> $$
>
> Now reduce modulo $7$:
>
> $$
> 13 \equiv 6 \pmod{7}
> $$
>
> So the weekday moves forward by $6$ days from Monday:
>
> $$
> \text{Monday} \to \text{Sunday}
> $$
>
> Therefore, $1^\text{st}$ Jan $2025$ would be on **Sunday**.
