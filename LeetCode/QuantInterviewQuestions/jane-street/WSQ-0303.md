> [!example] Question 303 · Jane Street
> ## Quant Intern Interview

What is the sum of the even numbers from 1 to 60?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=304)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The even numbers from $1$ to $60$ form the arithmetic sequence
>
> $$
> 2, 4, 6, \dots, 60.
> $$
>
> This sequence has first term $a_1 = 2$, last term $a_n = 60$, and common difference $d = 2$.
>
> First, find the number of terms:
>
> $$
> n = \frac{60 - 2}{2} + 1 = 30.
> $$
>
> Use the formula for the sum of an arithmetic sequence:
>
> $$
> S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n).
> $$
>
> Substitute the values:
>
> $$
> S_{30} = \frac{30}{2}(2 + 60) = 15 \cdot 62 = 930.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the sum of the even numbers from $1$ to $60$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{930}.
> $$
