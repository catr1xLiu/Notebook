> [!example] Question 30 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

What is the minimum number of people required in a group so that at least two of them share a birthday in the same month?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=31)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To guarantee that at least two people in a group have birthdays in the same month, apply the **Pigeonhole Principle**.
>
> There are $12$ months in a year, so there are $12$ possible birth-month categories.
>
> If there are only $12$ people, it is possible that each person was born in a different month. Thus, no repetition is forced.
>
> With one more person, making $13$ people, at least two people must share the same birth month.
>
> Therefore, the required number is
>
> $$13$$
