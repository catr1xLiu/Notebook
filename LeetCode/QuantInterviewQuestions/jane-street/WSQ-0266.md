> [!example] Question 266 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Intern Interview

What is the smallest number made up of only 1s and 0s that is divisible by 225?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=267)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To be divisible by $225$, a number must be divisible by both $9$ and $25$.
>
> We seek the smallest positive integer consisting only of the digits $0$ and $1$.
>
> ## Step 1: Divisibility by $25$
> A number is divisible by $25$ if its last two digits are one of
> $$00,\ 25,\ 50,\ 75.$$
> Since the number may contain only $0$ and $1$, the only possible ending is
> $$00.$$
>
> So the number must end in $00$.
>
> ## Step 2: Divisibility by $9$
> A number is divisible by $9$ if the sum of its digits is divisible by $9$.
>
> Because the digits are only $0$ and $1$, the sum of the digits equals the number of $1$'s. Thus the number of $1$'s must be a multiple of $9$.
>
> To make the number as small as possible, we should use the fewest possible digits and place the $1$'s as far to the right as allowed, while still ending in $00$.
>
> The smallest possible positive multiple of $9$ for the number of $1$'s is $9$. Hence we need exactly $9$ ones.
>
> With $9$ ones and the required ending $00$, the smallest such number is
> $$11111111100.$$
>
> ## Step 3: Check
> - Last two digits are $00$, so it is divisible by $25$.
> - Sum of digits is $9$, so it is divisible by $9$.
>
> Therefore it is divisible by
> $$225 = 9 \cdot 25.$$
>
> ## Final Answer
> $$\boxed{11111111100}$$
