> [!example] Question 501 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

What is the minimum number of people needed in a room so that at least five of them share the same birth month?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=502)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Use the Pigeonhole Principle with the $12$ months as the “holes.”
>
> To avoid having $5$ people in the same birth month, the most you can place is $4$ people in each month. That gives
>
> $$
> 12 \times 4 = 48
> $$
>
> people with no month containing $5$ birthdays.
>
> Therefore, one more person forces some month to contain at least $5$ people:
>
> $$
> 48 + 1 = 49
> $$
>
> So the minimum number of people required is
>
> $$
> \boxed{49}
> $$
