> [!example] Question 189 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trading Intern Interview

What is the expected value of the number of heads when you flip two fair coins?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=190)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the number of heads obtained when two fair coins are flipped.
>
> Each coin has probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$ of landing heads, so $X$ can take values $0$, $1$, or $2$ with probabilities
>
> $$
> P(X=0)=\tfrac{1}{4}, \qquad P(X=1)=\tfrac{1}{2}, \qquad P(X=2)=\tfrac{1}{4}.
> $$
>
> The expected value is
>
> $$
> E[X] = 0\cdot \tfrac{1}{4} + 1\cdot \tfrac{1}{2} + 2\cdot \tfrac{1}{4}.
> $$
>
> Compute:
>
> $$
> E[X] = 0 + \tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{1}{2} = 1.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected number of heads is
>
> $$
> \boxed{1}.
> $$
