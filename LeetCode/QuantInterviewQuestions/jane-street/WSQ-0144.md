> [!example] Question 144 · Jane Street
> ## Intern Interview

Sum all the odd numbers between 1 and 100.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=145)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The odd numbers between $1$ and $100$ are
>
> $$
> 1, 3, 5, \dots, 99.
> $$
>
> This is an arithmetic sequence with first term $a_1 = 1$, last term $a_n = 99$, and common difference $d = 2$.
>
> First find the number of terms:
>
> $$
> n = \frac{99 - 1}{2} + 1 = 50.
> $$
>
> Now use the sum formula for an arithmetic sequence:
>
> $$
> S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n).
> $$
>
> So,
>
> $$
> S_{50} = \frac{50}{2}(1 + 99) = 25 \cdot 100 = 2500.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the sum of all odd numbers between $1$ and $100$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{2500}.
> $$
>
> A quick mental-math observation is that the sum of the first $n$ odd numbers is $n^2$. Since there are $50$ odd numbers from $1$ to $99$,
>
> $$
> 1 + 3 + 5 + \cdots + 99 = 50^2 = 2500.
> $$
