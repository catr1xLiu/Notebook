> [!example] Question 65 · Jane Street
> ## Trading Interview

What is the probability that a best-of-7 series reaches the 7th game?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=66)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let each game be independent, and suppose either team is equally likely to win any game, so each game is a fair $50$-$50$ contest.
>
> A best-of-$7$ series reaches Game $7$ exactly when, after $6$ games, each team has won $3$ games. Thus we need the probability of a $3$-$3$ split in the first $6$ games.
>
> The total number of possible outcomes of the first $6$ games is
>
> $$
> 2^6 = 64.
> $$
>
> The number of ways for one designated team to win exactly $3$ of those $6$ games is
>
> $$
> \binom{6}{3} = 20.
> $$
>
> Since each sequence of $6$ results is equally likely, the probability is
>
> $$
> \frac{\binom{6}{3}}{2^6} = \frac{20}{64} = \frac{5}{16}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the chance that a best-of-$7$ series goes to a $7$th game is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{16} \approx 31.25\%}.
> $$
>
> More generally, if one team has per-game win probability $p$, then the probability the series reaches Game $7$ is
>
> $$
> \binom{6}{3} p^3 (1-p)^3.
> $$
>
> In the fair case $p=\tfrac12$, this becomes
>
> $$
> 20\left(\frac12\right)^6 = \frac{5}{16}.
> $$
