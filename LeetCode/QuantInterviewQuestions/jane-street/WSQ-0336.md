> [!example] Question 336 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

(i) How many 7-digit numbers are there in the form abcdcba (symmetric about the middle digit)? (ii) What is the average value of all such numbers?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=337)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let a $7$-digit number of the required form be
>
> $$
> N = \overline{abcdcba},
> $$
>
> where $a,b,c,d$ are digits and, since $N$ is a $7$-digit number, $a \neq 0$.
>
> ## (i) Number of such $7$-digit numbers
>
> - The first digit $a$ can be chosen in $9$ ways: $1,2,\dots,9$.
> - The digit $b$ can be chosen in $10$ ways.
> - The digit $c$ can be chosen in $10$ ways.
> - The middle digit $d$ can be chosen in $10$ ways.
>
> Once $a,b,c,d$ are chosen, the number is completely determined as $\overline{abcdcba}$.
>
> Hence the total number is
>
> $$
> 9 \cdot 10 \cdot 10 \cdot 10 = 9000.
> $$
>
> ## (ii) Average of all such numbers
>
> Write the number in expanded form:
>
> $$
> N = 1000000a + 100000b + 10000c + 1000d + 100c + 10b + a.
> $$
>
> So
>
> $$
> N = 1000001a + 100010b + 10100c + 1000d.
> $$
>
> To find the average, take the average value of each digit independently.
>
> - Since $a$ ranges from $1$ to $9$, its average is
>
> $$
> \frac{1+9}{2} = 5.
> $$
>
> - Since $b,c,d$ each range from $0$ to $9$, their average is
>
> $$
> \frac{0+9}{2} = 4.5.
> $$
>
> Therefore the average number is
>
> $$
> 1000001(5) + 100010(4.5) + 10100(4.5) + 1000(4.5).
> $$
>
> Compute:
>
> $$
> 1000001 \cdot 5 = 5000005,
> $$
>
> $$
> 100010 \cdot 4.5 = 450045,
> $$
>
> $$
> 10100 \cdot 4.5 = 45450,
> $$
>
> $$
> 1000 \cdot 4.5 = 4500.
> $$
>
> Adding,
>
> $$
> 5000005 + 450045 + 45450 + 4500 = 5500000.
> $$
>
> Thus the average of all such numbers is
>
> $$
> 5500000.
> $$
>
> ## Final answers
>
> $$
> \text{(i) } 9000
> $$
>
> $$
> \text{(ii) } 5500000
> $$
