> [!example] Question 263 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Intern Interview

You have 4 coins. You throw them in the air. For every coin that lands heads, you get 1 dollar. How much would you pay to play this game?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=264)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the number of heads obtained when $4$ fair coins are tossed.
>
> Each coin contributes:
>
> - $1$ dollar with probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$,
> - $0$ dollars with probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$.
>
> Thus the expected payoff from one coin is
>
> $$
> E[\text{payoff from one coin}] = 1 \cdot \frac{1}{2} + 0 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{2}.
> $$
>
> Since there are $4$ independent coins, the total expected payoff is
>
> $$
> E[X] = 4 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 2.
> $$
>
> Therefore, a fair price to pay to play the game is
>
> $$
> \boxed{2 \text{ dollars}}.
> $$
