> [!example] Question 97 · Jane Street
> ## Trading Interview

What is the last digit of 3 raised to the 33rd power? How many six-digit numbers contain all the digits from 1 to 6 inclusive? What is the mean of all such numbers?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=98)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To determine the last digit of $3^{33}$, examine the pattern of last digits of powers of $3$:
>
> $$
> 3^1 \equiv 3 \pmod{10},\quad
> 3^2 \equiv 9 \pmod{10},\quad
> 3^3 \equiv 7 \pmod{10},\quad
> 3^4 \equiv 1 \pmod{10}.
> $$
>
> The pattern repeats every $4$ powers. Since
>
> $$
> 33 \equiv 1 \pmod{4},
> $$
>
> $3^{33}$ has the same last digit as $3^1$, namely
>
> $$
> \boxed{3}.
> $$
>
> Now consider the $6$-digit numbers that include all digits from $1$ to $6$ inclusive. Since there are exactly $6$ positions and all six digits must appear, each such number is simply a permutation of the digits $1,2,3,4,5,6$.
>
> The number of such numbers is therefore
>
> $$
> 6! = 720.
> $$
>
> So the total number is
>
> $$
> \boxed{720}.
> $$
>
> To find the mean of all these numbers, use symmetry. In all $720$ permutations, each digit appears equally often in each place value.
>
> Fix any one position, such as the units place. For each choice of digit in that place, the remaining $5$ digits can be arranged in
>
> $$
> 5! = 120
> $$
>
> ways. Thus each of the digits $1,2,3,4,5,6$ appears exactly $120$ times in each position.
>
> The sum of the digits is
>
> $$
> 1+2+3+4+5+6 = 21.
> $$
>
> Hence the total contribution to any one position from all numbers is
>
> $$
> 120 \cdot 21.
> $$
>
> Therefore the sum of all $720$ numbers is
>
> $$
> 120 \cdot 21 \cdot (100000+10000+1000+100+10+1).
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> 100000+10000+1000+100+10+1 = 111111,
> $$
>
> the total sum is
>
> $$
> 120 \cdot 21 \cdot 111111.
> $$
>
> The mean is this total divided by $720$:
>
> $$
> \frac{120 \cdot 21 \cdot 111111}{720}.
> $$
>
> Because
>
> $$
> \frac{120}{720} = \frac{1}{6},
> $$
>
> the mean is
>
> $$
> \frac{21}{6}\cdot 111111 = \frac{7}{2}\cdot 111111 = 388888.5.
> $$
>
> Thus the mean of all such numbers is
>
> $$
> \boxed{388888.5}.
> $$
>
> Final answers:
>
> - Last digit of $3^{33}$: $\boxed{3}$
> - Number of such $6$-digit numbers: $\boxed{720}$
> - Mean of all such numbers: $\boxed{388888.5}$
