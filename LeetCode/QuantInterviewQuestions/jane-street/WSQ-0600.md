> [!example] Question 600 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is the nearest integer to the square root of 5500?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=601)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the nearest integer to $\sqrt{5500}$, compare nearby perfect squares:
>
> $$74^2 = 5476,\qquad 75^2 = 5625.$$
>
> Since
>
> $$5476 < 5500 < 5625,$$
>
> we have
>
> $$74 < \sqrt{5500} < 75.$$
>
> Now check which square is closer to $5500$:
>
> $$5500 - 5476 = 24,$$
> $$5625 - 5500 = 125.$$
>
> Because $24 < 125$, $\sqrt{5500}$ is closer to $74$ than to $75$.
>
> \[
> \boxed{74}
> \]
