> [!example] Question 465 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

If you earn one dollar for every head you get when flipping a fair coin, what is the expected amount you will make after 4 flips?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=466)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the number of heads obtained in $4$ fair coin flips. Since each flip is independent and has probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$ of landing heads, we have
>
> $$
> X \sim \text{Binomial}(4, \tfrac{1}{2}).
> $$
>
> You earn \(\text{\textdollar}1\) for each head, so the total amount earned is exactly $X$ dollars. The expected value of a binomial random variable is
>
> $$
> E[X] = np.
> $$
>
> Here, $n = 4$ and $p = \tfrac{1}{2}$, so
>
> $$
> E[X] = 4 \cdot \tfrac{1}{2} = 2.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected amount you will make is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\$2}.
> $$
