> [!example] Question 169 · Jane Street
> ## Intern Interview

What is the result of multiplying 567 by 39?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=170)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute by distribution:
> $$
> 567 \times 39 = 567 \times (40 - 1).
> $$
>
> Then
> $$
> 567 \times 40 = 22680,
> $$
> and
> $$
> 567 \times 1 = 567.
> $$
>
> So
> $$
> 567 \times 39 = 22680 - 567 = 22113.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the result is
> $$
> \boxed{22113}.
> $$
