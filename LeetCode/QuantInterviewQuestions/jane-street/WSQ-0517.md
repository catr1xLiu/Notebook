> [!example] Question 517 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

What is the weight of a bowling ball with a diameter of 12 inches, given that one with a diameter of 8 inches weighs 10 pounds?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=518)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Assume the bowling balls are made of the same material, so weight is proportional to volume.
>
> The volume of a sphere is
>
> $$
> V=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3
> $$
>
> Since the radius is half the diameter, volume is proportional to the cube of the diameter. Thus,
>
> $$
> \frac{W_2}{W_1}=\left(\frac{d_2}{d_1}\right)^3
> $$
>
> Given
>
> $$
> W_1=10,\quad d_1=8,\quad d_2=12
> $$
>
> we get
>
> $$
> W_2=10\left(\frac{12}{8}\right)^3
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> \frac{12}{8}=\frac{3}{2}
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> W_2=10\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^3=10\cdot \frac{27}{8}=\frac{270}{8}=33.75
> $$
>
> Therefore, the 12-inch bowling ball would weigh
>
> $$
> \boxed{33.75\text{ pounds}}
> $$
>
> provided the density is the same.
