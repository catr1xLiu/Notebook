> [!example] Question 228 · Jane Street
> ## Internship Interview

There are 8 people in a room. Everyone shakes hands with each other exactly once. Calculate the total number of handshakes.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=229)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Each handshake is determined by a pair of distinct people. Thus the total number of handshakes is the number of ways to choose $2$ people from $8$:
>
> $$
> \binom{8}{2} = \frac{8 \cdot 7}{2} = 28.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the number of handshakes is
>
> $$
> \boxed{28}.
> $$
