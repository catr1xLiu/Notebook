> [!example] Question 391 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is the expected number of heads when you toss a fair coin 6 times?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=392)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the number of heads obtained in $6$ tosses of a fair coin.
>
> Each toss can be modeled by an indicator random variable:
> $$
> X_i =
> \begin{cases}
> 1, & \text{if toss } i \text{ is heads},\\
> 0, & \text{if toss } i \text{ is tails}.
> \end{cases}
> $$
>
> Then
> $$
> X = X_1 + X_2 + \cdots + X_6.
> $$
>
> Since the coin is fair,
> $$
> E[X_i] = 1 \cdot \frac{1}{2} + 0 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{2}.
> $$
>
> By linearity of expectation,
> $$
> E[X] = E[X_1] + E[X_2] + \cdots + E[X_6] = 6 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 3.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected number of heads is
> $$
> \boxed{3}.
> $$
