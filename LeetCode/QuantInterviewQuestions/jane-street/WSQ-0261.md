> ## Trader Intern Interview

What is 1,000 to the 1,000th power?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=262)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Use exponent rules:
> $$1000^{1000} = (10^3)^{1000} = 10^{3000}.$$
>
> Thus,
> $$1000^{1000} = 10^{3000},$$
> which is the number $1$ followed by $3000$ zeros.
