> [!example] Question 548 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

If you have \$10 and each mint costs 27 cents, how many mints can you buy?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=549)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Convert the total amount to cents:
>
> $$
> \$10 = 1000 \text{ cents}
> $$
>
> Each mint costs $27$ cents, so the number of mints you can buy is
>
> $$
> \left\lfloor \frac{1000}{27} \right\rfloor = 37
> $$
>
> since
>
> $$
> 27 \cdot 37 = 999
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> 27 \cdot 38 = 1026 > 1000.
> $$
>
> Therefore, you can buy **$37$ mints**.
