> [!example] Question 353 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

If you flip 4 fair coins, what is the expected number of heads?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=354)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the number of heads obtained when $4$ fair coins are flipped.
>
> Define indicator variables
> $$
> X_i =
> \begin{cases}
> 1, & \text{if the $i$th coin is heads},\\
> 0, & \text{if the $i$th coin is tails},
> \end{cases}
> \qquad i=1,2,3,4.
> $$
>
> Then
> $$
> X = X_1 + X_2 + X_3 + X_4.
> $$
>
> Since each coin is fair,
> $$
> E[X_i] = 1 \cdot \frac{1}{2} + 0 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{2}.
> $$
>
> By linearity of expectation,
> $$
> E[X] = E[X_1] + E[X_2] + E[X_3] + E[X_4]
> = 4 \cdot \frac{1}{2}
> = 2.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected number of heads is
> $$
> \boxed{2}.
> $$
