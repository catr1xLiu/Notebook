> [!example] Question 579 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is the value of 49 × 51?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=580)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute
> $$49 \times 51.$$
>
> Use the identity
> $$
> (a-b)(a+b)=a^2-b^2
> $$
> with $a=50$ and $b=1$. Then
> $$
> 49 \times 51=(50-1)(50+1)=50^2-1^2=2500-1=2499.
> $$
>
> $$\boxed{2499}$$
