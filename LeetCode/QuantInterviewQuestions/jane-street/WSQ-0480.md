> [!example] Question 480 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

What is the angle between the hour and minute hands on a clock at 9:40?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=481)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> At $9{:}40$:
>
> - The minute hand is at $40$ minutes, so its angle from $12$ is
>   $$
>   40 \times 6^\circ = 240^\circ.
>   $$
>
> - The hour hand at $9{:}00$ is at
>   $$
>   9 \times 30^\circ = 270^\circ.
>   $$
>   In $40$ minutes, it moves further by
>   $$
>   40 \times 0.5^\circ = 20^\circ.
>   $$
>   So its angle from $12$ is
>   $$
>   270^\circ + 20^\circ = 290^\circ.
>   $$
>
> Therefore, the angle between the two hands is
> $$
> |290^\circ - 240^\circ| = 50^\circ.
> $$
>
> Thus, the smaller angle between the hands is
> $$
> \boxed{50^\circ}.
> $$
