> [!example] Question 486 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

The date is written as DD/MM/YYYY. What is the closest date that will use at most one digit throughout the date?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=487)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> A date that uses at most one digit throughout must have every digit the same. Thus the only possible dates are of the form
>
> $$
> dd/dd/dddd
> $$
>
> with a single repeated digit.
>
> For such a string to be a valid date:
>
> - the day must be a valid two-digit day,
> - the month must be a valid two-digit month.
>
> Check the possible repeated digits:
>
> - $00/00/0000$ is invalid.
> - $11/11/1111$ is valid: day $11$, month $11$.
> - $22/22/2222$ is invalid since month $22$ is impossible.
> - Any larger repeated digit gives an invalid month as well.
>
> So the only valid date using at most one digit is
>
> $$
> 11/11/1111.
> $$
>
> Hence the closest such date is
>
> $$
> \boxed{11/11/1111}.
> $$
