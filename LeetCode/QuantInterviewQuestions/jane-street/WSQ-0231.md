> [!example] Question 231 · Jane Street
> ## Internship Interview

What is 253 multiplied by 387? Solve this without using pen, paper, or a calculator.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=232)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute mentally by distributing:
>
> $$253 \cdot 387 = 253 \cdot (400 - 13).$$
>
> First,
>
> $$253 \cdot 400 = 101200.$$
>
> Next,
>
> $$253 \cdot 13 = 253 \cdot (10 + 3) = 2530 + 759 = 3289.$$
>
> So,
>
> $$253 \cdot 387 = 101200 - 3289 = 97911.$$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$253 \cdot 387 = 97911.$$
