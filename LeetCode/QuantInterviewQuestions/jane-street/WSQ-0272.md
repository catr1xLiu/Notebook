What is the cube root of 169381?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=273)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find $\sqrt[3]{169381}$, check nearby perfect cubes:
>
> $$55^3 = 55 \cdot 55 \cdot 55 = 3025 \cdot 55 = 166375$$
>
> $$56^3 = 56 \cdot 56 \cdot 56 = 3136 \cdot 56 = 175616$$
>
> Since
>
> $$166375 < 169381 < 175616,$$
>
> the cube root lies between $55$ and $56$.
>
> Now test $55.33$:
>
> $$55.33^3 \approx 169381$$
>
> Hence,
>
> $$\sqrt[3]{169381} \approx 55.33$$
>
> So the cube root is
>
> $$\boxed{55.33}$$
