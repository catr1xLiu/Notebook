> [!example] Question 554 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

How many digits are in 99 raised to the 10th power (99^10)?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=555)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To determine the number of digits in $99^{10}$, use the fact that a positive integer $N$ has
>
> $$
> \lfloor \log_{10} N \rfloor + 1
> $$
>
> digits.
>
> Here,
>
> $$
> N = 99^{10}.
> $$
>
> So the number of digits is
>
> $$
> \lfloor \log_{10}(99^{10}) \rfloor + 1
> = \lfloor 10 \log_{10}(99) \rfloor + 1.
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> \log_{10}(99) = \log_{10}(100) + \log_{10}(0.99)
> = 2 + \log_{10}(0.99),
> $$
>
> and $\log_{10}(0.99) < 0$, we have
>
> $$
> \log_{10}(99) < 2.
> $$
>
> Also, $99$ is very close to $100$, so
>
> $$
> 10 \log_{10}(99) \approx 19.956\ldots
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \lfloor 10 \log_{10}(99) \rfloor = 19.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the number of digits in $99^{10}$ is
>
> $$
> 19 + 1 = 20.
> $$
>
> \[
> \boxed{20}
> \]
