> [!example] Question 452 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

If Worker A takes 1 hour to finish a job and Worker B takes 2 hours to finish the same job, how long would it take them to complete the job working together?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=453)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To combine their work rates, add the fraction of the job each completes per hour.
>
> - Worker A completes $1$ job in $1$ hour, so A’s rate is
>   $$
>   1 \text{ job/hour}.
>   $$
>
> - Worker B completes $1$ job in $2$ hours, so B’s rate is
>   $$
>   \frac{1}{2} \text{ job/hour}.
>   $$
>
> Together, their combined rate is
> $$
> 1 + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2} \text{ jobs/hour}.
> $$
>
> The time to complete $1$ job is the reciprocal of the combined rate:
> $$
> \text{time} = \frac{1}{3/2} = \frac{2}{3} \text{ hour}.
> $$
>
> Thus, they would finish the job in
> $$
> \frac{2}{3} \text{ hour} = 40 \text{ minutes}.
> $$
>
> **Answer:** $40$ minutes.
