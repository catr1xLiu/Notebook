> [!example] Question 581 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is the probability of getting at least 2 heads when tossing a fair coin 4 times?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=582)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> For $4$ fair coin tosses, the total number of equally likely outcomes is
>
> $$
> 2^4 = 16.
> $$
>
> “At least $2$ heads” means getting either $2$, $3$, or $4$ heads.
>
> Count these outcomes using combinations:
>
> - Exactly $2$ heads:  
>   $$
>   \binom{4}{2} = 6
>   $$
> - Exactly $3$ heads:  
>   $$
>   \binom{4}{3} = 4
>   $$
> - Exactly $4$ heads:  
>   $$
>   \binom{4}{4} = 1
>   $$
>
> So the number of favorable outcomes is
>
> $$
> 6+4+1=11.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability is
>
> $$
> \frac{11}{16}.
> $$
>
> Thus, the probability of getting at least $2$ heads in $4$ coin tosses is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{11}{16}}.
> $$
