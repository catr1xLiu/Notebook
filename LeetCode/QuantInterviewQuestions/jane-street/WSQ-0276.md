> [!example] Question 276 · Jane Street
> ## Junior Trader Interview

If the chance of rain on Saturday is 70% and on Sunday is 60%, what is the probability that it will rain at least once during the weekend?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=277)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $A$ be the event that it rains on Saturday, and $B$ the event that it rains on Sunday. We are given
>
> $$
> P(A)=0.7, \qquad P(B)=0.6.
> $$
>
> The phrase “it rains this weekend” means that it rains on **at least one** of the two days, so we want
>
> $$
> P(A \cup B).
> $$
>
> Assuming the rain chances on Saturday and Sunday are independent, use the complement:
>
> $$
> P(A \cup B)=1-P(\text{no rain on both days}).
> $$
>
> The probability of no rain on Saturday is
>
> $$
> 1-0.7=0.3,
> $$
>
> and the probability of no rain on Sunday is
>
> $$
> 1-0.6=0.4.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> P(\text{no rain on both days})=0.3 \cdot 0.4=0.12.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> P(A \cup B)=1-0.12=0.88.
> $$
>
> So, the chance of it raining this weekend is
>
> $$
> \boxed{88\%}.
> $$
