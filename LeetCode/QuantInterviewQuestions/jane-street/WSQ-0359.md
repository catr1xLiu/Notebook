> [!example] Question 359 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

You are given a six-sided die. What is the expected value of the difference between two dice rolls?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=360)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let the two rolls be $X$ and $Y$, where $X, Y \in \{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$ and the rolls are independent.
>
> We want the expected value of the difference:
> $$
> E[X-Y].
> $$
>
> Using linearity of expectation,
> $$
> E[X-Y] = E[X] - E[Y].
> $$
>
> Since both rolls are from the same fair six-sided die,
> $$
> E[X] = E[Y] = \frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6} = \frac{21}{6} = 3.5.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
> $$
> E[X-Y] = 3.5 - 3.5 = 0.
> $$
>
> Thus, the expected value of the difference between the two rolls is
> $$
> \boxed{0}.
> $$
>
> If instead the intended meaning was the expected value of the absolute difference, $E[|X-Y|]$, that would be a different problem.
