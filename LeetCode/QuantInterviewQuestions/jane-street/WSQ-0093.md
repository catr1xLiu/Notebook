> [!example] Question 93 · Jane Street
> ## Trading Interview

What is the probability that the World Series goes to the 7th game?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=94)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Assume the two teams are evenly matched, so each game is independent and each team wins any given game with probability $1/2$.
>
> The World Series is a best-of-seven series, so it goes to the $7$th game exactly when, after $6$ games, each team has won $3$ games.
>
> Thus we need the probability of getting exactly $3$ wins for one team in $6$ games:
>
> $$
> P(\text{series reaches game } 7)=\binom{6}{3}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^6.
> $$
>
> Compute this:
>
> $$
> \binom{6}{3} = 20,
> \qquad
> \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^6 = \frac{1}{64}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> P(\text{series reaches game } 7)=20\cdot \frac{1}{64}=\frac{20}{64}=\frac{5}{16}.
> $$
>
> So the probability is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{16}}.
> $$
