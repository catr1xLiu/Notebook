> [!example] Question 338 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

You throw two eight-sided dice. What is the probability that the product of the two numbers shown is even?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=339)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Each eight-sided die has outcomes $1,2,\dots,8$, so there are
>
> $$
> 8 \times 8 = 64
> $$
>
> equally likely ordered outcomes.
>
> The product of the two numbers is even unless both numbers are odd. Thus it is easier to compute the complement.
>
> On one die, the odd outcomes are
>
> $$
> 1,3,5,7
> $$
>
> so the probability of rolling an odd number is
>
> $$
> \frac{4}{8}=\frac{1}{2}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability that both dice show odd numbers is
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{2}\cdot \frac{1}{2}=\frac{1}{4}.
> $$
>
> Hence, the probability that the product is even is
>
> $$
> 1-\frac{1}{4}=\frac{3}{4}.
> $$
>
> So the answer is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{3}{4}}.
> $$
