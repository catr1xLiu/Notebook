> [!example] Question 149 · Jane Street
> ## Intern Interview

Calculate the angle between the hour and minute hands of a clock at a given time.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=150)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let the time be $h$ hours and $m$ minutes.
>
> The hour hand moves
>
> $$
> 30^\circ \text{ per hour} = 0.5^\circ \text{ per minute},
> $$
>
> so at time $h:m$ its position is
>
> $$
> 30h + 0.5m.
> $$
>
> The minute hand moves
>
> $$
> 6^\circ \text{ per minute},
> $$
>
> so its position is
>
> $$
> 6m.
> $$
>
> Hence the angle between the two hands is
>
> $$
> \left| (30h + 0.5m) - 6m \right|
> = \left| 30h - 5.5m \right|.
> $$
>
> Since the smaller angle between the hands is usually desired, the answer is
>
> $$
> \theta = \min\!\left(\left|30h - 5.5m\right|,\; 360 - \left|30h - 5.5m\right|\right).
> $$
>
> Thus, the angle between the clock’s hands at time $h:m$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\min\!\left(\left|30h - 5.5m\right|,\; 360 - \left|30h - 5.5m\right|\right)^\circ}.
> $$
>
> If a specific time is given, substitute $h$ and $m$ into this formula.
