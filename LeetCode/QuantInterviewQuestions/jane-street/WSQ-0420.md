> [!example] Question 420 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is 87 multiplied by 46?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=421)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute by distribution:
> $$
> 87 \times 46 = 87 \times (40 + 6) = 87 \times 40 + 87 \times 6.
> $$
>
> Now evaluate each term:
> $$
> 87 \times 40 = 3480,
> \qquad
> 87 \times 6 = 522.
> $$
>
> Add them:
> $$
> 3480 + 522 = 4002.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
> $$
> 87 \times 46 = 4002.
> $$
