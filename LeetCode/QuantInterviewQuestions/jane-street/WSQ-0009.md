> [!example] Question 9 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

Twenty-five people each shake hands with every other person exactly once. How many handshakes take place in total?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=10)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Each handshake is determined by a pair of people. Thus the total number of handshakes is the number of ways to choose $2$ people from $25$:
>
> $$
> \binom{25}{2}=\frac{25\cdot 24}{2}=25\cdot 12=300.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the number of handshakes is
>
> $$
> \boxed{300}.
> $$
