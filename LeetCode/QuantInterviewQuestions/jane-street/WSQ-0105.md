> [!example] Question 105 · Jane Street
> ## Trading Interview

A white cube is painted red on its outer surface and then divided into 27 equal smaller cubes. If you randomly select one small cube and roll it, what is the probability that all 5 visible faces are white?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=106)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> $$37^2 = 1369.$$
>
> A cube is painted red on the outside and then divided into $27 = 3^3$ equal small cubes, so the large cube is split into a $3 \times 3 \times 3$ arrangement.
>
> To see $5$ white faces when one small cube is picked and rolled, the chosen small cube must have exactly one red face, since the only hidden face is the one on the bottom. Thus all other $5$ visible faces will be white.
>
> In a $3 \times 3 \times 3$ cube, the small cubes with exactly one painted face are the center cubes of each face. There are $6$ such cubes.
>
> The total number of small cubes is $27$.
>
> Therefore, the required probability is
> $$\frac{6}{27} = \frac{2}{9}.$$
>
> $$\boxed{1369,\ \frac{2}{9}}$$
