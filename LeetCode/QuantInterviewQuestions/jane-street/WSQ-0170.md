> [!example] Question 170 · Jane Street
> ## Intern Interview

What is the probability that if you roll two dice, the sum is greater than 7?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=171)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the probability that the sum of two fair six-sided dice is greater than $7$, count the favorable outcomes and divide by the total number of possible outcomes.
>
> ## Step 1: Total number of outcomes
>
> Each die has $6$ possible outcomes, so the total number of equally likely outcomes is
>
> $$
> 6 \times 6 = 36.
> $$
>
> ## Step 2: Favorable outcomes
>
> We need sums greater than $7$, namely:
>
> $$
> 8, 9, 10, 11, 12.
> $$
>
> Count the number of ways to obtain each sum:
>
> - Sum $8$: $(2,6), (3,5), (4,4), (5,3), (6,2)$ → $5$ ways
> - Sum $9$: $(3,6), (4,5), (5,4), (6,3)$ → $4$ ways
> - Sum $10$: $(4,6), (5,5), (6,4)$ → $3$ ways
> - Sum $11$: $(5,6), (6,5)$ → $2$ ways
> - Sum $12$: $(6,6)$ → $1$ way
>
> Thus, the total number of favorable outcomes is
>
> $$
> 5 + 4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 15.
> $$
>
> ## Step 3: Compute the probability
>
> $$
> P(\text{sum} > 7) = \frac{15}{36} = \frac{5}{12}.
> $$
>
> ## Final Answer
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{12}}
> $$
