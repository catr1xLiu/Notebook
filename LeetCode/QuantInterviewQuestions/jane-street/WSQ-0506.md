> [!example] Question 506 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

Find the sum of all odd numbers between 1 and 50. Then, how many 4-digit numbers can be made using the digits 1, 2, 3, and 4 (with or without repeating digits), and what is their average?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=507)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> ## 1. Sum of odd numbers between $1$ and $50$
>
> The odd numbers between $1$ and $50$ are
>
> $$
> 1,3,5,\dots,49.
> $$
>
> This is an arithmetic sequence with first term $a_1=1$, last term $a_n=49$, and common difference $d=2$.
>
> The number of terms is
>
> $$
> n=\frac{49-1}{2}+1=25.
> $$
>
> Hence the sum is
>
> $$
> S=\frac{n}{2}(a_1+a_n)=\frac{25}{2}(1+49)=\frac{25}{2}\cdot 50=625.
> $$
>
> Thus, the sum is
>
> $$
> \boxed{625}.
> $$
>
> ---
>
> ## 2. Number of $4$-digit numbers formed from digits $1,2,3,4$
>
> ### (a) Without repetition
>
> A $4$-digit number using $1,2,3,4$ without repetition must use each digit exactly once.
>
> The number of such numbers is
>
> $$
> 4! = 24.
> $$
>
> So, the number of $4$-digit numbers without repetition is
>
> $$
> \boxed{24}.
> $$
>
> ### Average of these numbers
>
> In all $24$ arrangements, each digit appears equally often in each place value.
>
> Each digit appears
>
> $$
> \frac{24}{4}=6
> $$
>
> times in the thousands place, hundreds place, tens place, and units place.
>
> Since
>
> $$
> 1+2+3+4=10,
> $$
>
> the total sum of all such numbers is
>
> $$
> 6\cdot 10 \cdot (1000+100+10+1).
> $$
>
> Now,
>
> $$
> 1000+100+10+1=1111,
> $$
>
> so the total sum is
>
> $$
> 6\cdot 10\cdot 1111=66660.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the average is
>
> $$
> \frac{66660}{24}=2777.5.
> $$
>
> Thus, without repetition:
>
> - Number of $4$-digit numbers: $$\boxed{24}$$
> - Average: $$\boxed{2777.5}$$
>
> ---
>
> ### (b) With repetition allowed
>
> Each of the $4$ positions can be filled in $4$ ways, so the total number is
>
> $$
> 4^4=256.
> $$
>
> Thus, the number of $4$-digit numbers with repetition allowed is
>
> $$
> \boxed{256}.
> $$
>
> ### Average of these numbers
>
> For each place value, each digit $1,2,3,4$ appears equally often.
>
> At any fixed position, each digit appears
>
> $$
> 4^3=64
> $$
>
> times.
>
> Hence the total sum of all numbers is
>
> $$
> 64\cdot (1+2+3+4)\cdot (1000+100+10+1).
> $$
>
> That is,
>
> $$
> 64\cdot 10\cdot 1111=711040.
> $$
>
> So the average is
>
> $$
> \frac{711040}{256}=2777.5.
> $$
>
> Thus, with repetition allowed:
>
> - Number of $4$-digit numbers: $$\boxed{256}$$
> - Average: $$\boxed{2777.5}$$
>
> ---
>
> ## Final Answers
>
> $$
> \text{Sum of odd numbers from }1\text{ to }49 = \boxed{625}
> $$
>
> Using digits $1,2,3,4$:
>
> - Without repetition:
>   $$
>   \boxed{24 \text{ numbers, average } 2777.5}
>   $$
>
> - With repetition:
>   $$
>   \boxed{256 \text{ numbers, average } 2777.5}
>   $$
