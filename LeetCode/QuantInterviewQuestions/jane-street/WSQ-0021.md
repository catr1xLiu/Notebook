> [!example] Question 21 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

What is the expected value of the sum when rolling two dice, one with 11 sides and the other with 7 sides?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=22)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the outcome of the $11$-sided die and $Y$ the outcome of the $7$-sided die. Assume each die is fair and labeled $1,2,\dots,11$ and $1,2,\dots,7$, respectively.
>
> The expected value of a fair $n$-sided die labeled $1$ through $n$ is
>
> $$
> E[\text{die}] = \frac{1+2+\cdots+n}{n} = \frac{n+1}{2}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{11+1}{2} = 6
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> E[Y] = \frac{7+1}{2} = 4.
> $$
>
> By linearity of expectation,
>
> $$
> E[X+Y] = E[X] + E[Y] = 6 + 4 = 10.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected sum is
>
> $$
> \boxed{10}.
> $$
