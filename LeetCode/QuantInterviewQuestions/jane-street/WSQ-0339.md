> [!example] Question 339 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is the last digit of 17 raised to the power of 17?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=340)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the last digit of $17^{17}$, only the last digit of the base matters. Thus we consider powers of $7$.
>
> Compute the pattern of last digits of powers of $7$:
>
> $$
> 7^1 \equiv 7 \pmod{10}
> $$
>
> $$
> 7^2 \equiv 49 \equiv 9 \pmod{10}
> $$
>
> $$
> 7^3 \equiv 7 \cdot 9 = 63 \equiv 3 \pmod{10}
> $$
>
> $$
> 7^4 \equiv 7 \cdot 3 = 21 \equiv 1 \pmod{10}
> $$
>
> So the last digits repeat every $4$ powers:
>
> $$
> 7, 9, 3, 1
> $$
>
> Now compute:
>
> $$
> 17 \equiv 1 \pmod{4}
> $$
>
> Therefore, $7^{17}$ has the same last digit as $7^1$, which is $7$.
>
> Hence, the last digit of $17^{17}$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{7}
> $$
