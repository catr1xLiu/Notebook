> [!example] Question 500 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

1. What is the probability of getting a head when flipping a fair coin once?
2. What is the expected value of the sum obtained when flipping 8 fair coins, where a head counts as 1 and a tail as 0?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=501)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ denote the result of one fair coin flip, where
>
> $$
> X=
> \begin{cases}
> 1, & \text{if Head},\\
> 0, & \text{if Tail}.
> \end{cases}
> $$
>
> ## 1. Probability of getting a head in one flip
>
> For a fair coin, the two outcomes are equally likely. Therefore,
>
> $$
> P(\text{Head})=\frac{1}{2}.
> $$
>
> ## 2. Expectation of the sum of $8$ coin flips
>
> Let $X_1, X_2, \dots, X_8$ be the indicator variables for the $8$ flips, where
>
> $$
> X_i=
> \begin{cases}
> 1, & \text{if the $i$th flip is Head},\\
> 0, & \text{if the $i$th flip is Tail}.
> \end{cases}
> $$
>
> The sum of the $8$ flips is
>
> $$
> S=X_1+X_2+\cdots+X_8.
> $$
>
> Since each flip is fair,
>
> $$
> E[X_i]=1\cdot \frac{1}{2}+0\cdot \frac{1}{2}=\frac{1}{2}.
> $$
>
> Using linearity of expectation,
>
> $$
> E[S]=E[X_1]+E[X_2]+\cdots+E[X_8]
> =8\cdot \frac{1}{2}=4.
> $$
>
> ## Final answers
>
> $$
> P(\text{Head in one flip})=\frac{1}{2}
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> E[\text{sum of $8$ flips}]=4.
> $$
