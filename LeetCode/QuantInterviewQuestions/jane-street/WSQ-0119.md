> [!example] Question 119 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Intern Interview

Given one fair 10-sided die and one fair 6-sided die, what is the expected value of the sum of their outcomes?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=120)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the outcome of the fair $10$-sided die and $Y$ be the outcome of the fair $6$-sided die. The total outcome is
>
> $$
> T = X + Y.
> $$
>
> For a fair die with faces numbered from $1$ to $n$, the expected value is
>
> $$
> E[\text{die}] = \frac{1+2+\cdots+n}{n} = \frac{n+1}{2}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{10+1}{2} = \frac{11}{2},
> \qquad
> E[Y] = \frac{6+1}{2} = \frac{7}{2}.
> $$
>
> By linearity of expectation,
>
> $$
> E[T] = E[X] + E[Y] = \frac{11}{2} + \frac{7}{2} = \frac{18}{2} = 9.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected value of the outcome is
>
> $$
> \boxed{9}.
> $$
