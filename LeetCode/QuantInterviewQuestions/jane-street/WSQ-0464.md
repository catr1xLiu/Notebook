> [!example] Question 464 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

The probability of raining on Saturday is 30%, and the probability of raining on Sunday is 40%.

(a) What is the probability it will rain on the weekend?
(b) What assumption is made in this calculation?
(c) If the events are not independent, what are the maximum and minimum possible probabilities that it will rain during the weekend? (Answer: 40%, 70%)

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=465)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> ## Solution
>
> Let $S$ be the event that it rains on Saturday, and let $U$ be the event that it rains on Sunday.
>
> We are given
>
> $$
> P(S)=0.3, \qquad P(U)=0.4.
> $$
>
> The event “it rains on the weekend” means that it rains on at least one of the two days, so the required probability is
>
> $$
> P(S\cup U).
> $$
>
> Using the addition rule,
>
> $$
> P(S\cup U)=P(S)+P(U)-P(S\cap U).
> $$
>
> ### (a) Probability of raining on the weekend
>
> If we assume Saturday and Sunday rain are independent, then
>
> $$
> P(S\cap U)=P(S)P(U)=0.3\times 0.4=0.12.
> $$
>
> Hence
>
> $$
> P(S\cup U)=0.3+0.4-0.12=0.58.
> $$
>
> So the probability of rain on the weekend is
>
> $$
> \boxed{58\%}.
> $$
>
> ### (b) Assumption made
>
> The assumption is that the events “rain on Saturday” and “rain on Sunday” are **independent**; that is,
>
> $$
> P(S\cap U)=P(S)P(U).
> $$
>
> ### (c) If they are not independent, find the maximum and minimum probability of weekend rain
>
> Without independence, we only know
>
> $$
> P(S\cup U)=P(S)+P(U)-P(S\cap U)=0.7-P(S\cap U).
> $$
>
> Thus the value depends on how large or small $P(S\cap U)$ can be.
>
> For any two events,
>
> $$
> \max\{0,\,P(S)+P(U)-1\}\le P(S\cap U)\le \min\{P(S),P(U)\}.
> $$
>
> Here,
>
> $$
> \max\{0,\,0.3+0.4-1\}=0,
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> \min\{0.3,0.4\}=0.3.
> $$
>
> So
>
> $$
> 0\le P(S\cap U)\le 0.3.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> - the **maximum** value of $P(S\cup U)$ occurs when $P(S\cap U)=0$:
>
> $$
> P(S\cup U)=0.7-0=0.7=70\%.
> $$
>
> - the **minimum** value of $P(S\cup U)$ occurs when $P(S\cap U)=0.3$:
>
> $$
> P(S\cup U)=0.7-0.3=0.4=40\%.
> $$
>
> Hence,
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Minimum }=40\%, \qquad \text{Maximum }=70\%.}
> $$
>
> ## Final Answers
>
> $$
> \text{(a) } 58\%
> $$
>
> $$
> \text{(b) Independence of Saturday and Sunday rain}
> $$
>
> $$
> \text{(c) Minimum }=40\%, \quad \text{Maximum }=70\%
> $$
