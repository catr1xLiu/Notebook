> [!example] Question 370 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

In a best-of-seven World Series, what are the odds that the series goes to seven games if each team has an equal chance of winning each game?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=371)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Assume the World Series is a best-of-$7$ series, so it goes exactly $7$ games if and only if, after $6$ games, each team has won $3$ games.
>
> Since the teams are equally likely to win any game, each sequence of $6$ game outcomes is equally likely, with probability
>
> $$
> \left(\frac12\right)^6 = \frac{1}{64}.
> $$
>
> For the series to reach a seventh game, one team must win exactly $3$ of the first $6$ games and the other team must also win exactly $3$. The number of such sequences is
>
> $$
> \binom{6}{3} = 20.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability that the series goes $7$ games is
>
> $$
> \binom{6}{3}\left(\frac12\right)^6
> = 20 \cdot \frac{1}{64}
> = \frac{20}{64}
> = \frac{5}{16}.
> $$
>
> Thus, the odds are
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{16}}
> $$
>
> which is approximately
>
> $$
> 0.3125 = 31.25\%.
> $$
>
> If expressed as betting-style odds in favor, this is
>
> $$
> 5:11
> $$
>
> since the probability is $5/16$ and the probability against is $11/16$.
