> [!example] Question 364 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

You flip 4 fair coins and receive \$1 for each coin that lands heads. What is the expected value of your total winnings?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=365)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the total amount won when $4$ fair coins are tossed, where each head pays $\text{\textdollar}1$.
>
> Each coin contributes either:
>
> - $\text{\textdollar}1$ with probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$ (head),
> - $\text{\textdollar}0$ with probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$ (tail).
>
> Thus, for one coin, the expected value is
>
> $$
> E[\text{one coin}] = 1 \cdot \frac{1}{2} + 0 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{2}.
> $$
>
> Since there are $4$ independent coins, the total expected value is
>
> $$
> E[X] = 4 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 2.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected winnings are
>
> $$
> \boxed{\$2}.
> $$
