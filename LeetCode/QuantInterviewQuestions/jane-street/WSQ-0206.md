> [!example] Question 206 · Jane Street
> ## Trading Intern Interview

Find the number of digits in 99 raised to the power of 99.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=207)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the number of digits in $99^{99}$, use the fact that for any positive integer $N$, the number of digits is
>
> $$
> \lfloor \log_{10} N \rfloor + 1.
> $$
>
> Here,
>
> $$
> N = 99^{99}.
> $$
>
> So,
>
> $$
> \log_{10}(99^{99}) = 99 \log_{10}(99).
> $$
>
> Now,
>
> $$
> \log_{10}(99) = \log_{10}(100) + \log_{10}(0.99) = 2 + \log_{10}(0.99).
> $$
>
> Using $\log_{10}(99) \approx 1.995635$,
>
> $$
> 99 \log_{10}(99) \approx 99 \times 1.995635 = 197.567865.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \lfloor 197.567865 \rfloor + 1 = 197 + 1 = 198.
> $$
>
> Thus, the number of digits in $99^{99}$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{198}.
> $$
