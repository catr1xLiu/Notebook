> [!example] Question 531 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

Create a four-wide market (i.e., offer four adjacent price ranges) on the possible values of the sum of four fair six-sided dice rolls.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=532)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $S$ be the sum of four fair six-sided die rolls. Then $S$ takes values from $4$ to $24$, with mean
>
> $$
> E[S] = 4 \cdot 3.5 = 14.
> $$
>
> A **four-wide market** is a bid-ask quote with spread $4$, centered near fair value. Since the fair value is $14$, a natural four-wide market is
>
> $$
> 12 \text{ bid at } 16 \text{ offered}.
> $$
>
> So the market is:
>
> $$
> \boxed{12\text{--}16}
> $$
>
> with midpoint $14$ and width $4$.
