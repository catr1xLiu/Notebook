> [!example] Question 516 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

If 1.5 chickens lay 1.5 eggs in 1.5 days, how many eggs do 9 chickens lay in 9 days?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=517)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $r$ be the number of eggs laid by one chicken in one day.
>
> From the statement,
>
> $$
> 1.5 \text{ chickens} \times 1.5 \text{ days} \times r = 1.5 \text{ eggs}.
> $$
>
> So,
>
> $$
> r = \frac{1.5}{1.5 \cdot 1.5} = \frac{1}{1.5} = \frac{2}{3}.
> $$
>
> Thus one chicken lays $\frac{2}{3}$ egg per day.
>
> For $9$ chickens in $9$ days, the number of eggs is
>
> $$
> 9 \cdot 9 \cdot \frac{2}{3} = 81 \cdot \frac{2}{3} = 54.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{54}
> $$
