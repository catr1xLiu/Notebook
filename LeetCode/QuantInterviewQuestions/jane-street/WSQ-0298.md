> [!example] Question 298 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trading Interview

You flip a coin 4 times. What is the probability of getting exactly 2 heads?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=299)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To get exactly $2$ heads in $4$ coin flips, count:
>
> - the total number of possible outcomes:  
>   $$2^4 = 16$$
>
> - the number of outcomes with exactly $2$ heads: choose which $2$ of the $4$ flips are heads, so  
>   $$\binom{4}{2} = 6$$
>
> Therefore, the probability is
>
> $$
> \frac{\binom{4}{2}}{2^4} = \frac{6}{16} = \frac{3}{8}.
> $$
>
> So the probability of getting exactly $2$ heads is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{3}{8}}.
> $$
