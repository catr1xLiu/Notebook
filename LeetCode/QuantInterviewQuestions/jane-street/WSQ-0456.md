> [!example] Question 456 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

Eight people walk into a room and each person shakes hands with every other person exactly once. How many handshakes occur?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=457)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Each handshake is determined by a pair of people. Thus the total number of handshakes is the number of ways to choose $2$ people from $8$:
>
> $$
> \binom{8}{2}=\frac{8\cdot 7}{2}=28.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the number of handshakes is
>
> $$
> 28.
> $$
