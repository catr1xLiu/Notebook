> [!example] Question 164 · Jane Street
> ## Intern Interview

What is the probability that the sum of the first eight prime numbers is odd?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=165)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The first eight prime numbers are
>
> $$
> 2,\,3,\,5,\,7,\,11,\,13,\,17,\,19.
> $$
>
> Among these, only $2$ is even; the other seven primes are odd.
>
> Now use the parity rule:
>
> - the sum of an even number and an odd number has the parity of the odd number,
> - the sum of $7$ odd numbers is odd, since an odd number of odd terms sums to an odd number.
>
> Thus the sum
>
> $$
> 2+3+5+7+11+13+17+19
> $$
>
> is
>
> $$
> \text{even} + \text{odd} = \text{odd}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the sum is certainly odd, so the probability is
>
> $$
> 1.
> $$
