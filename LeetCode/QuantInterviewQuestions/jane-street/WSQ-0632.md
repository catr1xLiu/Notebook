> [!example] Question 632 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is 76 multiplied by 84?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=633)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute
> $$
> 76 \times 84 = 76 \times (80 + 4) = 76 \times 80 + 76 \times 4 = 6080 + 304 = 6384.
> $$
>
> Thus, the result is
> $$
> 6384.
> $$
