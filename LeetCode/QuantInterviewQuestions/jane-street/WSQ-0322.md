> [!example] Question 322 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

How many handshakes occur among 8 men in a room if every pair shakes hands?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=323)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To count the number of handshakes, note that each handshake is determined by choosing a pair of people from the $8$ men.
>
> Thus the total number of handshakes is the number of ways to choose $2$ people from $8$:
>
> $$
> \binom{8}{2}=\frac{8\cdot 7}{2}=28
> $$
>
> Therefore, the number of handshakes is
>
> $$
> \boxed{28}
> $$
