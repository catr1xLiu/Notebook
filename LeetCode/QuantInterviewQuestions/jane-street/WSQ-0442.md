> [!example] Question 442 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

Sum all odd numbers from 1 to 2n + 1.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=443)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the sum of all odd numbers from $1$ to $2n+1$, write the series as
>
> $$
> 1 + 3 + 5 + \cdots + (2n+1).
> $$
>
> This is an arithmetic sequence with:
>
> - first term $a_1 = 1$,
> - last term $a_{n+1} = 2n+1$,
> - number of terms $n+1$.
>
> Using the formula for the sum of an arithmetic sequence,
>
> $$
> S = \frac{\text{number of terms}}{2}\,(\text{first term}+\text{last term}),
> $$
>
> we get
>
> $$
> S = \frac{n+1}{2}\bigl(1+(2n+1)\bigr).
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> 1+(2n+1)=2n+2=2(n+1),
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> S = \frac{n+1}{2}\cdot 2(n+1) = (n+1)^2.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 1+3+5+\cdots+(2n+1) = (n+1)^2.
> $$
>
> So the sum is
>
> $$
> \boxed{(n+1)^2}.
> $$
