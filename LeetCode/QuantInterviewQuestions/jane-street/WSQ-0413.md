> [!example] Question 413 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

A steel ball with an 8 cm diameter weighs 8 ounces. How much does a steel ball with a 12 cm diameter weigh, assuming both balls are made of the same material?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=414)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> For balls made of the same material, weight is proportional to volume. Since the volume of a sphere is
>
> $$
> V=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3,
> $$
>
> the weight scales with the cube of the diameter.
>
> Let $W_1=8$ ounces correspond to diameter $d_1=8$ cm, and let $W_2$ be the weight for diameter $d_2=12$ cm. Then
>
> $$
> \frac{W_2}{W_1}=\left(\frac{d_2}{d_1}\right)^3.
> $$
>
> Substitute the values:
>
> $$
> \frac{W_2}{8}=\left(\frac{12}{8}\right)^3=\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^3=\frac{27}{8}.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> W_2=8\cdot \frac{27}{8}=27.
> $$
>
> So the $12$ cm steel ball weighs
>
> $$
> \boxed{27\text{ ounces}}.
> $$
