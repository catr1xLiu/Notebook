> [!example] Question 247 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Intern Interview

How many heads would you expect to get if you toss 4 fair coins?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=248)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the number of heads obtained when tossing $4$ fair coins.
>
> Each coin has probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$ of landing heads, so we may write
> $$
> X = X_1 + X_2 + X_3 + X_4,
> $$
> where each $X_i$ is an indicator variable:
> $$
> X_i =
> \begin{cases}
> 1, & \text{if the $i$th coin is heads},\\
> 0, & \text{if the $i$th coin is tails}.
> \end{cases}
> $$
>
> For each coin,
> $$
> E[X_i] = 1\cdot \tfrac{1}{2} + 0\cdot \tfrac{1}{2} = \tfrac{1}{2}.
> $$
>
> By linearity of expectation,
> $$
> E[X] = E[X_1] + E[X_2] + E[X_3] + E[X_4]
> = 4 \cdot \tfrac{1}{2}
> = 2.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected number of heads is
> $$
> \boxed{2}.
> $$
