> [!example] Question 450 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

How many handshakes occur in a group of 25 people if each person shakes hands with every other person exactly once?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=451)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Each handshake is determined by choosing a pair of people from the $25$ people.
>
> Thus the number of handshakes is the number of unordered pairs:
>
> $$
> \binom{25}{2}=\frac{25 \cdot 24}{2}=300.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the group can have **$300$ handshakes**.
