> [!example] Question 543 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

If two dice are rolled, what is the probability that the sum is greater than 7?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=544)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> When two fair six-sided dice are rolled, there are
>
> $$
> 6 \times 6 = 36
> $$
>
> equally likely outcomes.
>
> To have a sum greater than $7$, the possible sums are
>
> $$
> 8, 9, 10, 11, 12.
> $$
>
> Count the outcomes for each sum:
>
> - Sum $8$: $(2,6), (3,5), (4,4), (5,3), (6,2)$ $\rightarrow 5$ outcomes
> - Sum $9$: $(3,6), (4,5), (5,4), (6,3)$ $\rightarrow 4$ outcomes
> - Sum $10$: $(4,6), (5,5), (6,4)$ $\rightarrow 3$ outcomes
> - Sum $11$: $(5,6), (6,5)$ $\rightarrow 2$ outcomes
> - Sum $12$: $(6,6)$ $\rightarrow 1$ outcome
>
> Total favorable outcomes:
>
> $$
> 5+4+3+2+1=15.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability is
>
> $$
> \frac{15}{36}=\frac{5}{12}.
> $$
>
> Thus, the probability that the sum is greater than $7$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{12}}.
> $$
