> [!example] Question 153 · Jane Street
> ## Intern Interview

What is the final digit of 17 raised to the 17th power (17^17)?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=154)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the final digit of $17^{17}$, only the final digit of the base matters. Since $17$ ends in $7$, we need the final digit of $7^{17}$.
>
> The final digits of powers of $7$ repeat in a cycle of length $4$:
>
> $$
> 7^1 \to 7,\quad
> 7^2 \to 9,\quad
> 7^3 \to 3,\quad
> 7^4 \to 1
> $$
>
> Then the pattern repeats:
> $$
> 7,\ 9,\ 3,\ 1,\ 7,\ 9,\ 3,\ 1,\dots
> $$
>
> Now compute $17 \bmod 4$:
>
> $$
> 17 \equiv 1 \pmod{4}
> $$
>
> So $7^{17}$ has the same final digit as $7^1$, which is
>
> $$
> \boxed{7}
> $$
