> [!example] Question 503 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

What is the probability of getting at least 4 heads in 7 coin flips?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=504)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Assume the coin is fair, so each flip has probability $\tfrac12$ of landing heads.
>
> Let $X$ be the number of heads in $7$ flips. Then
>
> $$
> X \sim \operatorname{Binomial}(7,\tfrac12).
> $$
>
> We want
>
> $$
> P(X \ge 4).
> $$
>
> Using the binomial formula,
>
> $$
> P(X \ge 4)=\sum_{k=4}^{7} \binom{7}{k}\left(\tfrac12\right)^7.
> $$
>
> Compute the relevant binomial coefficients:
>
> $$
> \binom{7}{4}=35,\quad \binom{7}{5}=21,\quad \binom{7}{6}=7,\quad \binom{7}{7}=1.
> $$
>
> So
>
> $$
> P(X \ge 4)=\frac{35+21+7+1}{2^7}
> =\frac{64}{128}
> =\frac12.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability of getting at least $4$ heads in $7$ coin flips is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac12}.
> $$
>
> In decimal form, this is
>
> $$
> \boxed{0.5}.
> $$
