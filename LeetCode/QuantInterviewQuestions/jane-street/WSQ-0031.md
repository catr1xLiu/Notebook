> [!example] Question 31 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

What is the sum of the odd numbers from 1 to 60?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=32)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The odd numbers from $1$ to $60$ are
>
> $$
> 1, 3, 5, \dots, 59.
> $$
>
> This is an arithmetic sequence with first term $a_1 = 1$, last term $a_n = 59$, and common difference $d = 2$.
>
> First find the number of terms:
>
> $$
> n = \frac{59 - 1}{2} + 1 = 30.
> $$
>
> Now use the sum formula for an arithmetic sequence:
>
> $$
> S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n).
> $$
>
> Substitute the values:
>
> $$
> S_{30} = \frac{30}{2}(1 + 59) = 15 \cdot 60 = 900.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the sum of the odd numbers from $1$ to $60$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{900}.
> $$
