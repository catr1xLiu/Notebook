> [!example] Question 602 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is the expected value of the product when rolling a six-sided die and an eight-sided die?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=603)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the outcome of a fair six-sided die and $Y$ the outcome of a fair eight-sided die. We seek
>
> $$
> E[XY].
> $$
>
> Since the dice are independent,
>
> $$
> E[XY] = E[X]E[Y].
> $$
>
> Compute each expected value:
>
> For the six-sided die,
>
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6} = \frac{21}{6} = \frac{7}{2}.
> $$
>
> For the eight-sided die,
>
> $$
> E[Y] = \frac{1+2+3+4+5+6+7+8}{8} = \frac{36}{8} = \frac{9}{2}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> E[XY] = \frac{7}{2}\cdot\frac{9}{2} = \frac{63}{4}.
> $$
>
> Thus, the expected value of the product is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{63}{4}}.
> $$
