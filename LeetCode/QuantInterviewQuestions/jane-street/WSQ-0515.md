> [!example] Question 515 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

How many numbers from 0 to 1000 contain at least one digit 2?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=516)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Count the integers from $0$ to $1000$ that contain at least one digit $2$.
>
> A convenient method is to count the complement: first count how many do **not** contain any $2$, then subtract from the total.
>
> ## Step 1: Represent the numbers uniformly
>
> Consider all integers from $0$ to $999$ written as $3$-digit strings, allowing leading zeros:
>
> $$
> 000,001,002,\dots,999
> $$
>
> There are
>
> $$
> 1000
> $$
>
> such strings.
>
> For each of the $3$ digit positions, any digit except $2$ is allowed if the number contains no $2$. Thus each position has
>
> $$
> 9
> $$
>
> choices.
>
> So the number of integers from $0$ to $999$ containing no $2$ is
>
> $$
> 9^3 = 729.
> $$
>
> Hence the number from $0$ to $999$ containing at least one $2$ is
>
> $$
> 1000 - 729 = 271.
> $$
>
> ## Step 2: Check the number $1000$
>
> The number $1000$ does not contain a $2$, so it does not increase the count.
>
> Therefore, among the integers from $0$ to $1000$, the number containing at least one $2$ is
>
> $$
> 271.
> $$
>
> ## Final Answer
>
> $$
> \boxed{271}
> $$
