> [!example] Question 331 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

If I flip four coins and give you a dollar for every head, what is the expected value of this game?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=332)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the number of heads obtained when four fair coins are flipped. The payoff is $X$ dollars, so the expected value of the game is $E[X]$.
>
> Each coin has probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$ of landing heads. Write
> $$
> X = X_1 + X_2 + X_3 + X_4,
> $$
> where
> $$
> X_i =
> \begin{cases}
> 1, & \text{if coin } i \text{ is heads},\\
> 0, & \text{if coin } i \text{ is tails}.
> \end{cases}
> $$
>
> Then for each coin,
> $$
> E[X_i] = 1 \cdot \frac{1}{2} + 0 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{2}.
> $$
>
> By linearity of expectation,
> $$
> E[X] = E[X_1] + E[X_2] + E[X_3] + E[X_4]
> = 4 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 2.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected value of the game is
>
> $$
> \boxed{2 \text{ dollars}}.
> $$
