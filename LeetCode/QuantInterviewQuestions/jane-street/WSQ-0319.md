> [!example] Question 319 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is the expected value of a game where you roll a fair die and receive the number shown in dollars?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=320)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The value of the game is its expected payoff.
>
> Let $X$ be the number showing on a fair six-sided die. Then $X$ can take the values
>
> $$
> 1,2,3,4,5,6
> $$
>
> each with probability
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{6}.
> $$
>
> So the expected value is
>
> $$
> E[X] = \sum_{x=1}^{6} x \cdot \frac{1}{6}
> = \frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6}
> = \frac{21}{6}
> = 3.5.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the predicted value of the game is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\$3.50}.
> $$
