> [!example] Question 152 · Jane Street
> ## Intern Interview

What is the expected waiting time to get three consecutive heads when flipping a fair coin repeatedly?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=153)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $E$ be the expected number of tosses needed to obtain three consecutive heads.
>
> A standard way to solve this is to introduce states according to the current run of consecutive heads:
>
> - $E_0$: expected additional tosses starting with no current consecutive heads,
> - $E_1$: expected additional tosses given that the current run is exactly one head,
> - $E_2$: expected additional tosses given that the current run is exactly two heads.
>
> We want $E_0$.
>
> Assume the coin is fair, so each toss is heads with probability $\tfrac12$ and tails with probability $\tfrac12$.
>
> ## Step 1: Set up the recurrences
>
> From state $E_0$:
>
> - one toss is used,
> - with probability $\tfrac12$ a head occurs, moving to state $E_1$,
> - with probability $\tfrac12$ a tail occurs, remaining in state $E_0$.
>
> Thus,
> $$
> E_0 = 1 + \frac12 E_1 + \frac12 E_0.
> $$
>
> From state $E_1$:
>
> - one toss is used,
> - with probability $\tfrac12$ a head occurs, moving to state $E_2$,
> - with probability $\tfrac12$ a tail occurs, resetting to state $E_0$.
>
> So,
> $$
> E_1 = 1 + \frac12 E_2 + \frac12 E_0.
> $$
>
> From state $E_2$:
>
> - one toss is used,
> - with probability $\tfrac12$ a head occurs, and the process ends,
> - with probability $\tfrac12$ a tail occurs, resetting to state $E_0$.
>
> Hence,
> $$
> E_2 = 1 + \frac12 \cdot 0 + \frac12 E_0
> = 1 + \frac12 E_0.
> $$
>
> ## Step 2: Solve the system
>
> First equation:
> $$
> E_0 = 1 + \frac12 E_1 + \frac12 E_0
> \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
> \frac12 E_0 = 1 + \frac12 E_1
> \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
> E_0 = 2 + E_1.
> $$
>
> Second equation:
> $$
> E_1 = 1 + \frac12 E_2 + \frac12 E_0.
> $$
>
> Substitute $E_2 = 1 + \frac12 E_0$:
> $$
> E_1 = 1 + \frac12\left(1 + \frac12 E_0\right) + \frac12 E_0
> = 1 + \frac12 + \frac14 E_0 + \frac12 E_0
> = \frac32 + \frac34 E_0.
> $$
>
> Now use $E_0 = 2 + E_1$:
> $$
> E_0 = 2 + \frac32 + \frac34 E_0
> = \frac72 + \frac34 E_0.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
> $$
> E_0 - \frac34 E_0 = \frac72
> \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
> \frac14 E_0 = \frac72
> \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
> E_0 = 14.
> $$
>
> ## Conclusion
>
> The expected waiting time to obtain three consecutive heads is
>
> $$
> \boxed{14}
> $$
>
> tosses.
