> [!example] Question 624 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

There are 4 coins. You earn a dollar for every head. All four coins are flipped. What is the expected payout? Additionally, if you have the option to either keep the result of the first flips or reflip all four coins, what is the optimal expected payout?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=625)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the number of heads obtained when $4$ fair coins are flipped. Since each head pays $\text{\textdollar}1$, the payoff equals $X$ dollars.
>
> ## 1. Outcome when all four coins are flipped once
>
> The random variable $X$ has a binomial distribution:
>
> $$
> X \sim \operatorname{Binomial}(4,\tfrac12).
> $$
>
> Thus
>
> $$
> P(X=k)=\binom{4}{k}\left(\tfrac12\right)^4=\frac{\binom{4}{k}}{16}, \qquad k=0,1,2,3,4.
> $$
>
> So the possible outcomes are:
>
> \[
> \begin{array}{c|ccccc}
> k & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 \\
> \hline
> P(X=k) & \frac{1}{16} & \frac{4}{16} & \frac{6}{16} & \frac{4}{16} & \frac{1}{16}
> \end{array}
> \]
>
> The expected payoff is
>
> $$
> E[X]=4\cdot \tfrac12=2.
> $$
>
> Hence, if the coins are simply flipped once, the expected outcome is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\$2}.
> $$
>
> ## 2. Outcome when you may keep the first result or reflip all four coins
>
> Let $X$ be the number of heads on the first set of $4$ flips, and let $Y$ be the number of heads on the second set if you choose to reflip. The second set is independent and has the same distribution, so
>
> $$
> E[Y]=2.
> $$
>
> To maximize expected payoff, compare the certain value of keeping $X$ with the expected value of rerolling, which is $2$.
>
> - If $X \ge 2$, keeping is at least as good as rerolling.
> - If $X < 2$, rerolling is better.
>
> Thus an optimal strategy is:
>
> - reflip if $X=0$ or $X=1$;
> - keep if $X=2,3,4$.
>
> Now compute the expected value under this strategy:
>
> $$
> E[\text{payoff}]
> =0\cdot P(X=0)\text{? No, if }X=0\text{ we reroll, so payoff }E[Y]=2,
> $$
>
> and similarly for $X=1$. Therefore
>
> $$
> E[\text{payoff}]
> =2\bigl(P(X=0)+P(X=1)\bigr)+2P(X=2)+3P(X=3)+4P(X=4).
> $$
>
> Substitute the probabilities:
>
> $$
> E[\text{payoff}]
> =2\left(\frac{1}{16}+\frac{4}{16}\right)
> +2\left(\frac{6}{16}\right)
> +3\left(\frac{4}{16}\right)
> +4\left(\frac{1}{16}\right).
> $$
>
> Simplifying,
>
> $$
> E[\text{payoff}]
> =\frac{10}{16}+\frac{12}{16}+\frac{12}{16}+\frac{4}{16}
> =\frac{38}{16}
> =\frac{19}{8}.
> $$
>
> So the optimal expected payoff is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{19}{8}=\$2.375}.
> $$
>
> ## Final answer
>
> - If all four coins are flipped once, the expected outcome is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\$2}.
> $$
>
> - If you may keep the first result or reflip all four coins, the optimal rule is to reflip when the first result has $0$ or $1$ head, and keep otherwise. The expected outcome is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{19}{8}=\$2.375}.
> $$
