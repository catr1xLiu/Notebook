> [!example] Question 325 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What day of the week is 180 days from today? What is the probability that it will rain on the weekend if there is a 50% chance it will rain on Saturday and a 60% chance for Sunday?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=326)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To determine the day of the week $180$ days from today, reduce $180$ modulo $7$:
>
> $$
> 180 \equiv 5 \pmod{7}.
> $$
>
> So $180$ days from today is the day that is $5$ weekdays after today.
>
> Using today’s date, May $1$, $2026$, which is a Friday, counting forward $5$ days gives:
>
> - Saturday: $1$
> - Sunday: $2$
> - Monday: $3$
> - Tuesday: $4$
> - Wednesday: $5$
>
> Hence, $180$ days from today is **Wednesday**.
>
> For the probability that it will rain on the weekend, interpret this as the probability that it rains on **at least one** of Saturday or Sunday. Let
>
> - $P(S) = 0.5$
> - $P(U) = 0.6$
>
> Assuming the rain events on Saturday and Sunday are independent,
>
> $$
> P(\text{rain on weekend}) = P(S \cup U).
> $$
>
> Using the addition rule,
>
> $$
> P(S \cup U) = P(S) + P(U) - P(S \cap U).
> $$
>
> By independence,
>
> $$
> P(S \cap U) = 0.5 \cdot 0.6 = 0.3.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> P(S \cup U) = 0.5 + 0.6 - 0.3 = 0.8.
> $$
>
> So the probability that it will rain on the weekend is **$0.8$**, or **$80\%$**.
>
> ## Final Answer
>
> - Day of the week $180$ days from today: **Wednesday**
> - Probability it rains on the weekend: **$80\%$**
