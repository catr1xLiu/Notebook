What is one million minus one hundred eleven?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=169)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute
> $$1{,}000{,}000 - 111.$$
>
> Subtract in parts:
> $$1{,}000{,}000 - 100 = 999{,}900,$$
> then
> $$999{,}900 - 11 = 999{,}889.$$
>
> Therefore, the result is
> $$999{,}889.$$
