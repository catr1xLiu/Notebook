> [!example] Question 382 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

If I write down all the numbers from 1 to 1,000,000 on a page, how many times do I write the digit 2?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=383)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Count how many times the digit $2$ appears in all integers from $1$ to $1{,}000{,}000$.
>
> ## Step 1: Count from $0$ to $999{,}999$
>
> Consider all $6$-digit strings from $000000$ to $999999$. There are $1{,}000{,}000$ such strings.
>
> In each of the $6$ digit positions:
>
> - each digit $0,1,2,\dots,9$ appears equally often,
> - so each digit appears $\frac{1{,}000{,}000}{10} = 100{,}000$ times in that position.
>
> Therefore, the digit $2$ appears
>
> $$
> 6 \cdot 100{,}000 = 600{,}000
> $$
>
> times among all numbers from $000000$ to $999999$.
>
> ## Step 2: Convert to the range $1$ to $1{,}000{,}000$
>
> Removing $000000$ does not change the count, since it contains no $2$'s.
>
> Adding $1{,}000{,}000$ also does not change the count, since it contains no $2$'s.
>
> Hence the total number of times the digit $2$ is written from $1$ to $1{,}000{,}000$ is
>
> $$
> 600{,}000.
> $$
>
> ## Final Answer
>
> $$
> \boxed{600{,}000}
> $$
