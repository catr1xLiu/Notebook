> [!example] Question 128 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Intern Interview

With dates written in DD/MM/YYYY format, what is the next date where no digit is repeated?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=129)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Write dates in the form $\text{DD/MM/YYYY}$ and require that the $8$ digits used are all distinct.
>
> We seek the first date after today, where today is $01/05/2026$.
>
> ## Step 1: Eliminate the current year
>
> Any date in $2026$ has year digits $2,0,2,6$, which already repeats the digit $2$.  
> So no date in $2026$ can qualify.
>
> ## Step 2: Check the next years
>
> A valid year must itself have four distinct digits, since all eight date digits must be distinct.
>
> - $2027$: repeats $2$
> - $2028$: repeats $2$
> - $2029$: repeats $2$
> - $2030$: repeats $0$
> - $2031$: repeats $0$
> - $2032$: repeats $2$
> - $2033$: repeats $3$
> - $2034$: digits are $2,0,3,4$, all distinct
>
> Thus the earliest possible year is $2034$.
>
> ## Step 3: Find the earliest valid date in $2034$
>
> The year $2034$ uses digits $\{2,0,3,4\}$.  
> So the day and month together must use four distinct digits, none of which are $2,0,3,4$.
>
> Available digits are therefore $\{1,5,6,7,8,9\}$.
>
> We now search for the earliest valid month and day.
>
> ### Month choice
>
> The earliest month whose two digits are distinct and avoid $\{2,0,3,4\}$ is:
>
> - $01$: contains $0$ not allowed
> - $02$: contains $0,2$ not allowed
> - $03$: contains $0,3$ not allowed
> - $04$: contains $0,4$ not allowed
> - $05$: contains $0$ not allowed
> - $06$: contains $0$ not allowed
> - $07$: contains $0$ not allowed
> - $08$: contains $0$ not allowed
> - $09$: contains $0$ not allowed
> - $10$: contains $0$ not allowed
> - $11$: repeats $1$
> - $12$: contains $2$ not allowed
>
> So no month in $2034$ works if interpreted this way? Let us check more carefully: every month from $01$ to $09$ contains $0$, and since $0$ is already in the year, those are invalid. Months $10$, $11$, and $12$ also fail as noted.
>
> Hence there is **no** valid date in $2034$.
>
> ## Step 4: Continue to the next year with four distinct digits
>
> Check subsequent years:
>
> - $2035$: digits $\{2,0,3,5\}$ distinct
> - $2036$: digits $\{2,0,3,6\}$ distinct
> - $2037$: digits $\{2,0,3,7\}$ distinct
> - $2038$: digits $\{2,0,3,8\}$ distinct
> - $2039$: digits $\{2,0,3,9\}$ distinct
>
> But all these years still contain $0$, so months $01$–$09$ are impossible, and months $10$–$12$ use $0$, $1$, or $2$ in conflicting ways. Check $11$ in particular: it repeats $1$, so impossible. Thus none of $2035$ through $2039$ works.
>
> Next:
>
> - $2040$: repeats $0$
> - $2041$: repeats $4$
> - $2042$: repeats $2$
> - $2043$: repeats $4$
> - $2044$: repeats $4$
> - $2045$: digits $\{2,0,4,5\}$ distinct
>
> Again the year contains $0$, so the same month obstruction applies. Therefore no year from $2034$ through any year in the $2000$s can work, because every such year begins with $20\ldots$ and therefore contains $0$, making every month impossible:
> - $01$–$09$ contain $0$,
> - $10$ and $12$ repeat digits already in the year,
> - $11$ repeats $1$ within the month.
>
> So no valid date exists in any year $2000$–$2099$.
>
> ## Step 5: Move to the next century
>
> Consider years in the $2100$s. A valid year must have four distinct digits.
>
> The earliest such year is $2130$, but it repeats $0$? Its digits are $2,1,3,0$, all distinct. However the month must avoid these digits.
>
> Try the earliest valid year systematically:
>
> - $2103$: digits $2,1,0,3$ distinct, but again year contains $0$.
> In fact every year from $2100$ to $2199$ contains digits $2,1$ and at least one more pair; importantly, every year contains either repeated digits or $0$. The presence of $0$ again blocks months $01$–$10$, and $11$, $12$ fail because of repetition or conflict.
>
> Thus no valid date exists in the $2100$s either.
>
> ## Step 6: Find the first year with no $0$ and four distinct digits
>
> The earliest year after $2026$ with four distinct digits and no $0$ is $2134$.
>
> Its digits are $\{2,1,3,4\}$.
>
> Now choose the earliest valid month:
> - $01$ uses $1$ and $0$; invalid
> - $02$ uses $2$; invalid
> - $03$ uses $3$; invalid
> - $04$ uses $4$; invalid
> - $05$ uses $0$; invalid
> - $06$ uses $0$; invalid
> - $07$ uses $0$; invalid
> - $08$ uses $0$; invalid
> - $09$ uses $0$; invalid
> - $10$ uses $1$ and $0$; invalid
> - $11$ repeats $1$
> - $12$ uses $1,2$; invalid
>
> Again impossible, because every $2$-digit month from $01$ to $12$ necessarily uses either $0$, $1$, or $2$, all already present in the year.
>
> Therefore $2134$ does not work.
>
> The same issue applies to any year beginning with $21\ldots$: all months use $0$, $1$, or $2$, so they conflict.
>
> ## Step 7: Earliest workable century
>
> To allow a month at all, the year must avoid the digits that appear unavoidably in early months.  
> The earliest month with two distinct digits and no $0$, $1$, or $2$ is impossible, since months only go up to $12$.
>
> So the year must leave available at least one valid month among:
> $$01,02,03,04,05,06,07,08,09,10,11,12.$$
>
> The only months with distinct digits are
> $$01,02,03,04,05,06,07,08,09,10,12.$$
> Thus the year must avoid the two digits of one of these months.
>
> The earliest year after $2026$ with four distinct digits and disjoint from some valid month is $2345$.
>
> Indeed, year digits are $\{2,3,4,5\}$.  
> The earliest month disjoint from these is $06$.
>
> Then the remaining digits available for the day are $\{1,7,8,9\}$ together with possibly $0$? No, $0$ is used in month $06$, so unavailable.
>
> The earliest valid day is $17$, and it is a valid day of June.
>
> Thus the date is
> $$17/06/2345.$$
>
> All digits in $17/06/2345$ are
> $$1,7,0,6,2,3,4,5,$$
> which are all distinct.
>
> ## Final answer
>
> $$\boxed{17/06/2345}$$
