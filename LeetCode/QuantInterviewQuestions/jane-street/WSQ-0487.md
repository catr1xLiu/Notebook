> [!example] Question 487 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

Decompose 300,000 into its prime factors. Then, arrange all the digits from these prime factors to form the smallest possible number.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=488)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> First factor $300{,}000$ into primes:
> $$
> 300{,}000 = 3 \times 100{,}000 = 3 \times (10^5) = 3 \times (2 \times 5)^5 = 2^5 \times 3 \times 5^5.
> $$
>
> Thus the prime factorization is
> $$
> 300{,}000 = 2^5 \cdot 3 \cdot 5^5.
> $$
>
> Writing out the prime factors explicitly gives the digits:
> $$
> 2,2,2,2,2,3,5,5,5,5,5.
> $$
>
> To form the smallest possible number from these digits, arrange them in increasing order:
> $$
> 22222355555.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the required number is
> $$
> \boxed{22222355555}.
> $$
