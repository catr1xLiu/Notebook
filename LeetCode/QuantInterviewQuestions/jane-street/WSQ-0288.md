> [!example] Question 288 · Jane Street
> ## Quant Trader Interview

How many people do you need to have in a room before you can be certain that at least 5 of them were born in the same month?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=289)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Apply the pigeonhole principle.
>
> There are $12$ months, which serve as the $12$ “boxes,” and each person is placed into the box corresponding to their birth month.
>
> To avoid having $5$ people in the same month for as long as possible, you could place at most $4$ people in each month. That gives
>
> $$
> 12 \cdot 4 = 48
> $$
>
> people without forcing any month to contain $5$ births.
>
> Therefore, the next person guarantees that some month has at least $5$ people born in it. Thus the required number is
>
> $$
> 48 + 1 = 49.
> $$
>
> So, the minimum number of people needed is
>
> $$
> \boxed{49}.
> $$
