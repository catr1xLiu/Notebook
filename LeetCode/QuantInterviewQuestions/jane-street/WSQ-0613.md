> [!example] Question 613 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is 29 multiplied by 33? Solve it without using pen and paper.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=614)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute mentally using distribution:
>
> $$29 \cdot 33 = (30 - 1)\cdot 33 = 30\cdot 33 - 1\cdot 33 = 990 - 33 = 957.$$
>
> Thus, the product is
>
> $$957.$$
