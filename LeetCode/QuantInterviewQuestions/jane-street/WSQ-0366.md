> [!example] Question 366 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

How many digits does 99 raised to the 99th power have?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=367)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the number of digits of $99^{99}$, use the fact that a positive integer $N$ has
>
> $$
> \lfloor \log_{10} N \rfloor + 1
> $$
>
> digits.
>
> Here,
>
> $$
> N = 99^{99}.
> $$
>
> So the number of digits is
>
> $$
> \left\lfloor \log_{10}(99^{99}) \right\rfloor + 1
> = \left\lfloor 99 \log_{10}(99) \right\rfloor + 1.
> $$
>
> Now compute:
>
> $$
> \log_{10}(99) = \log_{10}(9.9 \cdot 10) = 1 + \log_{10}(9.9) \approx 1.995635.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 99 \log_{10}(99) \approx 99 \cdot 1.995635 = 197.567865.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> \left\lfloor 197.567865 \right\rfloor + 1 = 197 + 1 = 198.
> $$
>
> Hence, $99^{99}$ has
>
> $$
> \boxed{198}
> $$
>
> digits.
