> [!example] Question 447 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

You have a fair coin and continually toss it. What is the expected number of tosses needed to obtain the sequence TTT (three tails in a row)?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=448)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $E$ denote the expected number of tosses needed to obtain the pattern $TTT$ for the first time.
>
> The key idea is to condition on the current relevant suffix of recent tosses. Define:
>
> - $E_0$: expected remaining tosses when no trailing $T$'s have been matched,
> - $E_1$: expected remaining tosses given that the current suffix is $T$,
> - $E_2$: expected remaining tosses given that the current suffix is $TT$.
>
> We seek $E_0$.
>
> ## Step 1: Set up the equations
>
> From state $E_0$:
>
> - One toss is made.
> - With probability $\tfrac12$, we get $H$, so we remain in state $E_0$.
> - With probability $\tfrac12$, we get $T$, so we move to state $E_1$.
>
> Thus,
> $$
> E_0 = 1 + \frac12 E_0 + \frac12 E_1.
> $$
>
> From state $E_1$:
>
> - One toss is made.
> - With probability $\tfrac12$, we get $H$, which breaks the run, so we return to $E_0$.
> - With probability $\tfrac12$, we get $T$, so we move to $E_2$.
>
> Thus,
> $$
> E_1 = 1 + \frac12 E_0 + \frac12 E_2.
> $$
>
> From state $E_2$:
>
> - One toss is made.
> - With probability $\tfrac12$, we get $T$, completing $TTT$, so no further tosses are needed.
> - With probability $\tfrac12$, we get $H$, so the run is broken and we return to $E_0$.
>
> Thus,
> $$
> E_2 = 1 + \frac12 \cdot 0 + \frac12 E_0
> = 1 + \frac12 E_0.
> $$
>
> ## Step 2: Solve the system
>
> From
> $$
> E_0 = 1 + \frac12 E_0 + \frac12 E_1,
> $$
> we get
> $$
> \frac12 E_0 = 1 + \frac12 E_1
> \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
> E_0 = 2 + E_1.
> $$
>
> From
> $$
> E_2 = 1 + \frac12 E_0.
> $$
>
> Substitute into the equation for $E_1$:
> $$
> E_1 = 1 + \frac12 E_0 + \frac12 E_2
> = 1 + \frac12 E_0 + \frac12\left(1 + \frac12 E_0\right).
> $$
> So
> $$
> E_1 = 1 + \frac12 E_0 + \frac12 + \frac14 E_0
> = \frac32 + \frac34 E_0.
> $$
>
> Now use $E_0 = 2 + E_1$:
> $$
> E_0 = 2 + \frac32 + \frac34 E_0
> = \frac72 + \frac34 E_0.
> $$
> Hence,
> $$
> E_0 - \frac34 E_0 = \frac72
> \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
> \frac14 E_0 = \frac72
> \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
> E_0 = 14.
> $$
>
> ## Final answer
>
> The expected number of tosses needed to get the sequence $TTT$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{14}.
> $$
