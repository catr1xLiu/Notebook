> [!example] Question 615 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

If I have a 4x4x4 cube and I paint the outside of the cube, then cut it into 64 1x1x1 cubes: 1) How many of the smaller cubes have at least one side painted? 2) If I pick one of the 64 cubes at random and roll it, what is the probability that the top side is painted?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=616)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Consider a $4 \times 4 \times 4$ cube made of $64$ unit cubes.
>
> When the outside is painted, only the cubes on the surface receive paint.
>
> ## 1) Number of unit cubes with at least one side painted
>
> First count the cubes with **no** paint: these are the cubes completely inside the large cube.
>
> The interior dimensions are found by removing the outer layer from each side:
>
> $$
> (4-2)\times(4-2)\times(4-2)=2\times2\times2=8
> $$
>
> So $8$ cubes are unpainted.
>
> Since there are $64$ cubes total, the number with at least one painted side is
>
> $$
> 64-8=56
> $$
>
> Thus, the number of cubes with at least one side painted is
>
> $$
> \boxed{56}
> $$
>
> ## 2) Probability that the top side is painted after picking a random cube and rolling it
>
> Choose one of the $64$ unit cubes uniformly at random, then roll it so that each of its $6$ faces is equally likely to end up on top.
>
> A cube with:
>
> - $0$ painted faces contributes probability $0$
> - $1$ painted face contributes probability $\frac{1}{6}$
> - $2$ painted faces contributes probability $\frac{2}{6}$
> - $3$ painted faces contributes probability $\frac{3}{6}$
>
> So we count how many cubes have $1$, $2$, and $3$ painted faces.
>
> ### Cubes with $3$ painted faces
> These are the corner cubes:
>
> $$
> 8
> $$
>
> ### Cubes with $2$ painted faces
> These are edge cubes excluding corners. A cube has $12$ edges, and each edge contributes $4-2=2$ such cubes:
>
> $$
> 12 \cdot 2 = 24
> $$
>
> ### Cubes with $1$ painted face
> These are face-center cubes, excluding edges. Each face has
>
> $$
> (4-2)^2 = 2^2 = 4
> $$
>
> such cubes, and there are $6$ faces:
>
> $$
> 6 \cdot 4 = 24
> $$
>
> ### Compute the probability
> Total probability:
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{64}\left(24\cdot\frac{1}{6} + 24\cdot\frac{2}{6} + 8\cdot\frac{3}{6}\right)
> $$
>
> Simplify:
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{64}\left(\frac{24+48+24}{6}\right)
> =
> \frac{1}{64}\left(\frac{96}{6}\right)
> =
> \frac{16}{64}
> =
> \frac{1}{4}
> $$
>
> Thus, the probability that the top side is painted is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{4}}
> $$
>
> ## Final Answers
>
> $$
> \boxed{56}
> \qquad\text{and}\qquad
> \boxed{\frac{1}{4}}
> $$
