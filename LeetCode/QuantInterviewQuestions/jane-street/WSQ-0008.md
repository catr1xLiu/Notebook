> [!example] Question 8 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

You toss 4 fair coins. You earn one dollar for each head. Compute the expected payoff.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=9)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the total payoff from tossing $4$ fair coins, where each head pays $\text{\textdollar}1$.
>
> Define indicator variables:
> - $X_1 = 1$ if the first coin is a head, and $0$ otherwise,
> - $X_2, X_3, X_4$ similarly.
>
> Then
> $$
> X = X_1 + X_2 + X_3 + X_4.
> $$
>
> Since each coin is fair,
> $$
> P(\text{head}) = \frac{1}{2},
> $$
> so for each $i$,
> $$
> E[X_i] = 1 \cdot \frac{1}{2} + 0 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{2}.
> $$
>
> By linearity of expectation,
> $$
> E[X] = E[X_1] + E[X_2] + E[X_3] + E[X_4]
> = 4 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 2.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected payoff is
>
> $$
> \boxed{2 \text{ dollars}}.
> $$
