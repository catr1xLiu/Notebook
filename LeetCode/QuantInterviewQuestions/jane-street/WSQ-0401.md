> [!example] Question 401 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is 37 times 37?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=402)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To compute $37 \times 37$, use expansion:
>
> $$
> 37 \times 37 = (30+7)(30+7)
> $$
>
> Apply the distributive property:
>
> $$
> (30+7)(30+7)=30^2+2\cdot 30 \cdot 7+7^2
> $$
>
> $$
> =900+420+49
> $$
>
> $$
> =1369
> $$
>
> Thus, the result is
>
> $$
> \boxed{1369}
> $$
