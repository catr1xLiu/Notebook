> [!example] Question 329 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

If 1.5 chickens lay 1.5 eggs in 1.5 days, how many eggs will 9 chickens lay in 9 days?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=330)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Use direct proportionality.
>
> If $1.5$ chickens lay $1.5$ eggs in $1.5$ days, then the rate per chicken per day is
>
> $$
> \frac{1.5\ \text{eggs}}{1.5\ \text{chickens} \times 1.5\ \text{days}}
> = \frac{1.5}{2.25}
> = \frac{2}{3}
> $$
>
> So each chicken lays $\frac{2}{3}$ of an egg per day.
>
> For $9$ chickens in $9$ days, the total number of eggs is
>
> $$
> 9 \times 9 \times \frac{2}{3} = 81 \times \frac{2}{3} = 54
> $$
>
> Therefore, the answer is
>
> $$
> \boxed{54\ \text{eggs}}
> $$
