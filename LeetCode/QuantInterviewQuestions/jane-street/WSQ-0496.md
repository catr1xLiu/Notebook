> [!example] Question 496 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

What is the result of multiplying 17 by 3.3?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=497)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute directly:
> $$
> 17 \times 3.3 = 17 \times \frac{33}{10} = \frac{561}{10} = 56.1
> $$
>
> Thus, the answer is
> $$
> 56.1
> $$
