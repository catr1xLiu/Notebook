> [!example] Question 418 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

I roll a die and will give you an amount in pounds equal to the number on the die. For example, if I get 5, I will give you 5 pounds. What is the price you are willing to pay for this game?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=419)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the payoff of the game, where $X$ is the number shown on a fair six-sided die. Then $X$ takes values
>
> $$
> 1,2,3,4,5,6
> $$
>
> each with probability $\frac{1}{6}$.
>
> The fair price to pay is the expected value of the payoff:
>
> $$
> E[X] = \sum_{x=1}^{6} x \cdot \frac{1}{6}
> = \frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6}
> = \frac{21}{6}
> = 3.5.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the price one should be willing to pay for this game is
>
> $$
> \boxed{£3.50}.
> $$
