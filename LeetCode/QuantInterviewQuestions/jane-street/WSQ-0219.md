> [!example] Question 219 · Jane Street
> ## Internship Interview

What is the probability of getting exactly 2 heads in 4 coin tosses?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=220)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> For $4$ fair coin flips, the total number of equally likely outcomes is
>
> $$
> 2^4 = 16.
> $$
>
> To get exactly $2$ heads, choose which $2$ of the $4$ flips are heads:
>
> $$
> \binom{4}{2} = 6.
> $$
>
> Thus, the probability is
>
> $$
> \frac{\binom{4}{2}}{2^4}=\frac{6}{16}=\frac{3}{8}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability of getting exactly $2$ heads in $4$ coin tosses is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{3}{8}}.
> $$
