> [!example] Question 403 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is 38 squared?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=404)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To compute $38^2$, write
>
> $$
> 38^2 = 38 \cdot 38.
> $$
>
> Use expansion:
>
> $$
> 38 \cdot 38 = (40 - 2)^2 = 40^2 - 2 \cdot 40 \cdot 2 + 2^2.
> $$
>
> Now evaluate:
>
> $$
> 40^2 = 1600, \quad 2 \cdot 40 \cdot 2 = 160, \quad 2^2 = 4.
> $$
>
> So,
>
> $$
> 38^2 = 1600 - 160 + 4 = 1444.
> $$
>
> Thus, the answer is
>
> $$
> \boxed{1444}.
> $$
