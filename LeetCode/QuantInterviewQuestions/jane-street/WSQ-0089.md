> [!example] Question 89 · Jane Street
> ## Trading Interview

Find the angle between the hour and minute hands of a clock at 9:30. Also, what is the probability that the World Series goes to the 7th game?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=90)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> ## 1. Angle between the clock hands at $9{:}30$
>
> At $9{:}00$, the hour hand is at the $9$, which is
>
> $$
> 9 \times 30^\circ = 270^\circ
> $$
>
> from $12$ o’clock.
>
> By $9{:}30$, the minute hand is at the $6$, which is
>
> $$
> 180^\circ
> $$
>
> from $12$ o’clock.
>
> However, the hour hand does not stay fixed at the $9$; in $30$ minutes it moves halfway toward the $10$. Since each hour mark is $30^\circ$, in half an hour it moves
>
> $$
> 15^\circ.
> $$
>
> So at $9{:}30$, the hour hand is at
>
> $$
> 270^\circ + 15^\circ = 285^\circ.
> $$
>
> The angle between the two hands is
>
> $$
> |285^\circ - 180^\circ| = 105^\circ.
> $$
>
> Thus the smaller angle is
>
> $$
> \boxed{105^\circ}.
> $$
>
> ---
>
> ## 2. Probability that the World Series goes to a $7$th game
>
> A best-of-$7$ series reaches game $7$ exactly when, after $6$ games, each team has won $3$ games.
>
> Assume the two teams are evenly matched, so each game is independent and each team has probability
>
> $$
> \frac12
> $$
>
> of winning any game.
>
> Then among the first $6$ games, we need exactly $3$ wins for one team and $3$ wins for the other. The number of such win-loss sequences is
>
> $$
> \binom{6}{3} = 20.
> $$
>
> Each particular sequence has probability
>
> $$
> \left(\frac12\right)^6 = \frac{1}{64}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability the series reaches game $7$ is
>
> $$
> \binom{6}{3}\left(\frac12\right)^6
> = 20 \cdot \frac{1}{64}
> = \frac{20}{64}
> = \frac{5}{16}.
> $$
>
> So the probability is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{16}}
> $$
>
> which is
>
> $$
> \boxed{31.25\%}.
> $$
