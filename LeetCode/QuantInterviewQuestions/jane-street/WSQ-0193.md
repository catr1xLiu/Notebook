> [!example] Question 193 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trading Intern Interview

What is the probability that the sum of the outcomes when rolling two 6-sided dice is an odd number?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=194)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To obtain an odd sum when rolling two fair $6$-sided dice, one die must show an odd number and the other must show an even number.
>
> Each die has:
> - $3$ odd outcomes: $\{1,3,5\}$
> - $3$ even outcomes: $\{2,4,6\}$
>
> The favorable outcomes are:
>
> - first die odd, second die even: $3 \cdot 3 = 9$
> - first die even, second die odd: $3 \cdot 3 = 9$
>
> So the total number of favorable outcomes is
>
> $$
> 9 + 9 = 18.
> $$
>
> The total number of possible outcomes when rolling two dice is
>
> $$
> 6 \cdot 6 = 36.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability is
>
> $$
> \frac{18}{36} = \frac{1}{2}.
> $$
>
> Thus, the probability that the outcome is an odd number is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{2}}.
> $$
