> [!example] Question 289 · Jane Street
> ## Quant Trader Interview

What is the probability of getting an even number of heads when tossing 4 coins?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=290)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the probability of getting an even number of heads when $4$ fair coins are tossed, count the outcomes with $0$, $2$, or $4$ heads.
>
> The total number of equally likely outcomes is
>
> $$
> 2^4 = 16.
> $$
>
> The number of favorable outcomes is:
>
> - $0$ heads: $\binom{4}{0} = 1$
> - $2$ heads: $\binom{4}{2} = 6$
> - $4$ heads: $\binom{4}{4} = 1$
>
> So the total number of favorable outcomes is
>
> $$
> 1 + 6 + 1 = 8.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability is
>
> $$
> \frac{8}{16} = \frac{1}{2}.
> $$
>
> Thus, the probability of getting an even number of heads is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{2}}.
> $$
