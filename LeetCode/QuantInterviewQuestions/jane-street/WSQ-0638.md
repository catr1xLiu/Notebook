> [!example] Question 638 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is 56 squared? Calculate it using mental math.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=639)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To compute $56^2$, use the square expansion:
>
> $$
> 56^2 = (50+6)^2
> $$
>
> Apply $(a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$:
>
> $$
> (50+6)^2 = 50^2 + 2(50)(6) + 6^2
> $$
>
> $$
> = 2500 + 600 + 36
> $$
>
> $$
> = 3136
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 56^2 = 3136
> $$
