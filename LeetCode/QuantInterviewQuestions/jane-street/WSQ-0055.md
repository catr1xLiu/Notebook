> [!example] Question 55 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

If X, Y, and Z are three random variables such that X and Y have a correlation of 0.9, and Y and Z have a correlation of 0.8, what are the minimum and maximum possible values for the correlation between X and Z?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=56)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let
> $$
> \rho_{XY}=0.9,\qquad \rho_{YZ}=0.8,\qquad \rho_{XZ}=\rho.
> $$
>
> We seek the possible range of $\rho$.
>
> ## Approach
>
> For any three random variables, the correlation matrix must be positive semidefinite. Thus the matrix
> $$
> R=
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 1 & 0.9 & \rho \\
> 0.9 & 1 & 0.8 \\
> \rho & 0.8 & 1
> \end{pmatrix}
> $$
> must be positive semidefinite.
>
> A necessary and sufficient condition is that its determinant be nonnegative:
> $$
> \det(R)\ge 0.
> $$
>
> ## Compute the determinant
>
> For a $3\times 3$ correlation matrix,
> $$
> \det(R)=1+2\rho_{XY}\rho_{YZ}\rho_{XZ}-\rho_{XY}^2-\rho_{YZ}^2-\rho_{XZ}^2.
> $$
> Substituting the given values,
> $$
> \det(R)=1+2(0.9)(0.8)\rho-0.9^2-0.8^2-\rho^2.
> $$
> Hence
> $$
> \det(R)=1+1.44\rho-0.81-0.64-\rho^2
> = -\rho^2+1.44\rho-0.45.
> $$
> So we require
> $$
> -\rho^2+1.44\rho-0.45\ge 0.
> $$
> Equivalently,
> $$
> \rho^2-1.44\rho+0.45\le 0.
> $$
>
> ## Solve the quadratic inequality
>
> The roots are
> $$
> \rho=\frac{1.44\pm\sqrt{1.44^2-4(0.45)}}{2}.
> $$
> Compute:
> $$
> 1.44^2=2.0736,\qquad 2.0736-1.8=0.2736,\qquad \sqrt{0.2736}\approx 0.5231.
> $$
> Thus
> $$
> \rho=\frac{1.44\pm 0.5231}{2}.
> $$
> Therefore,
> $$
> \rho_{\min}\approx \frac{1.44-0.5231}{2}\approx 0.4584,
> $$
> and
> $$
> \rho_{\max}\approx \frac{1.44+0.5231}{2}\approx 0.9816.
> $$
>
> Using the exact correlation bound formula,
> $$
> \rho_{XZ}\in \left[\rho_{XY}\rho_{YZ}-\sqrt{(1-\rho_{XY}^2)(1-\rho_{YZ}^2)},\;
> \rho_{XY}\rho_{YZ}+\sqrt{(1-\rho_{XY}^2)(1-\rho_{YZ}^2)}\right],
> $$
> we get
> $$
> \rho_{XZ}\in \left[0.9\cdot 0.8-\sqrt{(1-0.9^2)(1-0.8^2)},\;
> 0.9\cdot 0.8+\sqrt{(1-0.9^2)(1-0.8^2)}\right]
> $$
> $$
> = \left[0.72-\sqrt{0.19\cdot 0.36},\; 0.72+\sqrt{0.19\cdot 0.36}\right]
> = \left[0.72-\sqrt{0.0684},\; 0.72+\sqrt{0.0684}\right]
> $$
> $$
> = \left[0.72-0.2615,\; 0.72+0.2615\right]
> = [0.4585,\;0.9815].
> $$
>
> ## Final answer
>
> The correlation between $X$ and $Z$ can range from approximately
> $$
> 0.4585 \le \rho_{XZ} \le 0.9815.
> $$
> So the minimum possible correlation is about $0.4585$, and the maximum possible correlation is about $0.9815$.
