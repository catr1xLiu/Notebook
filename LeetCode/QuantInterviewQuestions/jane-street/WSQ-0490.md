> [!example] Question 490 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

Two teams are playing a best-of-7 tournament. Each team has a 50% chance of winning each game. What is the probability that the series reaches the 7th game?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=491)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To reach the $7$th game in a best-of-$7$ series, neither team can have already won $4$ games in the first $6$ games.
>
> Thus, after $6$ games, the only way the series continues to game $7$ is if the teams are tied $3$-$3$.
>
> Each sequence of $6$ games is equally likely, with probability
>
> $$
> \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^6 = \frac{1}{64}.
> $$
>
> The number of ways for the teams to split the first $6$ games evenly is
>
> $$
> \binom{6}{3} = 20.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability that the series reaches the $7$th game is
>
> $$
> \binom{6}{3}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^6 = \frac{20}{64} = \frac{5}{16}.
> $$
>
> So the probability is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{16}}.
> $$
