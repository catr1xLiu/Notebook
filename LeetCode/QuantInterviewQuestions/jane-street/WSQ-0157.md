> [!example] Question 157 · Jane Street
> ## Intern Interview

What is the last digit of 17 raised to the 17th power (17^17)?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=158)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the last digit of $17^{17}$, it suffices to find the last digit of powers of $7$, since the last digit of $17$ is $7$.
>
> Compute the pattern of last digits:
>
> $$
> 7^1 \equiv 7 \pmod{10}
> $$
>
> $$
> 7^2 = 49 \equiv 9 \pmod{10}
> $$
>
> $$
> 7^3 \equiv 7 \cdot 9 = 63 \equiv 3 \pmod{10}
> $$
>
> $$
> 7^4 \equiv 7 \cdot 3 = 21 \equiv 1 \pmod{10}
> $$
>
> Thus the last digits repeat in a cycle of length $4$:
>
> $$
> 7,\ 9,\ 3,\ 1
> $$
>
> Now reduce the exponent modulo $4$:
>
> $$
> 17 \equiv 1 \pmod{4}
> $$
>
> So $17^{17}$ has the same last digit as the first term in the cycle, namely $7$.
>
> $$
> \boxed{7}
> $$
