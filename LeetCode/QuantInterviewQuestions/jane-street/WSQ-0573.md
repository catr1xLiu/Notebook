> [!example] Question 573 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is eleven million minus one thousand and eleven? Calculate the answer in your head without writing it down.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=574)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute in two steps:
>
> $$11{,}000{,}000 - 1{,}000 = 10{,}999{,}000$$
>
> Then subtract the remaining $11$:
>
> $$10{,}999{,}000 - 11 = 10{,}998{,}989$$
>
> Thus, the result is
>
> $$10{,}998{,}989$$
