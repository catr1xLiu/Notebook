> [!example] Question 66 · Jane Street
> ## Trading Interview

What is the expected value of throwing a six-sided die and paying an amount equal to the number rolled?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=67)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> For a fair $6$-sided die, the possible outcomes are $1,2,3,4,5,6$, each with probability $\frac{1}{6}$.
>
> Let $X$ be the amount paid, equal to the number rolled. Then the expected value is
>
> $$
> E[X] = \sum_{x=1}^{6} x \cdot P(X=x)
> $$
>
> Since $P(X=x)=\frac{1}{6}$ for each $x$,
>
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{1}{6}(1+2+3+4+5+6)
> $$
>
> Compute the sum:
>
> $$
> 1+2+3+4+5+6 = 21
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{21}{6} = 3.5
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected payment is
>
> $$
> \boxed{3.5}
> $$
