> [!example] Question 634 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is the angle between the hour and minute hands of a clock at 2:30?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=635)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> At $2{:}30$:
>
> - The minute hand is at $30$ minutes, so it points at
>   $$
>   30 \times 6^\circ = 180^\circ
>   $$
>   from the $12$.
>
> - The hour hand moves $30^\circ$ per hour, so at $2{:}30$ it is at
>   $$
>   2 \times 30^\circ + 30 \times 0.5^\circ = 60^\circ + 15^\circ = 75^\circ.
>   $$
>
> Therefore, the angle between the hands is
> $$
> |180^\circ - 75^\circ| = 105^\circ.
> $$
>
> So the angle between the two arms is
> $$
> \boxed{105^\circ}.
> $$
