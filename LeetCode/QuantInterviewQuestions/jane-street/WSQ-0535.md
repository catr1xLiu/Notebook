> [!example] Question 535 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is 12% of 47?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=536)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find $12\%$ of $47$, convert the percent to a decimal and multiply:
>
> $$
> 12\% = \frac{12}{100} = 0.12
> $$
>
> Then compute:
>
> $$
> 0.12 \times 47 = 5.64
> $$
>
> Therefore, $12\%$ of $47$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{5.64}
> $$
