> [!example] Question 200 · Jane Street
> ## Trading Intern Interview

What is the expected value of the money you would receive if you receive one dollar for every 'head' of a die and get to throw the die four times?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=201)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Interpret the experiment as follows: on each of $4$ throws, a fair die is rolled, and you receive $\text{\textdollar}1$ if the outcome is a head. Since a standard die has no heads, the number of heads obtained in any throw is always $0$.
>
> Let $X$ be the total amount of money received after $4$ throws. Then
>
> $$
> X = 0+0+0+0 = 0.
> $$
>
> Hence the expected value is
>
> $$
> E[X] = 0.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected amount of money received is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\$0}.
> $$
