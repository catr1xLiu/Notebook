> [!example] Question 608 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is 19% of 19?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=609)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find $19\%$ of $19$, convert the percent to a decimal:
>
> $$
> 19\% = \frac{19}{100} = 0.19
> $$
>
> Then multiply:
>
> $$
> 0.19 \times 19 = 3.61
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 19\% \text{ of } 19 = 3.61
> $$
