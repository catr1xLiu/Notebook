> [!example] Question 335 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is the minimum number of people required to guarantee that at least 5 people share the same birthday month?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=336)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Use the **Pigeonhole Principle**.
>
> There are $12$ birthday months. To avoid having $5$ people in the same month for as long as possible, place at most $4$ people in each month.
>
> Thus the largest number of people possible without forcing $5$ in one month is
>
> $$
> 12 \cdot 4 = 48.
> $$
>
> One more person guarantees that some month has at least $5$ people.
>
> Therefore, the minimum number of people required is
>
> $$
> 48 + 1 = 49.
> $$
>
> So the answer is
>
> $$
> \boxed{49}.
> $$
