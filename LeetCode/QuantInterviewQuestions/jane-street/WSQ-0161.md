> [!example] Question 161 · Jane Street
> ## Intern Interview

Three coins are in a bag. The first coin flips heads with probability 50%, the second coin flips heads with probability 60%, and the third coin flips heads with probability 70%. I pull out a coin at random and flip it; it lands heads. If I flip this coin again, what is the probability it will land heads?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=162)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let the three coins be $C_1, C_2, C_3$, with
>
> $$
> P(H \mid C_1)=0.5,\qquad P(H \mid C_2)=0.6,\qquad P(H \mid C_3)=0.7.
> $$
>
> Since a coin is chosen uniformly at random from the bag,
>
> $$
> P(C_1)=P(C_2)=P(C_3)=\frac{1}{3}.
> $$
>
> We are told the first flip was heads. We want
>
> $$
> P(\text{heads on second flip} \mid \text{heads on first flip}).
> $$
>
> Because flips of the same coin are conditionally independent given the coin, this probability is the posterior expected head probability of the chosen coin after observing one head:
>
> $$
> P(H_2 \mid H_1)=\sum_{i=1}^3 P(H_2 \mid C_i,H_1)\,P(C_i \mid H_1).
> $$
>
> Since $P(H_2 \mid C_i,H_1)=P(H \mid C_i)$, we first compute the posterior probabilities $P(C_i \mid H_1)$ using Bayes' rule.
>
> The total probability of getting heads on the first flip is
>
> $$
> P(H_1)=\frac{1}{3}(0.5+0.6+0.7)=\frac{1.8}{3}=0.6.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> P(C_1 \mid H_1)=\frac{P(H_1 \mid C_1)P(C_1)}{P(H_1)}
> =\frac{0.5\cdot \frac13}{0.6}
> =\frac{5}{18},
> $$
>
> $$
> P(C_2 \mid H_1)=\frac{0.6\cdot \frac13}{0.6}
> =\frac{1}{3},
> $$
>
> $$
> P(C_3 \mid H_1)=\frac{0.7\cdot \frac13}{0.6}
> =\frac{7}{18}.
> $$
>
> Now compute the desired probability:
>
> $$
> P(H_2 \mid H_1)
> =0.5\cdot \frac{5}{18}+0.6\cdot \frac{1}{3}+0.7\cdot \frac{7}{18}.
> $$
>
> Using fractions,
>
> $$
> = \frac12\cdot \frac{5}{18}+\frac35\cdot \frac13+\frac{7}{10}\cdot \frac{7}{18}
> = \frac{25+36+49}{180}
> = \frac{110}{180}
> = \frac{11}{18}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability of getting heads on the second flip is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{11}{18}\approx 0.6111}.
> $$
