> [!example] Question 308 · Jane Street
> ## Analyst Interview

How much would you pay to play a game where you roll a die and receive the number of dots rolled in dollars?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=309)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the amount won, in dollars, when a fair six-sided die is rolled. Then $X$ takes values
>
> $$
> 1,2,3,4,5,6
> $$
>
> each with probability
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{6}.
> $$
>
> The fair price to pay is the expected value of $X$:
>
> $$
> E[X] = \sum_{x=1}^{6} x \cdot \frac{1}{6}
> = \frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6}
> = \frac{21}{6}
> = 3.5.
> $$
>
> Therefore, a fair amount to pay is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\$3.50}.
> $$
