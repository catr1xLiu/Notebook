> [!example] Question 479 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

Solve the following mental math problems: 1) What is 14% of 42? 2) What is 36 squared? 3) What day of the week is April 15th, 2142?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=480)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To answer each part:
>
> 1. **Compute $14\%$ of $42$**
>
> $$
> 14\% \text{ of } 42 = 0.14 \times 42 = 5.88
> $$
>
> 2. **Compute $36^2$**
>
> $$
> 36^2 = 36 \times 36 = 1296
> $$
>
> 3. **Find the day of the week for April $15$, $2142$**
>
> Use Zeller’s congruence for the Gregorian calendar. For the date April $15$, $2142$:
>
> - $q = 15$
> - $m = 4$ since April is month $4$
> - $K = 42$ since $2142 \mod 100 = 42$
> - $J = 21$ since $\lfloor 2142/100 \rfloor = 21$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> h = \left(q + \left\lfloor \frac{13(m+1)}{5} \right\rfloor + K + \left\lfloor \frac{K}{4} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{J}{4} \right\rfloor + 5J \right) \bmod 7
> $$
>
> Substitute:
>
> $$
> h = \left(15 + \left\lfloor \frac{13(5)}{5} \right\rfloor + 42 + \left\lfloor \frac{42}{4} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{21}{4} \right\rfloor + 5 \cdot 21 \right) \bmod 7
> $$
>
> $$
> h = (15 + 13 + 42 + 10 + 5 + 105) \bmod 7
> $$
>
> $$
> h = 190 \bmod 7 = 1
> $$
>
> In Zeller’s congruence:
>
> - $0 =$ Saturday
> - $1 =$ Sunday
> - $2 =$ Monday
> - $\dots$
>
> So April $15$, $2142$ falls on a **Sunday**.
>
> ## Final answers
>

> 14\% \text{ of } 42 = 5.88
> $$
>
> $$
> 36^2 = 1296
> $$
>
> $$
> \text{April } 15, 2142 \text{ is a Sunday}
> $$
