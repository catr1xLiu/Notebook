> [!example] Question 512 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

What is the sum of all odd integers from 1 to 50?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=513)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The odd integers from $1$ to $50$ are
>
> $$
> 1, 3, 5, \dots, 49.
> $$
>
> This is an arithmetic sequence with:
>
> - first term: $a_1 = 1$
> - last term: $a_n = 49$
> - common difference: $d = 2$
>
> First, find the number of terms:
>
> $$
> n = \frac{49 - 1}{2} + 1 = \frac{48}{2} + 1 = 24 + 1 = 25.
> $$
>
> Now use the formula for the sum of an arithmetic sequence:
>
> $$
> S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n).
> $$
>
> So,
>
> $$
> S_{25} = \frac{25}{2}(1 + 49) = \frac{25}{2}(50) = 25 \cdot 25 = 625.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the sum of all odd integers from $1$ to $50$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{625}.
> $$
