What is 37 multiplied by 43? (Solve without using pen and paper.)

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=641)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute mentally using distribution:
> $$
> 37 \cdot 43 = 37(40+3)=37\cdot 40+37\cdot 3=1480+111=1591.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
> $$
> 37\cdot 43 = 1591.
> $$
