> [!example] Question 155 · Jane Street
> ## Intern Interview

Find the sum 1 + 3 + 5 + ... + 99 (the sum of all odd numbers below 100).

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=156)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The sequence of odd numbers below $100$ is
>
> $$
> 1, 3, 5, \dots, 99.
> $$
>
> This is an arithmetic sequence with:
>
> - first term: $a_1 = 1$
> - last term: $a_n = 99$
> - common difference: $d = 2$
>
> First, find the number of terms. Since the $n$th odd number is
>
> $$
> a_n = 2n - 1,
> $$
>
> set
>
> $$
> 2n - 1 = 99.
> $$
>
> Then
>
> $$
> 2n = 100 \quad \Rightarrow \quad n = 50.
> $$
>
> Now use the sum formula for an arithmetic series:
>
> $$
> S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n).
> $$
>
> So,
>
> $$
> S_{50} = \frac{50}{2}(1 + 99) = 25 \cdot 100 = 2500.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 1 + 3 + 5 + \cdots + 99 = 2500.
> $$
