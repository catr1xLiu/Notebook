> [!example] Question 631 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

Calculate 15% of 155. You have 10 seconds.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=632)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find $15\%$ of $155$, convert the percent to a decimal:
>
> $$
> 15\% = 0.15
> $$
>
> Then multiply:
>
> $$
> 0.15 \times 155 = 23.25
> $$
>
> \[
> \boxed{23.25}
> \]
