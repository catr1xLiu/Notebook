> [!example] Question 431 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

We are going to play a game. We flip a fair coin repeatedly until either the sequence HHT or HTT appears. Which sequence would you choose, HHT or HTT, and why?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=432)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let the game stop the first time either $A=\text{HHT}$ or $B=\text{HTT}$ appears as a consecutive block of three flips. We compare the probabilities that $A$ appears before $B$.
>
> The key observation is that both target patterns begin with $\text{H}$ and then $\text{HT}$ may develop differently. A convenient way to analyze this is by tracking the relevant suffix of the current sequence.
>
> ## States
>
> Let the states be:
>
> - $S$: no useful partial match,
> - $H$: the current suffix is $\text{H}$,
> - $HH$: the current suffix is $\text{HH}$,
> - $HT$: the current suffix is $\text{HT}$.
>
> Let $p_X$ denote the probability that $\text{HHT}$ occurs before $\text{HTT}$ starting from state $X$.
>
> We want $p_S$.
>
> ## Recurrence equations
>
> From state $S$:
>
> - Flip $\text{H}$ with probability $\tfrac12$, moving to $H$.
> - Flip $\text{T}$ with probability $\tfrac12$, staying in $S$.
>
> Thus
> $$
> p_S=\frac12 p_H+\frac12 p_S.
> $$
> So
> $$
> p_S=p_H.
> $$
>
> From state $H$:
>
> - Flip $\text{H}$ with probability $\tfrac12$, moving to $HH$.
> - Flip $\text{T}$ with probability $\tfrac12$, moving to $HT$.
>
> Thus
> $$
> p_H=\frac12 p_{HH}+\frac12 p_{HT}.
> $$
>
> From state $HH$:
>
> - Flip $\text{T}$ with probability $\tfrac12$, producing $\text{HHT}$, so success for $A$.
> - Flip $\text{H}$ with probability $\tfrac12$, the relevant suffix remains $HH$.
>
> Thus
> $$
> p_{HH}=\frac12\cdot 1+\frac12 p_{HH}.
> $$
> Hence
> $$
> p_{HH}=1.
> $$
>
> From state $HT$:
>
> - Flip $\text{T}$ with probability $\tfrac12$, producing $\text{HTT}$, so $A$ loses.
> - Flip $\text{H}$ with probability $\tfrac12$, the new suffix is $H$.
>
> Thus
> $$
> p_{HT}=\frac12\cdot 0+\frac12 p_H=\frac12 p_H.
> $$
>
> ## Solve
>
> Substitute into the equation for $p_H$:
> $$
> p_H=\frac12(1)+\frac12\left(\frac12 p_H\right)
> = \frac12+\frac14 p_H.
> $$
> Therefore
> $$
> \frac34 p_H=\frac12,
> \qquad
> p_H=\frac23.
> $$
> Since $p_S=p_H$, we get
> $$
> p_S=\frac23.
> $$
>
> ## Conclusion
>
> The probability that $\text{HHT}$ appears before $\text{HTT}$ is
> $$
> \frac23,
> $$
> so the better choice is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{HHT}}.
> $$
