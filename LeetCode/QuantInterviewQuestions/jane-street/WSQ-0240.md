> [!example] Question 240 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Analyst Interview

What is the probability that the sum of the numbers is even when tossing two dice?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=241)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the probability that the sum of two fair six-sided dice is even, observe that a sum is even exactly when both dice show numbers of the same parity:
>
> - both are even, or
> - both are odd.
>
> Each die has $3$ even outcomes: $\{2,4,6\}$ and $3$ odd outcomes: $\{1,3,5\}$.
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> P(\text{both even})=\frac{3}{6}\cdot\frac{3}{6}=\frac{1}{4},
> $$
>
> and
>
> $$
> P(\text{both odd})=\frac{3}{6}\cdot\frac{3}{6}=\frac{1}{4}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the required probability is
>
> $$
> P(\text{even sum})=P(\text{both even})+P(\text{both odd})=\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{4}=\frac{1}{2}.
> $$
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{2}}
> $$
