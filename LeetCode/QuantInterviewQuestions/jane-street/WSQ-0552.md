> [!example] Question 552 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is the sum of the odd numbers from 1 to 100?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=553)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The odd numbers from $1$ to $99$ form an arithmetic sequence:
>
> $$
> 1, 3, 5, \dots, 99
> $$
>
> Its first term is $a_1 = 1$, its last term is $a_n = 99$, and the common difference is $d = 2$.
>
> First, find the number of terms:
>
> $$
> n = \frac{99 - 1}{2} + 1 = 50
> $$
>
> Now use the sum formula for an arithmetic sequence:
>
> $$
> S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n)
> $$
>
> So,
>
> $$
> S_{50} = \frac{50}{2}(1 + 99) = 25 \cdot 100 = 2500
> $$
>
> Therefore, the sum of all odd numbers between $1$ and $100$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{2500}
> $$
