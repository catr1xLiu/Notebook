> [!example] Question 406 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

If two equally talented teams are playing in the World Series, what is the probability that the series lasts seven games?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=407)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Assume the World Series is a best-of-seven series, so the first team to win $4$ games wins the series. If the teams are equally talented, then each game is won by either team with probability $\tfrac12$, independently.
>
> A seven-game series occurs exactly when, after the first $6$ games, each team has won $3$ games. Then the winner is decided in game $7$.
>
> Thus the probability is the probability of getting exactly $3$ wins by one team in $6$ games:
>
> $$
> \Pr(\text{series goes 7 games})=\binom{6}{3}\left(\frac12\right)^6.
> $$
>
> Now compute:
>
> $$
> \binom{6}{3}=20,
> \qquad
> \left(\frac12\right)^6=\frac{1}{64}.
> $$
>
> So
>
> $$
> \Pr(\text{series goes 7 games})=20\cdot \frac{1}{64}=\frac{20}{64}=\frac{5}{16}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability that the series goes seven games is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{16}}.
> $$
