> ## Trading Intern Interview

What is the sum of all odd numbers up to sixty?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=203)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the sum of all odd numbers up to $60$, list the odd numbers:
>
> $$
> 1, 3, 5, \dots, 59
> $$
>
> These form an arithmetic sequence with:
>
> - first term: $a_1 = 1$
> - last term: $a_n = 59$
> - common difference: $d = 2$
>
> First, determine the number of terms:
>
> $$
> n = \frac{59 - 1}{2} + 1 = 30
> $$
>
> Now use the sum formula for an arithmetic sequence:
>
> $$
> S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n)
> $$
>
> So,
>
> $$
> S_{30} = \frac{30}{2}(1 + 59) = 15 \cdot 60 = 900
> $$
>
> Therefore, the sum of all odd numbers up to $60$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{900}
> $$
