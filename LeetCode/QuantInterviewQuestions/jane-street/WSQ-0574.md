> [!example] Question 574 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is the sum of all even numbers from 0 to 100?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=575)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The even integers from $0$ to $100$ form an arithmetic sequence:
>
> $$
> 0, 2, 4, \dots, 100
> $$
>
> The first term is $a_1 = 0$, the last term is $a_n = 100$, and the common difference is $d = 2$.
>
> The number of terms is
>
> $$
> n = \frac{100 - 0}{2} + 1 = 51
> $$
>
> The sum of an arithmetic sequence is
>
> $$
> S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n)
> $$
>
> So,
>
> $$
> S_{51} = \frac{51}{2}(0 + 100) = \frac{51}{2}\cdot 100 = 51 \cdot 50 = 2550
> $$
>
> Therefore, the sum of all even numbers from $0$ to $100$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{2550}
> $$
