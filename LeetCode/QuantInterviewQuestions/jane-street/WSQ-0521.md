> [!example] Question 521 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

What is the largest amount of postage that cannot be obtained using any combination of 5-cent and 11-cent stamps?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=522)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the largest amount of postage that cannot be formed using only $5$-cent and $11$-cent stamps, we use the two-coin Frobenius formula.
>
> For two relatively prime positive integers $a$ and $b$, the largest integer that cannot be expressed in the form
>
> $$
> ax + by
> $$
>
> with $x,y \ge 0$ integers is
>
> $$
> ab - a - b.
> $$
>
> Here,
>
> $$
> a = 5, \qquad b = 11.
> $$
>
> Since
>
> $$
> \gcd(5,11)=1,
> $$
>
> the formula applies. Thus the largest unattainable amount is
>
> $$
> 5 \cdot 11 - 5 - 11 = 55 - 16 = 39.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the maximum amount of postage that cannot be bought is
>
> $$
> \boxed{39 \text{ cents}}.
> $$
