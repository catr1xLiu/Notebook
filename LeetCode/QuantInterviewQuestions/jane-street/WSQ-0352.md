> [!example] Question 352 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

There are 10 people in a room. If each person shakes hands with every other person exactly once, how many total handshakes are there?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=353)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Each handshake is determined by choosing a pair of people from the $10$ people in the room. Thus the total number of handshakes is the number of unordered pairs of $10$ people:
>
> $$
> \binom{10}{2}=\frac{10 \cdot 9}{2}=45.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the total number of handshakes is
>
> $$
> \boxed{45}.
> $$
