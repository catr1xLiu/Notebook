> [!example] Question 373 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is the expected value when rolling two fair dice? Also, if I offer you a game where I receive X dollars for outcome set A and you receive Y dollars for outcome set B, would you play? Explain your reasoning.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=374)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> ## 1. Expected value of the sum of two dice
>
> Let $D_1$ and $D_2$ be the outcomes of two fair six-sided dice.
>
> Each die has expected value
> $$
> E[D_i] = \frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6} = \frac{21}{6} = 3.5.
> $$
>
> Using linearity of expectation, the expected sum is
> $$
> E[D_1 + D_2] = E[D_1] + E[D_2] = 3.5 + 3.5 = 7.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected value of two dice, interpreted as the sum of their faces, is
> $$
> \boxed{7}.
> $$
>
> If instead “expected value of two dice” means the ordered pair of outcomes, then expectation is taken componentwise:
> $$
> E[(D_1,D_2)] = (E[D_1],E[D_2]) = (3.5,3.5).
> $$
>
> ## 2. Whether to play the game
>
> The question depends on the probabilities of the two outcome sets and on the payoffs.
>
> Suppose:
>
> - outcome set $A$ occurs with probability $P(A)$,
> - outcome set $B$ occurs with probability $P(B)$,
> - you receive $Y$ dollars if $B$ occurs,
> - the other player receives $X$ dollars if $A$ occurs.
>
> To decide whether the game is favorable **for you**, compute your expected payoff. If your net payoff is:
>
> - $+Y$ when $B$ occurs,
> - $-X$ when $A$ occurs,
>
> then your expected value is
> $$
> E = Y\,P(B) - X\,P(A).
> $$
>
> The decision rule is:
>
> - play if
> $$
> Y\,P(B) - X\,P(A) > 0,
> $$
> - decline if
> $$
> Y\,P(B) - X\,P(A) < 0,
> $$
> - be indifferent if
> $$
> Y\,P(B) - X\,P(A) = 0.
> $$
>
> Equivalently, if $P(B)>0$, play when
> $$
> \frac{Y}{X} > \frac{P(A)}{P(B)}.
> $$
>
> Thus, one cannot answer “would you play?” without knowing the sets $A$ and $B$ (hence their probabilities) and the values of $X$ and $Y$. The correct criterion is to compare expected values using
> $$
> \boxed{E = Y\,P(B) - X\,P(A)}.
> $$
