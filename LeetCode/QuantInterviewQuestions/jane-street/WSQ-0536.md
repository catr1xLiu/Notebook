> [!example] Question 536 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

If you toss 4 coins and receive \$1 for 1 head, \$2 for 2 heads, \$3 for 3 heads, and \$4 for 4 heads, how much should you be willing to pay to play this game? If you have the option to re-toss all 4 coins after the first toss (keeping the best outcome), how much should you pay now?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=537)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $H$ denote the number of heads obtained when $4$ fair coins are tossed. Then
>
> $$
> H \sim \text{Binomial}(4,\tfrac12).
> $$
>
> The payoff is equal to the number of heads, in dollars. Thus the value of the game is its expected payoff.
>
> ## 1. Value of the original game
>
> Since the expected number of heads in $4$ fair tosses is
>
> $$
> E[H] = 4 \cdot \frac12 = 2,
> $$
>
> the expected payoff is
>
> $$
> \$2.
> $$
>
> So a fair price to pay for the game is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\$2}.
> $$
>
> ---
>
> ## 2. Value when you may retoss all $4$ coins once after seeing the first result
>
> Now suppose you toss the $4$ coins once, observe the number of heads, and then may either:
>
> - keep that result, or
> - discard it and retoss all $4$ coins once.
>
> Let the first result be $H_1$. If you keep it, the payoff is $H_1$.
>
> If you retoss, the new number of heads $H_2$ again has expected value
>
> $$
> E[H_2] = 2.
> $$
>
> Therefore, after seeing $H_1$, the optimal decision is:
>
> - keep if $H_1 \ge 2$,
> - retoss if $H_1 < 2$.
>
> So the optimal payoff is
>
> $$
> \max(H_1, 2 \text{ in expectation via retoss}).
> $$
>
> More explicitly,
>
> - if $H_1 = 0$ or $1$, retoss, giving expected payoff $2$;
> - if $H_1 = 2,3,4$, keep.
>
> Now compute the expected value.
>
> The distribution of $H_1$ is
>
> $$
> P(H_1=0)=\frac{1}{16},\quad
> P(H_1=1)=\frac{4}{16},\quad
> P(H_1=2)=\frac{6}{16},\quad
> P(H_1=3)=\frac{4}{16},\quad
> P(H_1=4)=\frac{1}{16}.
> $$
>
> Hence
>
> $$
> E[\text{optimal payoff}]
> = 2\left(\frac{1}{16}+\frac{4}{16}\right)
> +2\left(\frac{6}{16}\right)
> +3\left(\frac{4}{16}\right)
> +4\left(\frac{1}{16}\right).
> $$
>
> Compute:
>
> $$
> E[\text{optimal payoff}]
> = 2\cdot \frac{5}{16} + 2\cdot \frac{6}{16} + 3\cdot \frac{4}{16} + 4\cdot \frac{1}{16}
> = \frac{10+12+12+4}{16}
> = \frac{38}{16}
> = \frac{19}{8}.
> $$
>
> Thus the fair price is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{19}{8}\text{ dollars} = \$2.375}.
> $$
>
> ## Final answers
>
> - Without the retoss option: $\boxed{\text{\textdollar}2}$.
> - With the option to retoss all $4$ coins once after seeing the first outcome: $\boxed{\text{\textdollar}2.375}$.
