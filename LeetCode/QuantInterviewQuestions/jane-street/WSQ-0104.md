> [!example] Question 104 · Jane Street
> ## Trading Interview

Sum the even numbers in the range 1 to 100.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=105)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> The even numbers from $1$ to $100$ form the arithmetic sequence
>
> $$
> 2,4,6,\dots,100.
> $$
>
> There are
>
> $$
> \frac{100}{2}=50
> $$
>
> even numbers in this list. Their sum is
>
> $$
> 2(1+2+3+\cdots+50).
> $$
>
> Using the formula
>
> $$
> 1+2+\cdots+n=\frac{n(n+1)}{2},
> $$
>
> with $n=50$, we get
>
> $$
> 2\cdot \frac{50\cdot 51}{2}=50\cdot 51=2550.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the sum of the even numbers from $1$ to $100$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{2550}.
> $$
