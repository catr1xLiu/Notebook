> [!example] Question 612 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is the last digit in the value of 3 raised to the power of 33 (i.e., 3^33)?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=613)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the last digit of $3^{33}$, consider the pattern of last digits of powers of $3$:
>
> $$
> 3^1 = 3 \quad \text{(last digit } 3)
> $$
>
> $$
> 3^2 = 9 \quad \text{(last digit } 9)
> $$
>
> $$
> 3^3 = 27 \quad \text{(last digit } 7)
> $$
>
> $$
> 3^4 = 81 \quad \text{(last digit } 1)
> $$
>
> The last digits repeat in a cycle of length $4$:
>
> $$
> 3, 9, 7, 1
> $$
>
> Now compute:
>
> $$
> 33 \div 4 \text{ leaves remainder } 1
> $$
>
> So $3^{33}$ has the same last digit as $3^1$, which is
>
> $$
> \boxed{3}
> $$
