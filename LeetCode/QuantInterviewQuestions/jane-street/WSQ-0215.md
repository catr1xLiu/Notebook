> [!example] Question 215 · Jane Street
> ## Trading Intern Interview

What is the result of 235 minus 438?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=216)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To compute $235 - 438$, note that this is the negative of $438 - 235$:
>
> $$
> 235 - 438 = -(438 - 235)
> $$
>
> Now compute:
>
> $$
> 438 - 235 = 203
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 235 - 438 = -203
> $$
>
> So the answer is
>
> $$
> \boxed{-203}
> $$
