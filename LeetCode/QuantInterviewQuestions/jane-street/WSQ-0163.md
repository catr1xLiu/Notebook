> [!example] Question 163 · Jane Street
> ## Intern Interview

What is the probability of getting exactly 2 heads when tossing a fair coin five times?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=164)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Each toss of a fair coin has probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$ of heads and $\tfrac{1}{2}$ of tails, independently.
>
> The number of ways to obtain exactly $2$ heads in $5$ tosses is
>
> $$
> \binom{5}{2} = 10.
> $$
>
> The total number of possible outcomes in $5$ tosses is
>
> $$
> 2^5 = 32.
> $$
>
> Since all outcomes are equally likely, the probability of exactly $2$ heads is
>
> $$
> \frac{\binom{5}{2}}{2^5} = \frac{10}{32} = \frac{5}{16}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{16}}.
> $$
