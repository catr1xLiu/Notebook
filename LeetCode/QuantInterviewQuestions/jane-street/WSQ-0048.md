> [!example] Question 48 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

What is the probability of picking 2 kings from a standard deck of cards?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=49)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To draw $2$ kings when $2$ cards are picked from a standard deck of $52$ cards, count favorable outcomes and divide by total outcomes.
>
> A standard deck has $4$ kings.
>
> The number of ways to choose $2$ cards from $52$ is
>
> $$
> \binom{52}{2}.
> $$
>
> The number of ways to choose $2$ kings from the $4$ kings is
>
> $$
> \binom{4}{2}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability is
>
> $$
> \frac{\binom{4}{2}}{\binom{52}{2}}
> = \frac{6}{1326}
> = \frac{1}{221}.
> $$
>
> Thus, the probability of picking $2$ kings is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{221}}.
> $$
>
> In decimal form,
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{221} \approx 0.00452.
> $$
