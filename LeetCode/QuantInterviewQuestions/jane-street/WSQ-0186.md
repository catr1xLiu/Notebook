> [!example] Question 186 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trading Intern Interview

What is the probability of rolling a sum of 7 with two dice?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=187)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the probability that the sum of two fair six-sided dice is $7$, list the equally likely outcomes.
>
> Each die has $6$ possible results, so the total number of outcomes is
>
> $$
> 6 \times 6 = 36.
> $$
>
> The outcomes that give a sum of $7$ are
>
> $$
> (1,6),\ (2,5),\ (3,4),\ (4,3),\ (5,2),\ (6,1),
> $$
>
> so there are $6$ favorable outcomes.
>
> Therefore, the probability is
>
> $$
> \frac{6}{36} = \frac{1}{6}.
> $$
>
> Thus, the probability of rolling a sum of $7$ on two dice is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{6}}.
> $$
