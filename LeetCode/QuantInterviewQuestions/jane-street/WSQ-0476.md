> [!example] Question 476 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

What is the expected value of a roll of a standard six-sided die?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=477)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> For a fair six-sided die, the possible outcomes are $1,2,3,4,5,6$, each with probability $\frac{1}{6}$.
>
> The expectation of a discrete random variable is
>
> $$
> E[X] = \sum x \, P(X=x).
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> E[X] = 1\cdot \frac{1}{6} + 2\cdot \frac{1}{6} + 3\cdot \frac{1}{6} + 4\cdot \frac{1}{6} + 5\cdot \frac{1}{6} + 6\cdot \frac{1}{6}.
> $$
>
> Factor out $\frac{1}{6}$:
>
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{1}{6}(1+2+3+4+5+6) = \frac{1}{6}\cdot 21 = 3.5.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expectation of a roll of a fair die is
>
> $$
> \boxed{3.5}.
> $$
