> [!example] Question 540 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

Find the probability that the first two top cards you pick from a standard deck of cards are both tens.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=541)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> From a standard deck of $52$ cards, there are $4$ tens.
>
> To get two tens in the first two cards:
>
> - The probability the first card is a ten is
>   $$
>   \frac{4}{52}.
>   $$
>
> - Given that the first card is a ten, there are now $3$ tens left among $51$ cards, so the probability the second card is also a ten is
>   $$
>   \frac{3}{51}.
>   $$
>
> Thus, the required probability is
> $$
> \frac{4}{52}\cdot\frac{3}{51}
> = \frac{12}{2652}
> = \frac{1}{221}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability is
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{221}}.
> $$
