> [!example] Question 427 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is 34 multiplied by 82? Solve within ten seconds without using a pen or pencil.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=428)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute mentally using distribution:
>
> $$34 \times 82 = 34 \times (80 + 2) = 34 \times 80 + 34 \times 2.$$
>
> Now,
>
> $$34 \times 80 = (34 \times 8)\times 10 = 272 \times 10 = 2720,$$
>
> and
>
> $$34 \times 2 = 68.$$
>
> So,
>
> $$2720 + 68 = 2788.$$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$34 \times 82 = 2788.$$
