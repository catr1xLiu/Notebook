> [!example] Question 260 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Intern Interview

What is the minimum number of people required in a group to ensure that at least 7 people share the same birthday month?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=261)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Use the **Pigeonhole Principle**.
>
> There are $12$ birthday months. To avoid having $7$ people in the same month for as long as possible, place at most $6$ people in each month.
>
> Thus the largest number of people possible without forcing $7$ in one month is
>
> $$
> 12 \cdot 6 = 72.
> $$
>
> One more person guarantees that some month has at least $7$ people. Therefore the minimum number required is
>
> $$
> 72 + 1 = 73.
> $$
>
> So the answer is
>
> $$
> \boxed{73}.
> $$
