> [!example] Question 64 · Jane Street
> ## Trading Interview

What is the angle between the hour and minute hands at 9:20?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=65)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> At $9{:}20$:
>
> - The minute hand is at
>   $$
>   20 \times 6^\circ = 120^\circ
>   $$
>   from the $12$ o’clock position.
>
> - The hour hand is at
>   $$
>   9 \times 30^\circ + 20 \times 0.5^\circ = 270^\circ + 10^\circ = 280^\circ.
>   $$
>
> Thus the angle between them is
> $$
> |280^\circ - 120^\circ| = 160^\circ.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the angle between the hour and minute hands is
> $$
> \boxed{160^\circ}.
> $$
