> [!example] Question 28 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

What is the expected value of rolling three six-sided dice (3d6)?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=29)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> For a standard six-sided die, the expected value of one roll is
>
> $$
> E[\text{d}6] = \frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6} = \frac{21}{6} = 3.5.
> $$
>
> A $3\text{d}6$ roll is the sum of three independent six-sided dice. By linearity of expectation,
>
> $$
> E[3\text{d}6] = E[\text{d}6_1 + \text{d}6_2 + \text{d}6_3]
> = E[\text{d}6_1] + E[\text{d}6_2] + E[\text{d}6_3]
> = 3.5 + 3.5 + 3.5 = 10.5.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected value of a $3\text{d}6$ roll is
>
> $$
> \boxed{10.5}.
> $$
