> [!example] Question 318 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is 5 to the power of 5?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=319)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To evaluate $5^5$, multiply $5$ by itself five times:
>
> $$
> 5^5 = 5 \cdot 5 \cdot 5 \cdot 5 \cdot 5
> $$
>
> Compute step by step:
>
> $$
> 5 \cdot 5 = 25
> $$
>
> $$
> 25 \cdot 5 = 125
> $$
>
> $$
> 125 \cdot 5 = 625
> $$
>
> $$
> 625 \cdot 5 = 3125
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> 5^5 = 3125
> $$
