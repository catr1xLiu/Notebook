> [!example] Question 411 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

Apples cost 27 cents each. How many apples can you purchase with \$10? (Solve this in under one minute.)

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=412)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Each apple costs $\text{\textdollar}0.27$, and the total amount available is $\text{\textdollar}10.00$.
>
> Convert $\text{\textdollar}10$ to cents:
>
> $$
> 10.00 = 1000 \text{ cents}
> $$
>
> Now divide by $27$:
>
> $$
> \frac{1000}{27} = 37 \text{ remainder } 1
> $$
>
> Since you can only buy whole apples, the greatest number of apples you can purchase is
>
> $$
> \boxed{37}
> $$
>
> So, you can buy $\boxed{37}$ apples.
