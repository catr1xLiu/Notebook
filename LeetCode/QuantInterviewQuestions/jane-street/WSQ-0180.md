What is the result when you subtract 12 from 1,000,000?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=181)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Subtracting $12$ from $1{,}000{,}000$ gives
>
> $$
> 1{,}000{,}000 - 12 = 999{,}988.
> $$
>
> So the result is **$999{,}988$**.
