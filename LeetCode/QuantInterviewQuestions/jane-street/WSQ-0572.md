> ## Assistant Trader Interview

How many digits are there in 7 raised to the 7th power?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=573)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To determine the number of digits of $7^7$, first compute the value:
>
> $$
> 7^7 = 823543.
> $$
>
> Now count its digits. The number $823543$ has $6$ digits.
>
> Therefore, the number of digits in $7^7$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{6}.
> $$
