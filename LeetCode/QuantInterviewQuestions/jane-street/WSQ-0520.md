> [!example] Question 520 · Jane Street
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

What is the probability of getting a sum of 10 when you roll three dice?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=521)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the probability that the sum of three fair six-sided dice is $10$, count:
>
> 1. the total number of possible outcomes, and  
> 2. the number of outcomes whose sum is $10$.
>
> ## Total outcomes
>
> Each die has $6$ possible results, so with three dice the total number of equally likely outcomes is
>
> $$
> 6^3 = 216.
> $$
>
> ## Favorable outcomes
>
> We need the number of ordered triples $(a,b,c)$ such that
>
> $$
> a+b+c=10,
> \qquad 1 \le a,b,c \le 6.
> $$
>
> List the possible value patterns:
>
> - $1,3,6$ and its permutations: $6$ outcomes
> - $1,4,5$ and its permutations: $6$ outcomes
> - $2,2,6$ and its permutations: $3$ outcomes
> - $2,3,5$ and its permutations: $6$ outcomes
> - $2,4,4$ and its permutations: $3$ outcomes
> - $3,3,4$ and its permutations: $3$ outcomes
>
> Thus the total number of favorable outcomes is
>
> $$
> 6+6+3+6+3+3=27.
> $$
>
> ## Probability
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> P(\text{sum }=10)=\frac{27}{216}=\frac{1}{8}.
> $$
>
> ## Final answer
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{8}}
> $$
