> [!example] Question 643 · Jane Street
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is 75 squared? You have ten seconds and cannot use pen or paper.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=644)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To compute $75^2$ mentally, use the identity
>
> $$
> (a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2.
> $$
>
> Let $75 = 70 + 5$. Then
>
> $$
> 75^2 = (70+5)^2 = 70^2 + 2(70)(5) + 5^2.
> $$
>
> Now evaluate each term:
>
> $$
> 70^2 = 4900,\qquad 2(70)(5) = 700,\qquad 5^2 = 25.
> $$
>
> Add them:
>
> $$
> 4900 + 700 + 25 = 5625.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 75^2 = 5625.
> $$
