> [!example] Question 429 · Jane Street
> ## Trader Interview

What is 91 squared (91^2)?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=430)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute directly:
> $$
> 91^2 = (90+1)^2 = 90^2 + 2\cdot 90 \cdot 1 + 1^2 = 8100 + 180 + 1 = 8281.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
> $$
> 91^2 = 8281.
> $$
