> [!example] Question 1219 · SIG
> ## Junior Trader Interview

What is the probability of rolling six consecutive 3s with a fair six-sided die?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1220)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> For a fair six-sided die, the probability of rolling a $3$ on any one roll is
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{6}.
> $$
>
> To get $6$ threes in a row, all $6$ independent rolls must be $3$. Thus the probability is
>
> $$
> \left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^6 = \frac{1}{46656}.
> $$
>
> As a decimal,
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{46656} \approx 0.0000214.
> $$
>
> As a percentage,
>
> $$
> 0.0000214 \times 100\% \approx 0.00214\%.
> $$
>
> So the odds of rolling $6$ threes in a row are
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{46656}}
> $$
>
> which is about
>
> $$
> \boxed{0.00214\%}.
> $$
>
> If “odds” is meant in the gambling sense of odds against, then the odds against this happening are
>
> $$
> 46655:1.
> $$
