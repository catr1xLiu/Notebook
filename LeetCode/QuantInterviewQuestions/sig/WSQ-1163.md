> [!example] Question 1163 · SIG
> ## Intern Interview

Suppose you and your opponent each have three fair coins and flip all three at once. After flipping, if both of you have the same number of heads from your own coins, your opponent pays you \$1. If the numbers are different, you pay your opponent \$2. Should you agree to play this game?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1164)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> ## Solution
>
> Let $X$ be the number of heads you get from your $3$ fair coins, and let $Y$ be the number of heads I get from my $3$ fair coins.
>
> Then
> $$
> X, Y \sim \operatorname{Binomial}(3,\tfrac12),
> $$
> independently.
>
> The payoff to you is:
>
> - $+1$ dollar if $X=Y$,
> - $-2$ dollars if $X \ne Y$.
>
> So the expected value of the game to you is
> $$
> E = 1 \cdot P(X=Y) + (-2)\cdot P(X\ne Y).
> $$
>
> Thus we need $P(X=Y)$.
>
> ### Step 1: Distribution of the number of heads
>
> For $k=0,1,2,3$,
> $$
> P(X=k)=\binom{3}{k}\left(\tfrac12\right)^3=\frac{\binom{3}{k}}{8}.
> $$
>
> Hence
> $$
> P(X=0)=\frac18,\quad P(X=1)=\frac38,\quad P(X=2)=\frac38,\quad P(X=3)=\frac18.
> $$
>
> The same holds for $Y$.
>
> ### Step 2: Probability of a tie
>
> Since $X$ and $Y$ are independent,
> $$
> P(X=Y)=\sum_{k=0}^3 P(X=k)P(Y=k)
> = \sum_{k=0}^3 P(X=k)^2.
> $$
>
> Therefore
> $$
> P(X=Y)
> = \left(\frac18\right)^2+\left(\frac38\right)^2+\left(\frac38\right)^2+\left(\frac18\right)^2
> = \frac{1+9+9+1}{64}
> = \frac{20}{64}
> = \frac{5}{16}.
> $$
>
> So
> $$
> P(X\ne Y)=1-\frac{5}{16}=\frac{11}{16}.
> $$
>
> ### Step 3: Expected value
>
> Now compute
> $$
> E = 1\cdot \frac{5}{16} - 2\cdot \frac{11}{16}
> = \frac{5}{16}-\frac{22}{16}
> = -\frac{17}{16}.
> $$
>
> Thus the expected payoff to you is
> $$
> -\frac{17}{16}\text{ dollars}.
> $$
>
> Equivalently, the expected payoff to the interviewer is
> $$
> \frac{17}{16}\text{ dollars}.
> $$
>
> ## Conclusion
>
> No, you should not play. The game is unfavorable to you: your expected loss is
> $$
> \frac{17}{16}
> $$
> dollars per game.
