> [!example] Question 1130 · SIG
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

Which is larger: 2 to the power of 1/3 or 10 to the power of 1/10?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1131)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To compare $2^{1/3}$ and $10^{1/10}$, raise both quantities to a common power that eliminates the fractional exponents. The least common multiple of $3$ and $10$ is $30$.
>
> Compute:
> $$
> \left(2^{1/3}\right)^{30} = 2^{10} = 1024,
> $$
> and
> $$
> \left(10^{1/10}\right)^{30} = 10^3 = 1000.
> $$
>
> Since
> $$
> 1024 > 1000,
> $$
> it follows that
> $$
> 2^{1/3} > 10^{1/10}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the bigger number is
> $$
> \boxed{2^{1/3}}.
> $$
