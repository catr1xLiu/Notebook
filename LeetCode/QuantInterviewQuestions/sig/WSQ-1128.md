> [!example] Question 1128 · SIG
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

We are playing Russian roulette with a standard 6-chamber revolver. Two bullets are placed in adjacent chambers. I spin the cylinder, point the gun at my head, and pull the trigger. I survive. Now it's your turn. You can either (a) re-spin, aim at your head, and pull the trigger, or (b) not spin, aim at your head, and pull the trigger. Which option gives you a higher chance of survival? Additionally, given that I spun the cylinder, what is the probability that I die this time?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1129)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> ## Setup
>
> Model the revolver as a circle of $6$ chambers, with $2$ adjacent chambers loaded and $4$ empty chambers. After a spin, each chamber is equally likely to be under the hammer.
>
> Let the chambers be arranged as
>
> $$
> B,\; B,\; E,\; E,\; E,\; E
> $$
>
> in circular order, where $B$ denotes a bullet and $E$ an empty chamber.
>
> You pull the trigger once and hear a click, so the current chamber is known to be empty.
>
> ---
>
> ## Part 1: Should the next player re-spin?
>
> There are two options.
>
> ### Option (a): Re-spin
>
> If the cylinder is spun again, all $6$ chambers are equally likely. Since $2$ of the $6$ chambers contain bullets,
>
> $$
> P(\text{death} \mid \text{re-spin}) = \frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}.
> $$
>
> Thus
>
> $$
> P(\text{survival} \mid \text{re-spin}) = 1 - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{3}.
> $$
>
> ---
>
> ### Option (b): Do not spin
>
> Now use the information that the previous trigger pull was a click. So the current chamber is one of the $4$ empty chambers.
>
> Among the $4$ empty chambers, examine what the *next* chamber would be:
>
> - If the current chamber is the empty chamber immediately before the block of bullets, then the next chamber is a bullet.
> - If the current chamber is any of the other $3$ empty chambers, the next chamber is empty.
>
> Because the initial chamber after the spin was uniformly random and we are conditioning on it being empty, each of the $4$ empty chambers is equally likely. Therefore,
>
> $$
> P(\text{death} \mid \text{no spin, after a click}) = \frac{1}{4}.
> $$
>
> Hence
>
> $$
> P(\text{survival} \mid \text{no spin, after a click}) = 1 - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4}.
> $$
>
> ---
>
> ## Comparison
>
> - Re-spin: survival probability $= \frac{2}{3}$.
> - Do not spin: survival probability $= \frac{3}{4}$.
>
> Since
>
> $$
> \frac{3}{4} > \frac{2}{3},
> $$
>
> the better choice is **(b) do not spin**.
>
> ---
>
> ## Part 2: Given that you spun, what is the probability that you die this time?
>
> If the cylinder is spun before this trigger pull, the chamber is again uniformly random among all $6$ chambers. Therefore,
>
> $$
> P(\text{death} \mid \text{spin}) = \frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}.
> $$
>
> ---
>
> ## Final answers
>
> 1. The safer choice is **(b) do not spin**.
> 2. The probability of dying if the cylinder is spun for this pull is
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{3}.
> $$
>
> For reference, without spinning after the initial click, the probability of dying on the next pull is
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{4}.
> $$
