> [!example] Question 1209 · SIG
> ## Equity Research Associate Interview

Five guys walk into a bar. In how many ways can they sit so that they are arranged from oldest to youngest?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1210)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To be arranged from oldest to youngest, the five men must sit in exactly the order determined by their ages.
>
> Assuming all five have distinct ages, once their left-to-right seating is required to be from oldest to youngest, there is no freedom in the arrangement: the oldest must sit first, the second-oldest next, and so on until the youngest sits last.
>
> Therefore, the number of possible seating arrangements is
>
> $$1$$
>
> So the answer is
>
> $$\boxed{1}$$
