> [!example] Question 1214 · SIG
> ## Quantitative Trading Intern Interview

You are betting \$50 on a basketball game and your friend is betting \$20. If you win, you gain your friend's \$20; if you lose, you forfeit your \$50 to your friend. What is your expected profit?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1215)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let the outcomes be equally likely: either you win the bet or your friend wins the bet.
>
> ## Step 1: Identify the profit in each outcome
>
> - If you win, you gain your friend’s \(\text{\textdollar}20,\) so your profit is $+\text{\textdollar}20$.
> - If you lose, your friend gets your \(\text{\textdollar}50,\) so your profit is $-\text{\textdollar}50$.
>
> Thus the profit random variable $X$ is
>
> $$
> X=
> \begin{cases}
> 20, & \text{with probability } \frac{1}{2},\\
> -50, & \text{with probability } \frac{1}{2}.
> \end{cases}
> $$
>
> ## Step 2: Compute the expected profit
>
> The expected value is
>
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{1}{2}(20) + \frac{1}{2}(-50).
> $$
>
> So
>
> $$
> E[X] = 10 - 25 = -15.
> $$
>
> ## Final Answer
>
> Your expected profit is
>
> $$
> -\$15.
> $$
>
> So, on average, you expect to **lose $15**.
