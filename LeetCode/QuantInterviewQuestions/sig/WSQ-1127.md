> [!example] Question 1127 · SIG
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

Given a multiplication table showing values such as 2^2, 2^3, and so on, estimate the range for the value of 14^7 divided by 5^3.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1128)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To estimate the size of
>
> $$
> \frac{14^7}{5^3},
> $$
>
> it is enough to compare it with nearby powers or compute it approximately.
>
> First compute the numerator:
>
> $$
> 14^2 = 196,\quad
> 14^3 = 2744,\quad
> 14^4 = 38416,
> $$
>
> $$
> 14^5 = 537824,\quad
> 14^6 = 7529536,\quad
> 14^7 = 105413504.
> $$
>
> Now compute the denominator:
>
> $$
> 5^3 = 125.
> $$
>
> So
>
> $$
> \frac{14^7}{5^3} = \frac{105413504}{125}.
> $$
>
> Divide by $125$:
>
> $$
> \frac{105413504}{125} = 843308.032.
> $$
>
> Thus the value is approximately
>
> $$
> 8.43 \times 10^5.
> $$
>
> So a reasonable guessed range is
>
> $$
> 800{,}000 \text{ to } 900{,}000.
> $$
>
> In particular,
>
> $$
> \frac{14^7}{5^3} \approx 843{,}308.
> $$
