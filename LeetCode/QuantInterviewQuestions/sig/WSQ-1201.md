> [!example] Question 1201 · SIG
> ## Quant Trader Intern Interview

What is the probability of rolling two sixes with a pair of standard six-sided dice?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1202)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Assume two fair six-sided dice are rolled.
>
> The probability that a single die shows a six is
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{6}.
> $$
>
> Since the two rolls are independent, the probability that both dice show six is
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{6}\cdot\frac{1}{6}=\frac{1}{36}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability of rolling $2$ sixes is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{36}}.
> $$
