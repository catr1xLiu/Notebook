> [!example] Question 1243 · SIG
> ## Graduate Trader Interview

What is the probability of receiving exactly 3 heads in 5 tosses of a fair coin?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1244)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> For $5$ fair coin tosses, each specific sequence of heads and tails has probability
>
> $$
> \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^5=\frac{1}{32}.
> $$
>
> To get exactly $3$ heads, choose which $3$ of the $5$ tosses are heads. The number of such choices is
>
> $$
> \binom{5}{3} = 10.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability is
>
> $$
> \binom{5}{3}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^5
> = 10 \cdot \frac{1}{32}
> = \frac{10}{32}
> = \frac{5}{16}.
> $$
>
> Thus, the probability of receiving exactly $3$ heads in $5$ fair coin tosses is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{16}}.
> $$
