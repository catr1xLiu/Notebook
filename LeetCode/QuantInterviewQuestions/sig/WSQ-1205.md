> [!example] Question 1205 · SIG
> ## Quantitative Analyst Interview

Mickey and Minnie plan to meet at a cafe, but each will independently arrive at a uniformly random time between 9:00 and 10:00. Mickey will wait 10 minutes for Minnie before leaving, and Minnie will wait 30 minutes for Mickey before leaving. What is the probability that they meet each other?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1206)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let Mickey’s arrival time be $M$ and Minnie’s arrival time be $N$, measured in minutes after $9{:}00$. Then
>
> $$
> M, N \sim \text{Uniform}(0,60)
> $$
>
> independently.
>
> They meet if the later person arrives before the earlier person leaves.
>
> - If Mickey arrives first, then Minnie must arrive within $10$ minutes:
>   $$
>   N - M \le 10 \quad \text{when } N \ge M.
>   $$
> - If Minnie arrives first, then Mickey must arrive within $30$ minutes:
>   $$
>   M - N \le 30 \quad \text{when } M \ge N.
>   $$
>
> Thus they meet exactly when
>
> $$
> -30 \le N - M \le 10.
> $$
>
> Geometrically, this is the region in the $60 \times 60$ square $[0,60] \times [0,60]$ between the lines
>
> $$
> N = M - 30
> \quad \text{and} \quad
> N = M + 10.
> $$
>
> The total area of the square is
>
> $$
> 60^2 = 3600.
> $$
>
> The complement consists of two right triangles:
>
> 1. Above the line $N = M + 10$, with side length $50$, so area
>    $$
>    \frac{1}{2}(50)(50) = 1250.
>    $$
>
> 2. Below the line $N = M - 30$, with side length $30$, so area
>    $$
>    \frac{1}{2}(30)(30) = 450.
>    $$
>
> Hence the favorable area is
>
> $$
> 3600 - 1250 - 450 = 1900.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability of meeting is
>
> $$
> \frac{1900}{3600} = \frac{19}{36}.
> $$
>
> So the probability that they meet is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{19}{36}}.
> $$
