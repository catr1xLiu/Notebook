> [!example] Question 1232 · SIG
> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

A deck of 18 custom playing cards is numbered from 1 to 18. A card is drawn 6 times with replacement. If X is the number of draws that result in a prime number, what is the expected value E(X)?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1233)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the number of draws, out of $6$, that produce a prime-numbered card.
>
> Each draw is independent and has the same probability of success, where a “success” means drawing a prime number. Thus $X$ follows a binomial distribution with parameters $n=6$ and $p=$ probability of drawing a prime from the numbers $1$ to $18$.
>
> First count the primes from $1$ to $18$:
> $$2,3,5,7,11,13,17,$$
> so there are $7$ primes.
>
> Hence
> $$p=\frac{7}{18}.$$
>
> For a binomial random variable,
> $$E(X)=np.$$
> Therefore,
> $$E(X)=6\cdot \frac{7}{18}=\frac{42}{18}=\frac{7}{3}.$$
>
> So,
> $$\boxed{E(X)=\frac{7}{3}}.$$
