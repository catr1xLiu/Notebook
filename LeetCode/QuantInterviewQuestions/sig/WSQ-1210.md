> [!example] Question 1210 · SIG
> ## Equity Research Associate Interview

If you have 8 marbles and one is lighter than the rest, how would you determine which one is lighter using a balance scale in two weighings?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1211)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Label the marbles $1,2,\dots,8$.
>
> ## Strategy
> Use the balance scale to split the possibilities as evenly as possible.
>
> ## Step 1
> Weigh $3$ marbles against $3$ marbles.
>
> For example, weigh $\{1,2,3\}$ against $\{4,5,6\}$.
>
> There are two cases:
>
> ### Case 1: The scale balances
> Then marbles $1$ through $6$ are normal, so the lighter marble must be either $7$ or $8$.
>
> ## Step 2
> Weigh $7$ against $8$.
>
> The lighter side contains the lighter marble.
>
> ---
>
> ### Case 2: The scale does not balance
> Then the lighter marble is among the $3$ marbles on the lighter side.
>
> Suppose $\{1,2,3\}$ is lighter; then the odd marble is one of $1,2,3$.
>
> ## Step 2
> Weigh $1$ against $2$.
>
> - If one side is lighter, that marble is the lighter one.
> - If they balance, then $3$ is the lighter marble.
>
> ## Conclusion
> In at most $2$ weighings, the lighter marble can always be identified:
> 1. Weigh $3$ against $3$.
> 2. Then weigh either $1$ against $2$ within the lighter group, or $7$ against $8$ if the first weighing balances.
