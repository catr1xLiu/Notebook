> [!example] Question 1171 · SIG
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

What is the expected number of times you need to roll a die to get a 6?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1172)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the number of rolls needed to obtain the first $6$ on a fair six-sided die.
>
> Each roll is independent, and the probability of success (rolling a $6$) on any roll is
>
> $$
> p=\frac{1}{6}.
> $$
>
> Thus, $X$ follows a geometric distribution with parameter $p=\frac{1}{6}$.
>
> For a geometric random variable counting the number of trials until the first success,
>
> $$
> E[X]=\frac{1}{p}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> E[X]=\frac{1}{1/6}=6.
> $$
>
> Hence, the expected number of rolls required to get a $6$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{6}.
> $$
