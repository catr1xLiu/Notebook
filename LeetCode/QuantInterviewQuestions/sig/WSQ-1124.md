> [!example] Question 1124 · SIG
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

What is the expected value of a single roll of a fair six-sided die?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1125)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> For a fair six-sided die, the possible outcomes are $1,2,3,4,5,6$, each with probability $\frac{1}{6}$.
>
> The expected value is
>
> $$
> E[X] = \sum_{x=1}^{6} x \cdot P(X=x)
> $$
>
> Since $P(X=x)=\frac{1}{6}$ for each outcome,
>
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{1}{6}(1+2+3+4+5+6)
> $$
>
> Compute the sum:
>
> $$
> 1+2+3+4+5+6 = 21
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{21}{6} = \frac{7}{2} = 3.5
> $$
>
> Therefore, the expected roll of a fair die is
>
> $$
> \boxed{3.5}
> $$
