> [!example] Question 1254 · SIG
> ## Senior Software Engineer Interview

Compress a string such that repeated characters are replaced by the character followed by the number of repetitions. For example, given 'AAABBCC', output 'A3B2C2'.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1255)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To compress the string, count consecutive repeated characters and write each character followed by its count.
>
> Given:
>
> $$\text{AAABBCC}$$
>
> Count runs:
>
> - $$A$$ appears $$3$$ times
> - $$B$$ appears $$2$$ times
> - $$C$$ appears $$2$$ times
>
> So the compressed form is:
>
> $$\text{A3B2C2}$$
