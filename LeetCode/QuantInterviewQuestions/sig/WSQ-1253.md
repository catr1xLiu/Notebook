> [!example] Question 1253 · SIG
> ## Trader Interview

If you roll two dice and add their results, what is the probability of obtaining a sum of 6?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1254)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> When two fair six-sided dice are rolled, there are
>
> $$6 \times 6 = 36$$
>
> equally likely outcomes.
>
> To obtain a sum of $6$, the favorable outcomes are:
>
> $$(1,5),\ (2,4),\ (3,3),\ (4,2),\ (5,1)$$
>
> Thus, there are $5$ favorable outcomes.
>
> Therefore, the probability is
>
> $$\frac{5}{36}$$
>
> So, the probability of obtaining a sum of $6$ is
>
> $$\boxed{\frac{5}{36}}$$
