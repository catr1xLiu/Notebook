> [!example] Question 1172 · SIG
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

What is the fair price to enter a game where you win the amount shown on a single rolled die?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1173)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To find the fair price, compute the expected value of the payoff.
>
> A fair six-sided die has outcomes $1,2,3,4,5,6$, each with probability $\frac{1}{6}$. If the payoff equals the number shown, then the expected winnings are
>
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6} = \frac{21}{6} = 3.5.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the fair price to enter the game is
>
> $$
> \boxed{3.5}
> $$
>
> in the same monetary units as the payoff.
