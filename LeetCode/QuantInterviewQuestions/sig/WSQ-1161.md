> ## Inheritance
>
> **Inheritance** is a fundamental concept in object-oriented programming in which one class acquires the properties and behaviors of another class.
>
> - The existing class is called the **base class**, **parent class**, or **superclass**.
> - The new class is called the **derived class**, **child class**, or **subclass**.
>
> Through inheritance, the child class can:
> - reuse data members and methods of the parent class,
> - add new features,
> - modify existing behavior.
>
> Thus, inheritance promotes **code reusability** and **hierarchical classification**.
>

> ## Polymorphism
>
> **Polymorphism** means “many forms.” In object-oriented programming, it refers to the ability of a single interface or function name to behave differently depending on the object or context.
>
> Common forms of polymorphism are:
>
> - **Compile-time polymorphism**: achieved by function overloading or operator overloading.
> - **Run-time polymorphism**: achieved by method overriding through inheritance.
>
> Hence, polymorphism allows the same operation to produce different behaviors for different objects.

> ## Probability that three people have birthdays on the same day of the week
>
> A week has $7$ days. Assume each person’s birthday is equally likely to fall on any day of the week, independently of the others.
>
> ### Total number of possible outcomes
>
> Each of the $3$ people can have a birthday on any of $7$ days, so the total number of possibilities is
>
> $$
> 7^3 = 343.
> $$
>
> ### Favorable outcomes
>
> For all three people to have birthdays on the same day of the week:
>
> - all three could be on Monday,
> - or all three on Tuesday,
> - and so on up to Sunday.
>
> Thus, there are $7$ favorable outcomes.
>
> ### Probability
>
> Therefore, the required probability is
>
> $$
> \frac{7}{7^3} = \frac{1}{7^2} = \frac{1}{49}.
> $$
>
> ## Final Answer
>
> - **Inheritance:** the mechanism by which one class acquires the properties and methods of another class.
> - **Polymorphism:** the ability of the same interface or method name to take different forms depending on the object or context.
> - **Probability that three people have birthdays on the same day of the week:** 
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{49}}
> $$
