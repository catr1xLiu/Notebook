> [!example] Question 1114 · SIG
> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

A deck of 18 custom playing cards is numbered from 1 to 18. A card is drawn 6 times with replacement. If X is the number of draws that result in a prime number, what is the expected value of X (E(X))?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1115)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the number of draws, out of $6$, that produce a prime number.
>
> Each draw is independent and made with replacement, so $X$ follows a binomial distribution with parameters $n=6$ and success probability $p$, where “success” means drawing a prime-numbered card.
>
> First count the prime numbers from $1$ to $18$:
> $$
> 2,3,5,7,11,13,17
> $$
> There are $7$ primes, so
> $$
> p=\frac{7}{18}.
> $$
>
> For a binomial random variable,
> $$
> E(X)=np.
> $$
> Thus,
> $$
> E(X)=6\cdot \frac{7}{18}=\frac{42}{18}=\frac{7}{3}.
> $$
>
> Therefore,
> $$
> E(X)=\frac{7}{3}.
> $$
