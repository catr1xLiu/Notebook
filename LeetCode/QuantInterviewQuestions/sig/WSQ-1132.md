> [!example] Question 1132 · SIG
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

You have five friends of different ages going to dinner. What is the probability that they will sit in age order around the table, either clockwise or counterclockwise?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1133)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> For $5$ distinct people seated uniformly at random around a round table, the number of circular arrangements is
>
> $$
> (5-1)! = 4! = 24.
> $$
>
> To be in age order around the table, the ages must appear consecutively either
>
> - in increasing order clockwise, or
> - in increasing order counterclockwise.
>
> These are the only $2$ favorable circular arrangements.
>
> Hence the probability is
>
> $$
> \frac{2}{24}=\frac{1}{12}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the probability that they sit in age order around the table is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{12}}.
> $$
