> [!example] Question 1122 · SIG
> ## Assistant Trader Interview

You roll a die and win twice the value of the face if it is even, or the face value if it is odd. What is the fair amount to charge to play this game? If you are given the option to roll again and will only be paid based on the result of the second roll if you choose to roll again (i.e., the result of the last roll), what is the fair charge to play the game with this option?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1123)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> ## Solution
>
> Let $X$ be the payoff from one roll of a fair six-sided die.
>
> - If the outcome is odd, the payoff equals the face value.
> - If the outcome is even, the payoff equals twice the face value.
>
> Thus the possible payoffs are:
>
> - Roll $1 \to 1$
> - Roll $2 \to 4$
> - Roll $3 \to 3$
> - Roll $4 \to 8$
> - Roll $5 \to 5$
> - Roll $6 \to 12$
>
> Since each outcome occurs with probability $\frac{1}{6}$, the fair price to charge is the expected payoff.
>
> ### 1. Price for one play
>
> $$
> E[X] = \frac{1}{6}(1+4+3+8+5+12)
> = \frac{33}{6}
> = 5.5
> $$
>
> So the fair amount to charge for one play is
>
> $$
> \boxed{5.50}
> $$
>
> ---
>
> ## 2. Option to roll again, with only the last roll counting
>
> Now suppose after seeing the first payoff, the player may choose to roll again, but then only the **second** roll counts. This is an optimal stopping problem with one possible reroll.
>
> Let $X$ be the first-roll payoff. If the player keeps it, they receive $X$. If they reroll, the expected value of the second roll is again
>
> $$
> E[X] = 5.5
> $$
>
> Therefore, after the first roll, the rational decision is:
>
> - **Keep** the first payoff if $X \ge 5.5$
> - **Reroll** if $X < 5.5$
>
> The first-roll payoffs are $1,4,3,8,5,12$. Hence:
>
> - Reroll after $1,4,3,5$
> - Keep after $8,12$
>
> So the value of the game with this option is
>
> $$
> E[\max(X,5.5)]
> $$
>
> computed directly as
>
> $$
> \frac{1}{6}(5.5+5.5+5.5+8+5.5+12)
> $$
>
> since for outcomes $1,4,3,5$ we reroll and obtain expected value $5.5$, while for $8,12$ we keep the original payoff.
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> E[\text{optimal payoff}]
> = \frac{1}{6}(5.5+5.5+5.5+8+5.5+12)
> = \frac{42}{6}
> = 7
> $$
>
> So the fair amount to charge when the player has the option to reroll once and only the final roll counts is
>
> $$
> \boxed{7.00}
> $$
>
> ## Final Answers
>
> - Fair price for one roll: $$\boxed{5.50}$$
> - Fair price with the option to reroll once, with only the last roll counting: $$\boxed{7.00}$$
