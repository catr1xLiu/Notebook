> [!example] Question 1152 · SIG
> ## Software Developer Interview

Is 1599 a prime number?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1153)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To determine whether $1599$ is prime, test divisibility by small primes.
>
> First, observe that the sum of its digits is
>
> $$
> 1+5+9+9=24.
> $$
>
> Since $24$ is divisible by $3$, the number $1599$ is also divisible by $3$.
>
> Compute:
>
> $$
> 1599 \div 3 = 533.
> $$
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> 1599 = 3 \cdot 533.
> $$
>
> So $1599$ is **not** a prime number.
