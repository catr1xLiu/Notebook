> [!example] Question 1158 · SIG
> ## Intern Interview

It costs \$1 to play a game where you flip a fair coin four times. If you get four consecutive heads, you win \$10. Should you play the game?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1159)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Compute the probability of winning.
>
> To win, all four flips must be heads. Since the coin is fair and the flips are independent,
>
> $$
> P(\text{4 heads})=\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^4=\frac{1}{16}.
> $$
>
> If you win, your prize is $\text{\textdollar}10$. Thus the expected payout is
>
> $$
> E(\text{payout})=10\cdot \frac{1}{16}=\frac{10}{16}=\frac{5}{8}=\$0.625.
> $$
>
> Since it costs $\text{\textdollar}1$ to play, the expected net gain is
>
> $$
> E(\text{net})=\$0.625-\$1=-\$0.375.
> $$
>
> So the game has a negative expected value of $\text{\textdollar}0.375$ per play.
>
> **Conclusion:** No, you should not play the game.
