> [!example] Question 1043 · IMC
> ## Graduate Trader Interview

Which type of graph is best used to describe time-series data?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1044)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> A **line graph** is best for describing a time series.
>
> ### Explanation
> A time series records values of a variable at successive points in time. A line graph is most appropriate because it:
>
> - places **time** on the horizontal axis,
> - shows the variable’s values on the vertical axis,
> - connects consecutive data points to display **change over time** clearly,
> - makes trends, cycles, and fluctuations easy to see.
>
> ### Final Answer
> The best graph for a time series is a **line graph**.
