> [!example] Question 1087 · IMC
> ## Trading Interview

Draw the payoff diagrams for buying and selling a call option and a put option.

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1088)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> ## Payoff diagrams for basic option positions
>
> Let $S_T$ denote the stock price at expiration and $K$ the strike price.
>
> A **payoff diagram** shows the value of the option position at expiration, ignoring the initial premium.
>
> ---
>
> ## 1. Long call (buying a call)
>
> A call gives the right to buy the stock at price $K$.
>
> - If $S_T \le K$, exercising is not worthwhile, so payoff is $0$.
> - If $S_T > K$, the payoff is $S_T - K$.
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \text{Payoff}=\max(S_T-K,0)
> $$
>
> ### Diagram
>
> ```text
> Payoff
>   ^
>   |
>   |                    /
>   |                  /
>   |                /
>   |              /
>   |_____________/______________> S_T
>                 K
> ```
>
> ---
>
> ## 2. Short call (selling a call)
>
> The seller of the call takes the opposite payoff of the buyer.
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \text{Payoff}=-\max(S_T-K,0)
> $$
>
> ### Diagram
>
> ```text
> Payoff
>   ^
>   |
>   |
>   |
>   |_____________
>   |             \
>   |              \
>   |               \
>   |________________\___________> S_T
>                  K
> ```
>
> ---
>
> ## 3. Long put (buying a put)
>
> A put gives the right to sell the stock at price $K$.
>
> - If $S_T \ge K$, exercising is not worthwhile, so payoff is $0$.
> - If $S_T < K$, the payoff is $K - S_T$.
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \text{Payoff}=\max(K-S_T,0)
> $$
>
> ### Diagram
>
> ```text
> Payoff
>   ^
>   |\
>   | \
>   |  \
>   |   \
>   |    \______________
>   |
>   |___________________________> S_T
>                K
> ```
>
> ---
>
> ## 4. Short put (selling a put)
>
> The seller of the put has the opposite payoff of the buyer.
>
> Thus,
>
> $$
> \text{Payoff}=-\max(K-S_T,0)
> $$
>
> ### Diagram
>
> ```text
> Payoff
>   ^
>   |
>   |            ______________
>   |          /
>   |        /
>   |      /
>   |    /
>   |___/______________________> S_T
>       K
> ```
>
> ---
>
> ## Summary of payoff formulas
>
> $$
> \text{Long call}=\max(S_T-K,0)
> $$
>
> $$
> \text{Short call}=-\max(S_T-K,0)
> $$
>
> $$
> \text{Long put}=\max(K-S_T,0)
> $$
>
> $$
> \text{Short put}=-\max(K-S_T,0)
> $$
>
> If the **profit** diagram is required instead of the payoff diagram, subtract the premium paid for a long position and add the premium received for a short position.
