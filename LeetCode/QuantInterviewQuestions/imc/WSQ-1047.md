> [!example] Question 1047 · IMC
> ## Quantitative Trader Interview

Six people sit randomly around a round table. What is the probability that they are seated in increasing order by their age?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1048)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Around a round table, arrangements that differ only by rotation are considered the same. Thus the total number of distinct seatings of $6$ people is
>
> $$
> (6-1)! = 5! = 120.
> $$
>
> To sit “in order by their age” means that, going around the table, the people appear in increasing age order. Since the table is circular, there are exactly two such circular orders:
>
> - increasing clockwise,
> - increasing counterclockwise.
>
> Hence the number of favorable arrangements is $2$.
>
> Therefore, the required probability is
>
> $$
> \frac{2}{120} = \frac{1}{60}.
> $$
>
> So the probability is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{1}{60}}.
> $$
