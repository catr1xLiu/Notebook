> [!example] Question 1030 · IMC
> ## Trader Interview

What is the probability of rolling an increasing sequence when you roll a die three times?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1031)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Each die throw can result in any of $6$ values, so the total number of possible outcomes in three throws is
>
> $$
> 6^3 = 216.
> $$
>
> An increasing sequence means the three results are strictly increasing, say
>
> $$
> a < b < c.
> $$
>
> To form such a sequence, choose any $3$ distinct numbers from $\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$. Once the $3$ numbers are chosen, there is exactly one way to arrange them in increasing order.
>
> Thus the number of increasing sequences is
>
> $$
> \binom{6}{3} = 20.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the required probability is
>
> $$
> \frac{20}{216} = \frac{5}{54}.
> $$
>
> So the chance is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{5}{54}}.
> $$
