> [!example] Question 1064 · IMC
> ## Trading Intern Interview

What is the variance of a six-sided dice roll?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1065)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $X$ be the outcome of a fair six-sided die roll. Then $X$ takes values
>
> $$
> 1,2,3,4,5,6
> $$
>
> each with probability $\frac{1}{6}$.
>
> To find the variance, use
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(X)=E[X^2]-(E[X])^2.
> $$
>
> First compute the mean:
>
> $$
> E[X]=\frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6}=\frac{21}{6}=\frac{7}{2}.
> $$
>
> Next compute the second moment:
>
> $$
> E[X^2]=\frac{1^2+2^2+3^2+4^2+5^2+6^2}{6}
> =\frac{1+4+9+16+25+36}{6}
> =\frac{91}{6}.
> $$
>
> Now substitute:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac{91}{6}-\left(\frac{7}{2}\right)^2
> =\frac{91}{6}-\frac{49}{4}.
> $$
>
> Use a common denominator of $12$:
>
> $$
> \frac{91}{6}=\frac{182}{12}, \qquad \frac{49}{4}=\frac{147}{12}.
> $$
>
> So,
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac{182}{12}-\frac{147}{12}=\frac{35}{12}.
> $$
>
> Therefore, the variance of a fair six-sided die roll is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{35}{12}}.
> $$
