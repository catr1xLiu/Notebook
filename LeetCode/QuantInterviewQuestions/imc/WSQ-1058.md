> [!example] Question 1058 · IMC
> ## Quantitative Researcher Interview

A fair coin is tossed repeatedly. What is the expected number of tosses required to first see the sequence 'HHH' (three consecutive heads)?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1059)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $E$ denote the expected number of tosses needed to obtain the pattern $HHH$ for the first time.
>
> A convenient way to solve this is to track the current progress toward the target pattern.
>
> Define:
>
> - $E_0$: expected additional tosses starting with no relevant trailing heads,
> - $E_1$: expected additional tosses given that the current suffix is $H$,
> - $E_2$: expected additional tosses given that the current suffix is $HH$.
>
> We want $E_0$.
>
> ## Step 1: Set up the equations
>
> From state $E_0$:
>
> - We toss once.
> - With probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$, we get $T$, and remain in state $E_0$.
> - With probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$, we get $H$, and move to state $E_1$.
>
> Thus
> $$
> E_0 = 1 + \frac{1}{2}E_0 + \frac{1}{2}E_1.
> $$
>
> From state $E_1$:
>
> - We toss once.
> - With probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$, we get $T$, which breaks the run, so we return to $E_0$.
> - With probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$, we get $H$, so we move to $E_2$.
>
> Thus
> $$
> E_1 = 1 + \frac{1}{2}E_0 + \frac{1}{2}E_2.
> $$
>
> From state $E_2$:
>
> - We toss once.
> - With probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$, we get $H$, and the pattern $HHH$ is completed.
> - With probability $\tfrac{1}{2}$, we get $T$, so the run is broken and we return to $E_0$.
>
> Thus
> $$
> E_2 = 1 + \frac{1}{2}\cdot 0 + \frac{1}{2}E_0
> = 1 + \frac{1}{2}E_0.
> $$
>
> ## Step 2: Solve the system
>
> Substitute $E_2$ into the equation for $E_1$:
> $$
> E_1 = 1 + \frac{1}{2}E_0 + \frac{1}{2}\left(1 + \frac{1}{2}E_0\right)
> = \frac{3}{2} + \frac{3}{4}E_0.
> $$
>
> Now substitute this into the equation for $E_0$:
> $$
> E_0 = 1 + \frac{1}{2}E_0 + \frac{1}{2}E_1
> = 1 + \frac{1}{2}E_0 + \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{3}{2} + \frac{3}{4}E_0\right).
> $$
>
> Simplify:
> $$
> E_0 = 1 + \frac{1}{2}E_0 + \frac{3}{4} + \frac{3}{8}E_0
> = \frac{7}{4} + \frac{7}{8}E_0.
> $$
>
> Hence
> $$
> E_0 - \frac{7}{8}E_0 = \frac{7}{4},
> $$
> so
> $$
> \frac{1}{8}E_0 = \frac{7}{4}.
> $$
>
> Therefore
> $$
> E_0 = 14.
> $$
>
> ## Final answer
>
> The expected number of tosses needed to get $HHH$ is
>
> $$
> \boxed{14}.
> $$
