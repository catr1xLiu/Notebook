> [!example] Question 1085 · IMC
> ## Quant Trading Intern Interview

Given six distinct weights labeled 101, 102, 103, 104, 105, and 106, three weights are placed on each side of a balance scale. What is the probability that the weight labeled 106 is on the heavier side of the scale?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1086)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let the six distinct weights be $101,102,103,104,105,106$. They are randomly divided into two groups of three, one group on each side of the balance.
>
> We want the probability that the side containing the weight $106$ is the heavier side.
>
> ## Step 1: Total sum of all weights
>
> The total weight is
>
> $$
> 101+102+103+104+105+106 = 621.
> $$
>
> If one side is heavier, its sum must be greater than
>
> $$
> \frac{621}{2} = 310.5.
> $$
>
> So the side containing $106$ is heavier exactly when the sum of the three weights on that side exceeds $310.5$, i.e. is at least $311$.
>
> ## Step 2: Choose the two other weights accompanying $106$
>
> Besides $106$, choose any $2$ of the remaining $5$ weights. Thus there are
>
> $$
> \binom{5}{2} = 10
> $$
>
> equally likely triples containing $106$.
>
> Let the other two chosen weights be $a$ and $b$. Then the side with $106$ has total
>
> $$
> 106+a+b.
> $$
>
> We need
>
> $$
> 106+a+b > 310.5,
> $$
>
> so
>
> $$
> a+b > 204.5.
> $$
>
> Since $a+b$ is an integer, this means
>
> $$
> a+b \ge 205.
> $$
>
> ## Step 3: Count favorable pairs
>
> The remaining weights are $101,102,103,104,105$. Their pair sums are:
>
> - $101+102=203$
> - $101+103=204$
> - $101+104=205$
> - $101+105=206$
> - $102+103=205$
> - $102+104=206$
> - $102+105=207$
> - $103+104=207$
> - $103+105=208$
> - $104+105=209$
>
> Favorable pairs are those with sum at least $205$. There are $8$ such pairs.
>
> ## Step 4: Compute the probability
>
> Therefore,
>
> $$
> P(\text{$106$ is on the heavier side})=\frac{8}{10}=\frac{4}{5}.
> $$
>
> ## Final Answer
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{4}{5}}
> $$
