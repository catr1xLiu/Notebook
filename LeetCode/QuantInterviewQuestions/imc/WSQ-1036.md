> ## Trader Interview

What is 72 multiplied by 73?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1037)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> To compute $72 \times 73$, use distribution:
>
> $$
> 72 \times 73 = 72 \times (70 + 3) = 72 \times 70 + 72 \times 3 = 5040 + 216 = 5256.
> $$
>
> Thus, the result is
>
> $$
> \boxed{5256}.
> $$
