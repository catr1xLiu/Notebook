> [!example] Question 1035 · IMC
> ## Trader Interview

You have a deck of 3 black cards and 3 red cards in random order. In a game, you turn over one card at a time; if it is red, you win a dollar, and if it is black, you lose a dollar. What is the fair price to pay to play this game?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1036)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let $V(b,r)$ denote the expected future gain when there are $b$ black cards and $r$ red cards remaining, assuming optimal play. Since one may stop at any time, the value satisfies the dynamic-programming recursion
>
> $$
> V(b,r)=\max\left\{0,\; \frac{r}{b+r}\bigl(1+V(b,r-1)\bigr)+\frac{b}{b+r}\bigl(-1+V(b-1,r)\bigr)\right\}.
> $$
>
> The term $0$ corresponds to stopping immediately; the other term corresponds to turning over the next card.
>
> We compute $V(b,r)$ for small values, starting from boundary cases.
>
> ### Boundary values
> If only black cards remain, one should stop:
> $$
> V(b,0)=0.
> $$
>
> If only red cards remain, one should take them all:
> $$
> V(0,r)=r.
> $$
>
> ### Compute the needed values
>
> $$
> V(1,1)=\max\left\{0,\frac12(1+V(1,0))+\frac12(-1+V(0,1))\right\}
> =\max\left\{0,\frac12(1)+\frac12(0)\right\}
> =\frac12.
> $$
>
> $$
> V(1,2)=\frac23(1+V(1,1))+\frac13(-1+V(0,2))
> =\frac23\left(1+\frac12\right)+\frac13(1)
> =1+\frac13=\frac43.
> $$
>
> $$
> V(2,1)=\max\left\{0,\frac13(1+V(2,0))+\frac23(-1+V(1,1))\right\}
> =\max\left\{0,\frac13+\frac23\left(-\frac12\right)\right\}
> =0.
> $$
>
> $$
> V(1,3)=\frac34(1+V(1,2))+\frac14(-1+V(0,3))
> =\frac34\left(1+\frac43\right)+\frac14(2)
> =\frac74+\frac12=\frac94.
> $$
>
> $$
> V(2,2)=\max\left\{0,\frac12(1+V(2,1))+\frac12(-1+V(1,2))\right\}
> =\max\left\{0,\frac12(1)+\frac12\left(-1+\frac43\right)\right\}
> =\frac12+\frac16=\frac23.
> $$
>
> $$
> V(3,1)=\max\left\{0,\frac14(1+V(3,0))+\frac34(-1+V(2,1))\right\}
> =\max\left\{0,\frac14+\frac34(-1)\right\}
> =0.
> $$
>
> $$
> V(2,3)=\frac35(1+V(2,2))+\frac25(-1+V(1,3))
> =\frac35\left(1+\frac23\right)+\frac25\left(-1+\frac94\right)
> =1+\frac12=\frac32.
> $$
>
> $$
> V(3,2)=\max\left\{0,\frac25(1+V(3,1))+\frac35(-1+V(2,2))\right\}
> =\max\left\{0,\frac25+\frac35\left(-\frac13\right)\right\}
> =\frac15.
> $$
>
> Finally,
>
> $$
> V(3,3)=\frac12(1+V(3,2))+\frac12(-1+V(2,3))
> =\frac12\left(1+\frac15\right)+\frac12\left(-1+\frac32\right)
> =\frac35+\frac14=\frac{17}{20}.
> $$
>
> Therefore the value of the game is
>
> $$
> \boxed{\frac{17}{20}\text{ dollars} = \$0.85}.
> $$
>
> So a fair price to pay is $\boxed{\text{\textdollar}0.85}$.
