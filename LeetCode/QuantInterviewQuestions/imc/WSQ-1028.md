> [!example] Question 1028 · IMC
> ## Trader Interview

You have a 100-story building and two balls that break when dropped from too high a floor. What is the minimum number of drops needed on average to determine the highest floor from which a ball can be safely dropped?

[Source](https://www.thewallstreetquants.com/ebook#p=1029)

> [!success]- Solution (Click to expand)
> Let the critical floor be $F$, meaning that a ball survives from floors $1,\dots,F$ and breaks from floors $F+1,\dots,100$. The task is to determine $F$ using two balls while minimizing the number of drops in the worst case.
>
> ## Strategy
>
> With two balls, the standard optimal strategy is:
>
> - Drop the first ball from increasing floors with decreasing step sizes.
> - When the first ball breaks, use the second ball to search linearly through the interval below that floor.
>
> Suppose the first ball is dropped first from floor $x$, then $x+(x-1)$, then $x+(x-1)+(x-2)$, and so on.  
> This makes the worst-case number of drops the same regardless of when the first ball breaks:
>
> - If it breaks on the first drop: at most $x$ drops total.
> - If it breaks on the second drop: $2$ drops have been used, and at most $x-2$ more linear checks remain, totaling $x$.
> - In general, each case totals at most $x$ drops.
>
> Thus we need the smallest $x$ such that the triangular sum reaches $100$:
>
> $$
> 1+2+\cdots+x = \frac{x(x+1)}{2} \ge 100.
> $$
>
> Solve:
>
> $$
> \frac{x(x+1)}{2} \ge 100
> $$
>
> $$
> x^2+x-200 \ge 0.
> $$
>
> The positive root is
>
> $$
> x = \frac{-1+\sqrt{1+800}}{2} = \frac{-1+\sqrt{801}}{2} \approx 13.66.
> $$
>
> So the smallest integer is
>
> $$
> x=14.
> $$
>
> ## Optimal drop floors
>
> Use floors
>
> $$
> 14,\ 27,\ 39,\ 50,\ 60,\ 69,\ 77,\ 84,\ 90,\ 95,\ 99,\ 100.
> $$
>
> These increments are
>
> $$
> 14,\ 13,\ 12,\ 11,\ 10,\ 9,\ 8,\ 7,\ 6,\ 5,\ 4,\ 1.
> $$
>
> If the first ball breaks at one of these points, the second ball checks the floors immediately below, and the total number of drops is never more than $14$.
>
> ## Conclusion
>
> The minimum number of drops needed is
>
> $$
> \boxed{14}.
> $$
>
> This is the optimal worst-case number of drops with two balls and $100$ floors.
